# Sinister

> A 2012 supernatural horror film directed by Scott Derrickson about a true-crime writer who discovers dangerous home movies in his attic.

## Key Facts

- Sinister is a horror film directed by [[people/Scott-Derrickson]] [src:Scott-Derrickson]
- It is a 2012 supernatural horror film [src:Sinister-film]
- It was written by [[people/Scott-Derrickson]] and [[people/C-Robert-Cargill]] [src:Sinister-film]
- It stars [[people/Ethan-Hawke]] as Ellison Oswalt, a fictional true-crime writer [src:Sinister-film]
- The plot involves Ellison discovering a box of home movies in his attic that puts his family in danger [src:Sinister-film]
