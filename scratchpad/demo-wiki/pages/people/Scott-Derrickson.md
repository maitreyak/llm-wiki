# Scott Derrickson

> American director, screenwriter, and producer best known for directing horror films and the 2016 Marvel Cinematic Universe film Doctor Strange.

## Key Facts

- Scott Derrickson was born on July 16, 1966 [src:Scott-Derrickson]
- He is an American director, screenwriter and producer [src:Scott-Derrickson]
- He lives in Los Angeles, California [src:Scott-Derrickson]
- He is best known for directing horror films [[media/Sinister]], [[media/The-Exorcism-of-Emily-Rose]], and [[media/Deliver-Us-From-Evil]] [src:Scott-Derrickson]
- He directed [[media/Doctor-Strange]], a 2016 [[organizations/Marvel-Cinematic-Universe]] installment [src:Scott-Derrickson]
- He directed the short film [[media/Love-in-the-Ruins]] (1995) with [[people/Adam-Collis]] as assistant director [src:Adam-Collis]
