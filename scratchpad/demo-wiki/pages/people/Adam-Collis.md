# Adam Collis

> American filmmaker and actor known for early work as assistant director and film acting.

## Key Facts

- Adam Collis is an American filmmaker and actor [src:Adam-Collis]
- He attended Duke University from 1986 to 1990 [src:Adam-Collis]
- He studied cinema at the University of Southern California from 1991 to 1997 [src:Adam-Collis]
- He attended the University of California, Los Angeles from 2007 to 2010 [src:Adam-Collis]
- His first work was as assistant director for [[people/Scott-Derrickson]]'s short film [[media/Love-in-the-Ruins]] (1995) [src:Adam-Collis]
- In 1998, he played the character "Crankshaft" in [[media/Hundred-Percent]], directed by [[people/Eric-Koyanagi]] [src:Adam-Collis]
