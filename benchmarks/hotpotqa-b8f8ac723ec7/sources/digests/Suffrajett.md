Suffrajett is a U.S. rock band originally from New York City and currently based in Chicago, Illinois, with singer/violinist Simi Sernaker, guitarist Jason Chasko, bassist Kevin Roberts, and drummer Danny Severson. The band has toured with Local H, The Last Vegas, Bob Schneider, Juliette and the Licks, and Scissors for Lefty, and opened for the reunited MC5. Their self-titled debut album was released February 2, 2003, and the album "Black Glitter" was released January 1, 2007.
