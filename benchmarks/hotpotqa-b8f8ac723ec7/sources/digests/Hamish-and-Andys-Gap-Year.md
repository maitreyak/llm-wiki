Hamish & Andy's Gap Year is a Logie Award-winning Australian comedy series starring comedians Hamish Blake and Andy Lee travelling internationally. Season one comprised ten episodes broadcast weekly from New York City in America; season two (2012) was titled "Hamish & Andy's Euro Gap Year" with seven episodes from The Lord Stanley, a disused pub in East London. The third season (2013), "Hamish and Andy's Gap Year Asia", was broadcast from a Bangkok bar called 'The Raintree'.
