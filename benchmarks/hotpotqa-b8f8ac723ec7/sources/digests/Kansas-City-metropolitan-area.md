The Kansas City metropolitan area is a 15-county, bi-state metro area anchored by Kansas City, Missouri, straddling the Missouri–Kansas border with a population of about 2,340,000, making it the second largest metro area with its core in Missouri after Greater St. Louis. Besides Kansas City, it includes Overland Park, Kansas City (Kansas), Olathe, and Independence, each with more than 100,000 residents. The Mid-America Regional Council (MARC) serves as the area's Council of Governments and Metropolitan Planning Organization.
