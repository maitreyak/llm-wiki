The New York Society of Model Engineers, documented as early as 1905 and incorporated in New York in 1926 to obtain a permit for model motorboat races on a Central Park lake, began its first model railroad, "The Union Connecting," that same year. After moving twice within Manhattan (each move doubling layout size) and dismantling its largest layout in the Astor Building basement for the WW2 war effort, the Society accepted the Lackawanna Railroad's invitation to build what was then the world's largest model railroad in the former ferry waiting room of the Hoboken Terminal, modeled on the Lackawanna line from Hoboken to Scranton. In the early 1950s the organization moved to its current location in Carlstadt, New Jersey.
