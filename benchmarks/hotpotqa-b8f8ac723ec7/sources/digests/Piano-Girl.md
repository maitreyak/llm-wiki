Piano Girl (Turkish: Deli Deli Olma) is a 2009 Turkish comedy-drama directed by Murat Saraçoğlu, starring Tarık Akan and Şerif Sezer as two elderly people confronting their pasts and secrets. Released nationwide in Turkey in 2009, it opened the Sinema Burada Film Festival in İzmir and screened in competition at other festivals, including the 46th Antalya Golden Orange Film Festival, where Today's Zaman writer Terry Richardson reported the audience gave it a standing ovation.
