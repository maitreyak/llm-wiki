James P. Comer (born September 25, 1934, East Chicago, Indiana) is the Maurice Falk Professor of Child Psychiatry at the Yale Child Study Center since 1976 and an associate dean at the Yale School of Medicine. A leading child psychiatrist, he founded the Comer School Development Program in 1968 to improve scholastic performance of lower-income and minority children; it has been used in over 600 schools across 82 districts. He has written ten books and hundreds of articles, consulted for the Children's Television Workshop, co-founded the Black Psychiatrists of America, and holds 47 honorary degrees.
