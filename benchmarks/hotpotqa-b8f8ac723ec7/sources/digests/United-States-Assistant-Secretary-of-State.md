The passage explains that Assistant Secretary of State (A/S) is a title for many executive positions in the U.S. Department of State, ranking below the Under Secretaries. Six Assistant Secretaries reporting to the Under Secretary for Political Affairs manage diplomatic missions in designated geographic regions, plus one handling international organizations. Assistant Secretaries usually manage individual bureaus, may have equivalents of "Assistant Secretary equivalent rank" when titled otherwise (e.g., "Director"), and typically have Deputy Assistant Secretaries.
