Lil Ru (Sylvester Samuels) is an American rapper from Ridgeway, South Carolina, signed to Def Jam Recordings, whose debut album 21 & Up was released August 25, 2009. He entered the music business at 16, inspired by New Orleans' Cash Money Crew, and was helped by fellow South Carolinian Angie Stone to secure an Elektra Records deal, though he became unsigned after Elektra merged with Atlantic Records. His singles "Will Destroy" (2001) and "Shawty What You Doin'" (2002) both charted on Billboard's R&B/Hip-Hop charts and led to a deal with Capitol Records.
