The Wilford H. Fawcett House in Breezy Point, Minnesota is a National Register of Historic Places-listed home built as a private residence within the resort property by publisher Wilford H. "Captain Billy" Fawcett. Fawcett started "Captain Billy's Whiz Bang" as a joke book for WWI soldiers and founded Fawcett Publications in 1919 in Robbinsdale, Minnesota; in 1920 he bought land where Breezy Point Resort now stands and built cottages and a large log lodge (since burnt down). The house was designed in the Swiss Chateau style by Minneapolis architects Magney and Tusler, its rustic log style ironic given the earlier decline of local logging.
