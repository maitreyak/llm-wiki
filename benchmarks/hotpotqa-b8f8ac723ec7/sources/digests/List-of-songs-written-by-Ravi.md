Ravi is a South Korean rapper, songwriter and producer signed to Jellyfish Entertainment, who debuted as a rapper in the boy group VIXX in 2012 and formed the sub-unit VIXX LR with Leo in 2015. His songwriting career began with co-writing VIXX's debut single "Super Hero", and by November 2016, with the release of "VIXX 2016 Conception Ker", he had contributed to writing and composing over 46 VIXX songs. He is known especially for composing and writing rap portions, as well as lyrics and music, for the group.
