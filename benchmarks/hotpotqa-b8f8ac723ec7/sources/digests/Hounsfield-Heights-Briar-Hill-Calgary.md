Hounsfield Heights/Briar Hill is an inner suburban neighbourhood in northwest Calgary, Alberta, located north of the Hillhurst and West Hillhurst communities. Its boundaries are 16th Avenue N (Trans-Canada Highway) to the north, 14th Street W to the east, a lane north of 7th Avenue N to 19th Street W plus 8th Avenue N to the south, and Crowchild Trail, 12th Avenue N, Juniper Road and 22nd Street W to the west. The Lions Park C-Train station lies within the community, which is built on an escarpment prized for views of downtown to the south and the Rocky Mountains to the west.
