The Andre Norton Award for Young Adult Science Fiction and Fantasy is an annual SFWA award for the best young adult or middle grade science fiction or fantasy book published in the United States the previous year. Named for author Andre Norton (1912–2005), it was established by then SFWA president Catherine Asaro and the SFWA Young Adult Fiction committee and announced on February 20, 2005. Any YA or middle grade SF/fantasy novel, including graphic novels, is eligible with no word count limit, and it is presented alongside the Nebula Awards under the same nomination and voting rules, permitting simultaneous nomination for both.
