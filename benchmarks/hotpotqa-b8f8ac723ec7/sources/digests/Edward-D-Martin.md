Rear Admiral Edward Dana Martin (MD) is the former Acting Assistant Secretary of Defense (Health Affairs), appointed to two terms in 1993 and 1997, serving as the Secretary's principal advisor on the military health system. The Military Health System provides health services supporting the US military mission and comprises the Office of the Assistant Secretary of Defense (Health Affairs), the medical departments of the Army, Navy, Marine Corps, Air Force, Coast Guard, and Joint Chiefs of Staff, Combatant Command surgeons, and TRICARE providers.
