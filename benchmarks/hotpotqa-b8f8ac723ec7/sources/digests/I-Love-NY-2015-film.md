I Love NY (also known as I Love New Year) is an Indian romantic comedy directed by Radhika Rao and Vinay Sapru, starring Sunny Deol and Kangana Ranaut, produced by Bhushan Kumar and Krishan Kumar under Super Cassettes Industries Ltd. It was shot extensively in Mumbai, New York City and Bangkok, with its main plot taken from the 1976 Russian romantic comedy The Irony of Fate. After numerous delays, it released on 10 July 2015.
