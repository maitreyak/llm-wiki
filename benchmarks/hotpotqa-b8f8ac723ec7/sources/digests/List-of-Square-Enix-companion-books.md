Since 1998, Square (later Square Enix) has produced dozens of companion books focused on artwork, developer interviews, and fictional world/character background rather than gameplay. The first line was the "Perfect Works" series by Square subsidiary DigiCube (three books, 1998–1999), replaced by the "Ultimania" series written by Studio BentStuff. DigiCube published the Ultimania books until its dissolution in 2003; after Square merged with Enix on April 1, 2003 to form Square Enix, publication resumed.
