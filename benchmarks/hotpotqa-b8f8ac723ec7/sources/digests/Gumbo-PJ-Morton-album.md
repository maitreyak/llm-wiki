Gumbo is PJ Morton's fourth studio album and his first self-released one, issued April 14, 2017 on Morton Records as the follow-up to 2013's New Orleans. Entirely produced by Morton, it blends R&B with older soul elements and lyrics about romance plus political and personal themes. Guests include Pell, BJ the Chicago Kid and The HamilTones, and the record contains a cover of the Bee Gees' "How Deep Is Your Love".
