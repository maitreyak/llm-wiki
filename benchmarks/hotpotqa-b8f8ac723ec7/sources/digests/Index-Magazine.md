index Magazine was a New York City-based publication founded by Peter Halley and Bob Nickas in 1996 and running until late 2005, known for in-depth interviews with art and culture figures. Covering 1990s indie culture, it employed rising photographers such as Juergen Teller, Terry Richardson, Wolfgang Tillmans, and Ryan McGinley, and featured interviews with Björk, Brian Eno, Marc Jacobs, and Scarlett Johansson, as well as lesser-known New York personalities like Queen Itchie and Ducky Doolittle.
