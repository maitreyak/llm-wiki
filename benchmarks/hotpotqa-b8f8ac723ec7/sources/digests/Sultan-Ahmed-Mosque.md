The Sultan Ahmed Mosque (Turkish: Sultan Ahmet Camii), popularly known as the Blue Mosque, is a historic mosque in Istanbul, Turkey, built between 1609 and 1616 during the rule of Ahmed I. Its külliye contains Ahmed's tomb, a madrasah and a hospice, and hand-painted blue tiles adorn its interior walls, with lights at night framing its five main domes, six minarets and eight secondary domes. A popular tourist site next to the Hagia Sophia, it still functions as a working mosque.
