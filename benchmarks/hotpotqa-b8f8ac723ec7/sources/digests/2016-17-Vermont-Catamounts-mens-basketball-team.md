The 2016–17 Vermont Catamounts men's basketball team, coached by sixth-year head coach John Becker, played home games at Patrick Gym in Burlington, Vermont as a member of the America East Conference. The team finished 29–6 overall and a perfect 16–0 in conference play to win the regular season title, then beat Maine, New Hampshire and Albany to win the America East Tournament and its automatic NCAA Tournament bid. Seeded No. 13 in the Midwest region, Vermont lost to No. 4 seed Purdue in the first round of the 2017 NCAA Tournament.
