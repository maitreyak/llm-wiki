Steven Kenneth Galson (born 1956) is an American public health physician who is Senior Vice President for Global Regulatory Affairs at Amgen and Professor-at-Large at the Keck Graduate Institute for Applied Life Sciences in Claremont, California. A retired rear admiral in the United States Public Health Service Commissioned Corps, he served as acting Surgeon General of the United States from October 1, 2007 to October 1, 2009, and concurrently as acting Assistant Secretary for Health from January 22, 2009 to June 25, 2009. He was also Deputy Director and Director of the FDA's Center for Drug Evaluation and Research from 2001 to 2007.
