Ungod is Stabbing Westward's 1994 debut album on Columbia Records, recorded in six weeks in 1993 in Chiswick, England, and released February 15, 1994. Its sales and touring (including with Depeche Mode) led to a second album, Wither Blister Burn & Peel. Songs from the album appeared in films including Bad Boys, Johnny Mnemonic, Mortal Kombat and Clerks, and the guitar riff in the chorus of "Ungod" was also used in Filter's "Hey Man, Nice Shot" via Stuart Zechman.
