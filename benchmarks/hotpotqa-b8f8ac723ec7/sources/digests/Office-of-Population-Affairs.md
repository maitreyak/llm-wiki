The Office of Population Affairs (OPA) is a unit of the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services within the Office of Public Health and Science that advises the Secretary and Assistant Secretary for Health on reproductive health topics such as adolescent pregnancy, family planning, sterilization, and other population issues. It was created by an Act of Congress in 1970 (Public Law 91-572, 84 Stat. 1504, Dec. 24, 1970) and is directed by the Deputy Assistant Secretary of Population Affairs (DASPA). It comprises three component offices: the Office of Family Planning, the Office of Adolescent Pregnancy Programs, and the Office of Research and Evaluation.
