Giuseppe Verdi is a 1953 Italian biographical film directed by Raffaello Matarazzo and starring Pierre Cressoy, based on the adult life of the composer Giuseppe Verdi. It was released theatrically in the USA as The Life and Music of Giuseppe Verdi and on video as Verdi, the King of Melody. The film was a commercial success, grossing over 957 million lire at the Italian box office.
