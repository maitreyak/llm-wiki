Higgins Lake is a 9,900-acre recreational and fishing lake in Roscommon County, Michigan, the state's 10th largest, known for deep clear waters and named after Sylvester Higgins of the Michigan Geological Survey. It measures 7 mi long by 4 mi wide with a maximum depth of 135 ft, mean depth 44 ft, nearly 20 billion cubic feet of water, and a retention time of about 12.5 years. Half its water comes from submerged springs; it drains via the Cut River into Marl Lake and Houghton Lake, eventually reaching Lake Michigan.
