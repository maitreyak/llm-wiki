Mary Alden Osgood Childers, MBE (14 December 1875 – 1 January 1964), known as Molly Childers, was an American-born Irish writer and Irish nationalist. She was a daughter of Dr Hamilton Osgood and Margaret Cushing Osgood of Beacon Hill, Boston, and the younger sister of Gretchen Osgood Warren. She married writer and Irish nationalist Robert Erskine Childers; their son Erskine Hamilton Childers became the fourth President of Ireland.
