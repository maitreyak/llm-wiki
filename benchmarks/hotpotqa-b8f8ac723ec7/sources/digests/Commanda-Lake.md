Commanda Lake is a "3"-shaped, mud-bottom lake with tea-coloured water in the Almaguin Highlands of the District of Parry Sound, central Ontario, straddling the border of Patterson and Pringle townships at 222 m elevation. It is fed by Gee, Commanda and Beaudry Creeks and drained by the Restoule River, with the community of Restoule on its north shore, cottages and crown land along the shoreline, one permanent island, and a public boat launch off Commanda Lake Road. Its fish include northern pike, walleye, smallmouth bass and largemouth bass, and thick winter ice makes it a year-round fishing lake.
