The passage introduces the discography of South Korean boy group BTS, tracing their earliest releases and chart placements. BTS debuted in June 2013 with the single album "2 Cool 4 Skool" (No. 5 on the Week 31 Gaon Weekly Chart), followed by the EP "O!RUL8,2?" in September 2013 (No. 4, Week 38). Their second EP "Skool Luv Affair" (February 2014) reached No. 1 on the Week 18 Gaon Weekly Chart and was their first album to chart internationally on Billboard World Albums and Japan's Oricon Chart; its May 2014 repackage, "Skool Luv Affair Special Addition", also hit No. 1 (Week 21).
