South Korean boy group Shinee, formed by S.M. Entertainment in 2008, has received multiple awards and nominations. Their debut album "The Shinee World" (2008) won Newcomer Album of the Year at the 23rd Golden Disk Awards, and its single "Sanso Gateun Neo (Love Like Oxygen)" gave the group their first Korean music show win on "M Countdown" on September 18, 2008. The album "Lucifer" (2010) won the Disk Bonsang and Popularity Awards at the 25th Golden Disk Awards and brought a Best Dance Performance nomination at the 2010 Mnet Asian Music Awards, while the 2012 EP "Sherlock" won a Disk Bonsang at the 27th Golden Disc Awards, a Bonsang at the 22nd Seoul Music Award, and a Song of the Year nomination at the 2012 MAMA.
