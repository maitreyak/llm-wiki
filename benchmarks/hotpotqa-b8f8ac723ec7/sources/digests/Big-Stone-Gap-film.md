Big Stone Gap is a 2014 American drama romantic comedy film written and directed by Adriana Trigiani and produced by Donna Gigliotti for Altar Identity Studios, a subsidiary of Media Society. It is based on Trigiani's 2000 best-selling novel of the same name and is set in the Virginia town of Big Stone Gap circa the 1970s. The film premiered at the Virginia Film Festival on November 6, 2014.
