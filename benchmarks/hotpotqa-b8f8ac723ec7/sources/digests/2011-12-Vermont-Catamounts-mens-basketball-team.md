The 2011–12 Vermont Catamounts men's basketball team, in first-year head coach John Becker's debut season, went 24–12 overall and 13–3 in America East play for second place, playing home games at Patrick Gym. Vermont won the America East Basketball Tournament to claim the conference's automatic bid to the 2012 NCAA Tournament, where it beat Lamar in the First Four before losing to North Carolina in the second round.
