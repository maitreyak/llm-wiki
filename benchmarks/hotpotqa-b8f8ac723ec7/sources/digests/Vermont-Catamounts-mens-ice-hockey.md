The Vermont Catamounts men's ice hockey team is an NCAA Division I program representing the University of Vermont, playing at Gutterson Fieldhouse in Burlington, Vermont. The team joined Hockey East in 2005 after competing in ECAC Hockey from 1974 to 2005. Since moving to Division I in 1974–75, Vermont has made five NCAA Men's Hockey Championship appearances, including Frozen Four trips in 1996 and 2009.
