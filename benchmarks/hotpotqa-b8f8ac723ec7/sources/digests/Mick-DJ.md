Mick Batyske, known as Mick (formerly Mick Boogie), is an American DJ and entrepreneur who has spun private parties for celebrities including Kanye West, LeBron James, Jay-Z and Will Smith. Besides mixtape releases, he has performed internationally in New York City, Dubai, Tokyo, Las Vegas and Los Angeles. As an entrepreneur he has invested in start-ups including Localeur, where he is also an advisor and consultant.
