Downtown Kansas City is the central business district of Kansas City, Missouri and its metropolitan area. The Downtown Council of Kansas City defines it as bounded by the Missouri River on the north, 31st Street on the south, the Kansas–Missouri state line on the west, and Bruce R. Watkins Drive on the east; this is the most commonly accepted definition. The City of Kansas City's 2010 Greater Downtown Area Plan defines a Greater Downtown Area bounded by the city limits of North Kansas City and Missouri to the north, the state line to the west, 31st Street to the south, and Woodland Avenue to the east.
