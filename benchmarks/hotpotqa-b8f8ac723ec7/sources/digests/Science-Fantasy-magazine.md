Science Fantasy was a British fantasy and science fiction magazine launched in 1950 by Nova Publications as a companion to New Worlds, edited briefly by Walter Gillings and then by John Carnell until Nova folded in early 1964. Roberts & Vinter then acquired the title and hired Kyril Bonfiglioli, who renamed it Impulse in early 1966 and later SF Impulse; distributor confusion cut sales though the magazine stayed profitable. It ceased publication in 1967 after Roberts & Vinter's printer went bankrupt.
