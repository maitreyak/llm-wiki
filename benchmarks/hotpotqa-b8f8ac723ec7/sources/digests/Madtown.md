Madtown (매드타운, stylized MADTOWN) is a South Korean boy group formed in 2014 by J. Tune Camp, consisting of Moos, Daewon, Lee Geon, Jota, Heo Jun, Buffy and H.O. Their debut album "Mad Town" was released October 6, 2014, and their fan-base is called Mad-people. Members Moos and Buffy previously debuted as the hip hop duo Pro C in 2013, and from December 22, 2016 the group's contract was sold to GNI Entertainment after J. Tune Camp closed.
