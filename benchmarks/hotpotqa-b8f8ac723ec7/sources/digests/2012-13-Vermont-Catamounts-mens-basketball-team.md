The 2012–13 Vermont Catamounts men's basketball team, in second-year head coach John Becker's tenure, represented the University of Vermont in the 2012–13 NCAA Division I season, playing home games at Patrick Gym as an America East Conference member. Vermont went 21–12 overall and 11–5 in conference play, tying for second place, and reached the America East Tournament championship game, where it lost to Albany. The Catamounts then accepted a bid to the 2013 College Basketball Invitational, losing in the first round to Santa Clara.
