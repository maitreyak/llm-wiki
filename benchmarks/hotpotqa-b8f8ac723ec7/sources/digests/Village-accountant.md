The Village Accountant is a rural administrative government position in the Indian subcontinent, known by many regional names. It is called "patwari" in Telangana, Bengal, North India and Pakistan, "tapedar" in Sindh, "karnam" in Andhra Pradesh, "patnaik" in Orissa, "adhikari" in Tamil Nadu, and "talati" in Karnataka, Gujarat and Maharashtra. Historically it was the "kulkarni" in Northern Karnataka and Maharashtra and the "shanbogaru" in South Karnataka.
