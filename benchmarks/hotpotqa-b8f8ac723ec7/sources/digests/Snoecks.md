Snoecks is a Belgian magazine of over 550 pages that appears once a year in October, covering the most interesting new international developments in the arts, photography and literature. Recent editions featured artists including Anton Corbijn, Larry Sultan, Matthew Barney, Terry Richardson, Ron Mueck, Alberto Garcia-Alix, Peter Lindbergh, Albert Watson, Desiree Dolron, Bettina Rheims, Diana Scheunemann, Timothy Greenfield-Sanders and Andres Serrano.
