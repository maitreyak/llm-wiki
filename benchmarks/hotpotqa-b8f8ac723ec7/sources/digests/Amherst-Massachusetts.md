Amherst is a town in Hampshire County, Massachusetts, located in the Connecticut River valley, with a 2010 census population of 37,819 — the largest municipality in the county, though Northampton is the county seat. It hosts Amherst College, Hampshire College, and the University of Massachusetts Amherst, three of the Five Colleges. The town's name is pronounced "AM-erst", prompting the local saying "only the 'h' is silent", referring both to pronunciation and to its politically active populace.
