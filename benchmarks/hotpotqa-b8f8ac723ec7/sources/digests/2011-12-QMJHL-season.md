The 2011–12 QMJHL season was the league's 43rd, with seventeen teams playing 68 games each from September 2011 to March 2012. It was the first season for the Blainville-Boisbriand Armada, relocated from Verdun where they had played as the Montreal Junior Hockey Club (2008–2011), while charter member Lewiston Maineiacs folded after the previous season and an expansion team for Sherbrooke was announced for 2012–13. In the playoffs, the Saint John Sea Dogs became the seventh team in league history to win consecutive President's Cup championships.
