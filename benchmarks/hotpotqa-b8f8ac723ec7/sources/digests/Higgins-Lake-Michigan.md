Higgins Lake is a small unincorporated community along the southern and western shores of Higgins Lake in Roscommon County, Michigan, and also the name of the local post office (ZIP code 48627). The ZIP code covers numerous small lakeshore locales — Hillcrest, Lyon Manor, Detroit Park, Cook Corner, Sharps Corner, Almeda Beach, Oak Grove, Ritz Corner, Pine Bluffs and Cottage Grove — which are often considered part of the Higgins Lake community. The lake lies on the boundary between Lyon Township to the west and Gerrish Township to the east, with US 127 to the west, I-75 and M-18 a few miles east, and Houghton Lake a few miles south.
