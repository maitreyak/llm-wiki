Ophur was a rock band from the Chicago suburbs in DuPage County that performed across the midwestern United States for seven years, sharing bills with national acts such as The Plain White T's, Sum 41, Violent Femmes, Local H, Lucky Boys Confusion, Veruca Salt, Two Skinee J's, Duvall, and Sleeping at Last. Their sound has been described as an eclectic amalgamation of U2, Red Hot Chili Peppers, and Pink Floyd.
