Real Estate Technology (RE Tech / Proptech) is an economic industry of companies using technology to make real estate transactions more efficient, and is a sub-category of financial technology because real estate is an asset class representing significant investment. Investors generate income and capital gains from residential, commercial and industrial property categories. Historically a tactical investment or source of portfolio alpha, real estate has become part of long-term core strategies due to increased market efficiency and concerns over long-term variability of stock and bond returns, and it is known as a portfolio diversifier and inflation hedge.
