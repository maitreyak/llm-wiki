Case Gym is a 1,800-seat multi-purpose arena at Boston University that opened in 1972 within the Harold Case Physical Education Center, named for BU's fifth president Harold C. Case; nicknamed "The Roof" because it sits above Walter Brown Arena. It hosts BU Terriers men's and women's basketball and men's wrestling, with games also played at Agganis Arena and occasionally Walter Brown Arena. After BU left the America East Conference for the Patriot League in July 2013, home men's basketball conference games moved to Agganis Arena while non-conference games remained at Case.
