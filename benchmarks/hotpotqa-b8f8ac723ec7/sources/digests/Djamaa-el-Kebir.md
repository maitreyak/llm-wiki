The Great Mosque of Algiers (Djamaâ el Kebir) is a mosque in Algiers, Algeria near Algiers Harbor, built in 1097 according to an inscription on its minbar. Built under sultan Ali ibn Yusuf, it is one of the few remaining examples of Almoravid architecture and the oldest mosque in Algiers, said to be the oldest in Algeria after Sidi Okba Mosque. Its minaret dates from 1332 (1324 in some sources) and was built by the Ziyyanid Sultan of Tlemcen, while the outside gallery was added in 1840 following a French reconstruction of the street.
