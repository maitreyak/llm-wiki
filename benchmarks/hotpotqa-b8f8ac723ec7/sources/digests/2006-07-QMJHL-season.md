The 2006–07 QMJHL season was the 38th season of the Quebec Major Junior Hockey League, with a regular season running from September 14, 2006 to March 18, 2007 in which eighteen teams each played 70 games. The Lewiston Maineiacs finished first overall, earning their first Jean Rougeau Trophy, and went 16-1 in the playoffs to win their first President's Cup by defeating the Val-d'Or Foreurs in the finals.
