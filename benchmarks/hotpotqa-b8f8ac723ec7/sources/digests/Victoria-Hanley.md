Victoria Hanley is an American young adult fantasy novelist whose first three books — The Seer And The Sword, The Healer's Keep, and The Light Of The Oracle — are companion books. Her newest book, released March 2012, is Indigo Magic, a series sequel published by Egmont USA. She has also published two non-fiction writing guides through Cotton Wood Press: Seize the Story: A Handbook For Teens Who Like To Write, and Wild Ink: A Grownups Guide To Writing Fiction For Teens.
