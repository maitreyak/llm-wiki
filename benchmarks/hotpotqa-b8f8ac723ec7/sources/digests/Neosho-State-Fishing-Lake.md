Neosho State Fishing Lake, also called Lake McKinley, is a 92-acre lake in Kansas completed in 1927, located 5 miles north on highway 59 and 4 miles east on 40th Rd from Parsons, Kansas, with 124 public use acres surrounding it. Facilities include a shelter house, fishing piers, picnic tables, barbecue grills, vault toilets and a water hydrant, supporting fishing, camping and picnicking. Below the Lake McKinley dam is a catch-and-release youth fishing pond for anglers 15 and under accompanied by an adult. The lake is operated by the Kansas Department of Wildlife and Parks.
