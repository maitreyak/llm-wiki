Guns N' Roses is an American hard rock band formed in Los Angeles in 1985 by members of Hollywood Rose and L.A. Guns. Its discography comprises six studio albums, two live albums, two compilation albums, two EPs, seven video albums, eighteen singles, twenty-four music videos and one video single. After the independent 1986 EP release of "Live ?! *@ Like a Suicide" the prior year, the band signed with Geffen Records in 1986; debut album "Appetite for Destruction" (1987) topped the Billboard 200 and sold 18 million units in the US and about 33 million worldwide.
