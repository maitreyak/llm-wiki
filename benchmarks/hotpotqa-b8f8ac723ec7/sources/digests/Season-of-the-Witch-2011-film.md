Season of the Witch is a 2011 American historical fantasy adventure film directed by Dominic Sena, with extensive uncredited reshoots by Brett Ratner, starring Nicolas Cage and Ron Perlman as Teutonic Knights returning from the Crusades to a fatherland ravaged by the Black Death. Church elders accuse a girl (Claire Foy) of witchcraft and order the knights to escort her to a monastery so monks can lift her curse. The film draws inspiration from the 1957 film The Seventh Seal and reunited Sena and Cage after Gone in 60 Seconds.
