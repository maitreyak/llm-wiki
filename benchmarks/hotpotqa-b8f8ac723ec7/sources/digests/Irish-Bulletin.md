The Irish Bulletin was the official gazette of the government of the Irish Republic, produced by the Department of Propaganda during the Irish War of Independence, with offices originally at No. 6 Harcourt Street, Dublin. Its first editor was Desmond FitzGerald, who was replaced after his arrest by Robert Erskine Childers. It appeared in weekly editions from 11 November 1919 to 11 July 1921.
