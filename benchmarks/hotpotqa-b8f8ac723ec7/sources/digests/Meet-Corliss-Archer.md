Meet Corliss Archer was a Golden Age radio program that ran from January 7, 1943 to September 30, 1956. Created as CBS's answer to NBC's popular "A Date with Judy", it was also carried by NBC in 1948 as a summer replacement for "The Bob Hope Show", and aired on ABC from October 3, 1952 to June 26, 1953 before returning to CBS. Despite its long run, fewer than 24 episodes are known to survive.
