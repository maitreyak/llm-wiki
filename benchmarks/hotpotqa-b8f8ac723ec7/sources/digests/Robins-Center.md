The Robins Center is a 7,201-seat multi-purpose arena in Richmond, Virginia, opened in 1972 as home to University of Richmond Spiders basketball. Named for benefactor E. Claiborne Robins Sr. (class of 1931), it returned Spider basketball on campus for the first time since the mid-1940s, after years playing at venues such as the Richmond Coliseum, Richmond Arena, Benedictine High School gym, and area armories. It hosted the 1983 ECAC South men's basketball tournament, university commencements, and a 1992 presidential debate featuring Bill Clinton, George H. W. Bush, and Ross Perot.
