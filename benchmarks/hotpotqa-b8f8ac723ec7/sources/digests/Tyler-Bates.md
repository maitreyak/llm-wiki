Tyler Bates (born June 5, 1965) is an American musician, producer, and composer for film, television, and video games, known largely for action and horror films such as Dawn of the Dead, 300, Sucker Punch, and John Wick. He has collaborated with directors including Zack Snyder, Rob Zombie, Neil Marshall, William Friedkin, Scott Derrickson, and James Gunn, scoring every Gunn film including Guardians of the Galaxy (one of 2014's highest grossing domestic films) and its 2017 sequel. He is also the lead guitarist of Marilyn Manson and produced the albums The Pale Emperor and Heaven Upside Down.
