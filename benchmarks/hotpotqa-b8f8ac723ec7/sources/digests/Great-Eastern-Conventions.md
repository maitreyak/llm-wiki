Great Eastern Conventions, Inc. was an entertainment company that produced comic book conventions, most actively from 1987 to 1996. Its New York City shows bridged the gap between the mid-1980s demise of the Comic Art Convention and Creation Conventions and the 1996 founding of Michael Carbonaro's Big Apple Comic Con; from 1993 to 1995 it held two NYC shows a year at the Jacob K. Javits Convention Center. It also ran shows in New Jersey, Pennsylvania, Massachusetts, Oregon, Minnesota, and Texas.
