Deliver Us from Evil is a 2014 American supernatural horror film directed by Scott Derrickson and produced by Jerry Bruckheimer, released July 2, 2014. It is officially based on the 2001 non-fiction book "Beware the Night" by Ralph Sarchie and Lisa Collier Cool, and was marketed as "inspired by actual accounts". The cast includes Eric Bana, Édgar Ramírez, Sean Harris, Olivia Munn, and Joel McHale.
