Bradford P. Campbell served as Assistant Secretary for Employee Benefits Security at the U.S. Department of Labor, heading the Employee Benefits Security Administration (EBSA). Nominated by President George W. Bush on May 3, 2007 and unanimously confirmed by the Senate on August 3, 2007, he held the post until January 20, 2009. He had earlier been Acting Assistant Secretary from October 30, 2006 and EBSA's Deputy Assistant Secretary for Policy from March 5, 2004.
