The University of Kansas (KU) is a public research university in Kansas, with its main campus in Lawrence on Mount Oread, the city's highest elevation. It has branch campuses in the Kansas City metro area — the Edwards Campus in Overland Park and its medical school and hospital in Kansas City — plus sites in Parsons, Topeka, Garden City, Hays, and Leavenworth and medical school branches in Wichita and Salina. KU is one of the 62 members of the Association of American Universities.
