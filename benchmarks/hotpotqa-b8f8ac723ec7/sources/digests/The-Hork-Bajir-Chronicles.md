The Hork-Bajir Chronicles is the second companion book to K. A. Applegate's Animorphs series. In continuity it falls before book #23, The Pretender, with its events occurring between The Ellimist Chronicles and The Andalite Chronicles. Introduced by Tobias, who visits the valley of the free Hork-Bajir, the book recounts Jara Hamee's story of the Yeerk enslavement of the Hork-Bajir and the efforts of the Andalite Aldrea and her Hork-Bajir companion Dak Hamee to save their world, narrated alternately from the viewpoints of Aldrea, Dak Hamee, and Esplin 9466 in the style of the Megamorphs books.
