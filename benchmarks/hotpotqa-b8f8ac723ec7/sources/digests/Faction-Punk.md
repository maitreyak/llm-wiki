Faction With Jason Ellis is an uncensored mixed music channel (hard rock, punk, hip hop, heavy metal) on Sirius XM Satellite Radio. It aired on channel 41 until mid-July 2017, when it was temporarily replaced by Guns N' Roses radio; after August 16, 2017, channel 41 became Turbo (1990s/2000s hard rock) and Faction moved to channel 314, Turbo's former channel. Faction is available on select Sirius XM radios, streaming, and the smartphone app.
