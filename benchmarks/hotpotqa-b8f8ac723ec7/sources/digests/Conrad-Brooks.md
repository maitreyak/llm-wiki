Conrad Brooks, born Conrad Biedrzycki on January 3, 1931 in Baltimore, Maryland, is an American actor who moved to Hollywood in 1948. He began his film career in Ed Wood films including "Plan 9 from Outer Space", "Glen or Glenda", and "Jail Bait". After a break from acting in the 1960s and 1970s, renewed interest in Ed Wood's films led to his 1980s reemergence as a prolific actor who also writes, produces, and directs.
