Gone in 60 Seconds is a 2000 American action heist film directed by Dominic Sena, written by Scott Rosenberg, and produced by Jerry Bruckheimer. It stars Nicolas Cage, Angelina Jolie, Giovanni Ribisi, Christopher Eccleston, Robert Duvall, Vinnie Jones, and Will Patton, and is a loose remake of H.B. Halicki's 1974 film of the same name. Bruckheimer also produced The Rock and Con Air (both starring Cage) and Armageddon (starring Patton).
