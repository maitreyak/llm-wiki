City of Angels is a 1998 American romantic fantasy film directed by Brad Silberling, starring Nicolas Cage and Meg Ryan, set in Los Angeles. It is a loose remake of Wim Wenders' 1987 Berlin-set film Wings of Desire (Der Himmel über Berlin). The story follows an angel who falls in love with a mortal woman and wishes to become human, guided by a man (Dennis Franz) who already made the transition from immortality.
