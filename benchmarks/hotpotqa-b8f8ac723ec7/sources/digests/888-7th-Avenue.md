888 7th Avenue is a 628 ft (191 m), 46-story modern-style office skyscraper in Midtown Manhattan, completed in 1969 and designed by Emery Roth & Sons. It ties with Central Park Place as the 65th tallest building in New York City, houses Vornado Realty Trust's corporate headquarters, and was formerly called the Arlen Building after its builder, Arlen Realty & Development Corporation. The Red Eye Grill occupies street level in the building.
