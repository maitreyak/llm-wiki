The 1978 United States Senate election in Illinois, held November 7, 1978, pitted incumbent Republican Senator Charles H. Percy against Democratic nominee Alex Seith, an attorney and first-time candidate. Though Percy was expected to win easily, the race became competitive, prompting Percy to air a last-minute television ad apologizing and saying "I got your message and you're right." Percy narrowly edged out Seith to win his third and final Senate term.
