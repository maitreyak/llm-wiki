Amaruk Caizapanta Anchapacxi (stage name Amaruk Kayshapanta), born in Quito on January 30, 1970, is an Ecuadorian multidisciplinary artist known in Spain and Ecuador for work supporting immigrants' human rights in Spain. He was named "Chasqui de Oro", received the "Cultural Ambassador of the Andes-Mushuk Nina 2014" award and the "100 Latinos Madrid" prize from the Community of Madrid, and played the character "Edgar" in the TV series Hospital Central. His philosophy, "Amawtica Amarukiana Desestructuration", presents the Andean Worldview as a spiritual legacy of transformation and balance for awakening collective consciousness.
