South Phoenix is a region of Phoenix, Arizona, bounded by the Gila River Indian Community to the south and west, 48th Street or Interstate-10 to the east, and the Salt River to the north. It comprises the urban villages of South Mountain Village, Laveen Village, and Ahwatukee Village, and is sometimes called "the Southside" by residents. Major arterial east-west streets (Broadway, Southern, Baseline, Dobbins, Elliott, Warner, Chandler Blvd, Pecos) largely connect it to Tempe and Chandler, while north-south arterials link its villages to central, north, west, and east Phoenix.
