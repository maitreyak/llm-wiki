The Divide trilogy is a fantasy young adult novel trilogy by Elizabeth Kay set in an alternate universe, comprising The Divide (2002), Back to The Divide (2005), and Jinx on The Divide (2006). The first book was originally published by small press Chicken House (now a division of Scholastic), with later volumes and a reprint of the first published by Scholastic. The books have been translated into ten languages, and interior illustrations are by Ted Dewan.
