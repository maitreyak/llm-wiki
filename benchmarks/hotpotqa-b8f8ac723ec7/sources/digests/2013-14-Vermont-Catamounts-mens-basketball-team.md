The 2013–14 Vermont Catamounts men's basketball team, coached by third-year head coach John Becker, represented the University of Vermont in the 2013–14 NCAA Division I season, playing home games at Patrick Gym as an America East Conference member. Vermont finished 22–11 overall and 15–1 in conference play to win the America East regular season title, but lost in the conference tournament semifinals to Albany. As regular season champion that failed to win the tournament, Vermont received an automatic bid to the National Invitation Tournament, losing in the first round to Georgia.
