Charles Craft (1902–1968) was an English-born American film and television editor who entered Hollywood in 1927, beginning with Universal's silent film "Painting the Town," and edited 90 feature films over 25 years. In the 1950s he moved to television, serving as main editor on "Racket Squad" and working on "Meet Corliss Archer," "Science Fiction Theatre," "Highway Patrol," and "Sea Hunt." His final film work was "Flipper's New Adventure" (1964), and he edited the first 28 episodes of the "Flipper" TV series before retiring in 1966; he died in Los Angeles in 1968.
