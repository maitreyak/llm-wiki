Last Action Hero is a 1993 American fantasy action comedy film directed and produced by John McTiernan, satirizing action-film clichés via films within the film. Arnold Schwarzenegger stars as LAPD detective Jack Slater of the "Jack Slater" franchise and also plays himself as the actor portraying Slater, while serving as executive producer. Austin O'Brien co-stars as a boy magically transported into the "Slater" universe, and Charles Dance plays an assassin who escapes from the "Slater" world into the real world.
