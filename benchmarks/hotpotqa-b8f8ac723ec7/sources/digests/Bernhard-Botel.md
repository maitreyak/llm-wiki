Bernhard Bötel (1883–1953) was a German operatic tenor and actor active in Germany and Austria in the first half of the 20th century, recording for labels including His Master's Voice, Odeon, Polydor, Tri-Ergon and Vox. On stage he sang leading and comprimario roles in operas and operettas, including Belmonte, Chapelou, Count Almaviva, Daniel, the Duke of Mantua, Gabriel von Eisenstein, Indigo, Jeník, Paolino, Pâris, Pietro and Wilhelm Meister.
