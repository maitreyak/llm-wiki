Robert D. "Bob" Hormats, born April 13, 1943 in Baltimore, Maryland, is Vice Chairman of Kissinger Associates and previously served as U.S. Under Secretary of State for Economic Growth, Energy, and the Environment (2009–2013). He was Vice Chairman of Goldman Sachs (International) from 1982, held posts at the Bureau of Economic and Business Affairs, served as Ambassador and Deputy U.S. Trade Representative (1979–1981), and was a senior National Security Council staff member for International Economic Affairs (1969–1977), advising Henry Kissinger, Brent Scowcroft and Zbigniew Brzezinski. He helped manage the Nixon administration's opening of relations with communist China and received the French Legion of Honor (1982) and the Arthur S. Flemming Award (1974).
