Woodson is a census-designated place in Pulaski County, Arkansas, with a 2010 census population of 403, and is part of the Little Rock–North Little Rock–Conway Metropolitan Statistical Area. Woodson, along with Woodson Lake and Wood Hollow, is named for Ed Wood Sr., a prominent plantation owner, trader, and businessman around the turn of the 20th century. The CDP is adjacent to the Wood Plantation, the largest of Ed Wood Sr.'s plantations.
