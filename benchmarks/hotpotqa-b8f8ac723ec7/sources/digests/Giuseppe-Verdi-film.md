Giuseppe Verdi is a 1938 Italian biographical film directed by Carmine Gallone, starring Fosco Giachetti, Gaby Morlay and Germana Paolieri, depicting the life of composer Giuseppe Verdi (1813–1901). Giachetti was cast to emphasise Verdi's patriotism, following his recent patriotic roles in films such as The White Squadron. The film was shot at the Cinecittà Studios in Rome and is also known as The Life of Giuseppe Verdi.
