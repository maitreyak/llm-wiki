Erskine Barton Childers (1929–1996) was an Irish writer, BBC correspondent and senior UN civil servant, eldest son of Ireland's fourth President Erskine Hamilton Childers and Ruth Ellen Dow Childers. His grandparents Mary Alden (Molly) Childers and Robert Erskine Childers, plus the latter's double first cousin Robert Barton, were Irish nationalists heavily involved in negotiating Irish independence, which ultimately led to his grandfather's execution during the Irish Civil War. His great aunt was Gretchen Osgood Warren.
