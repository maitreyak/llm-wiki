The Teatro Giuseppe Verdi is a small 300-seat opera house in a wing of the Rocca dei Marchesi Pallavicino on the Piazza Giuseppe Verdi in Busseto, Italy, a town closely associated with composer Giuseppe Verdi. The rocca (fortress) served as the Pallavicino family's palace from the 13th century and became the city hall after the municipality acquired it in 1856. The theatre opened on 15 August 1868.
