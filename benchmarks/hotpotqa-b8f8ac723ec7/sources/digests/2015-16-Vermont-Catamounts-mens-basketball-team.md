The 2015–16 Vermont Catamounts men's basketball team, in John Becker's fifth year as head coach, played at Patrick Gym in the America East Conference and finished 23–14 (11–5, tie for third). Vermont beat Maine and New Hampshire to reach the America East Tournament championship game, losing to Stony Brook. In the College Basketball Invitational, Vermont defeated Western Carolina and Seattle before losing to Nevada in the semifinals.
