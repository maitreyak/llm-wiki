End of Days is a 1999 American fantasy action horror thriller film directed by Peter Hyams, starring Arnold Schwarzenegger, Gabriel Byrne, Robin Tunney, Kevin Pollak, Rod Steiger, CCH Pounder, and Udo Kier. The plot follows former NYPD detective Jericho Cane, who saves a banker from an assassin, becomes embroiled in a religious conflict, and must protect a young woman chosen by evil forces to conceive the Antichrist with Satan.
