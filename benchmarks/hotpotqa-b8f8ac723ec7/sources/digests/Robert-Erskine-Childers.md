Robert Erskine Childers DSC (25 June 1870 – 24 November 1922), known as Erskine Childers, was a British writer whose works included the novel "The Riddle of the Sands", and a Fenian revolutionary who smuggled guns to Ireland aboard his yacht Asgard. He was executed by the authorities of the nascent Irish Free State during the Irish Civil War. He was the son of Orientalist Robert Caesar Childers, cousin of Hugh Childers and Robert Barton, and father of Erskine Hamilton Childers, fourth President of Ireland.
