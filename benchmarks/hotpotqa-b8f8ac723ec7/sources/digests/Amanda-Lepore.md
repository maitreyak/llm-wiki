Amanda Lepore (born November 21, 1967) is an American transgender model, celebutante, singer, and performance artist, and a former Club Kid who has appeared in advertising for numerous companies. She is best known as photographer David LaChapelle's muse, and has worked with other photographers including Terry Richardson; she participated in LaChapelle's "Artists and Prostitutes 1985–2005" exhibit in New York City, "living" in a voyeuristic life-sized set. Lepore has released several singles, many written by and/or recorded with Cazwell, and in 2011 released her debut studio album "I... Amanda Lepore" on Peace Bisquit.
