Douglas Joseph "Doug" Bennet, Jr. (b. June 23, 1938) is a former U.S. political official and college president who served as the fifteenth president of Wesleyan University from 1995 to 2007. He previously was Assistant Secretary of State for Legislative Affairs under Carter (1977–79), head of USAID (1979–81), President and CEO of National Public Radio (1983–93), and Assistant Secretary of State for International Organization Affairs under Clinton (1993–95).
