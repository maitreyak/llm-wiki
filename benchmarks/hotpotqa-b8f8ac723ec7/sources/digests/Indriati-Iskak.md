Indriati Gerald Bernardina (born 9 June 1942), known as Indriati Iskak and later Indri Makki, is an Indonesian actress who became a psychologist and marketer. Born in Surabaya, she became popular through Usmar Ismail's commercially successful film "Tiga Dara" (1957), appeared in eight more films, formed her own girl group, and retired from cinema in 1963. She earned a psychology degree from the University of Indonesia in 1968, worked 26 years at Unilever, and since 1994 has been a marketing consultant with Makki Makki.
