Christopher Nicholas Sarantakos (born December 19, 1967), known as Criss Angel, is an American magician, illusionist and musician who began his career in New York City before relocating to the Las Vegas Valley. He is known for "Criss Angel Mindfreak" and the Cirque du Soleil collaboration "Criss Angel Believe" at the Luxor casino, which generated $150 million in Las Vegas tourist revenue in 2010 before being replaced by "Mindfreak LIVE" on 11 May 2016. He also starred in "Criss Angel BeLIEve" on Spike TV, the NBC reality-competition show "Phenomenon", and the 2014 stage show "Criss Angel Magicjam".
