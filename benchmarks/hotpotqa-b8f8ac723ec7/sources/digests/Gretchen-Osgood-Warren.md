Gretchen Osgood Warren (19 March 1868 – September 1961) was an actress, singer and poet, and the wife of Fiske Warren. She was the daughter of Dr. Hamilton Osgood and Margaret Cushing Osgood of Beacon Hill, Boston, Massachusetts, and her younger sister was Mary Alden Childers, wife of the writer and Irish nationalist Robert Erskine Childers. Her nephew Erskine Hamilton Childers served as the fourth President of Ireland from 1973 to 1974.
