Lanre Dabiri (born May 23, 1977), known as eLDee, is a former Nigerian-American rapper, singer and record producer who now works as an IT consultant in the United States. His name "Lanre" is short for the Yoruba name "Olanrewaju" ("Wealth is moving forward"), and he holds a master's degree in Architecture from the University of Lagos. He is an original member of the band Trybesmen, formed in 1998 with rappers KB and Freestyle, and hails from Lagos Island, Lagos State, Nigeria.
