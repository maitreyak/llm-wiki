Hammerjacks Concert Hall and Nightclub was a large downtown Baltimore concert hall owned by Louis J. Principio III, operating through the 1980s into the 1990s and billed as "The largest nightclub on the east coast." It hosted big-name and rising acts spanning punk, glam and heavy metal (Guns N' Roses, Kix, Ratt, Skid Row, Extreme) as well as pop (Badfinger) and alternative rock (Goo Goo Dolls), drawing patrons in big hair, leather, lace, spandex and heavy makeup and earning a reputation as a "hard rock shrine." It was torn down on June 12, 1997 to make way for an M&T Bank Stadium parking lot.
