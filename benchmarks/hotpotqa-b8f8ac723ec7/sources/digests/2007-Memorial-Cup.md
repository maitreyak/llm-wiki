The 2007 MasterCard Memorial Cup, the 89th edition of the CHL major junior championship, was held in May 2007 at Vancouver's Pacific Coliseum. Host team the Vancouver Giants won their first Memorial Cup, beating the WHL champion Medicine Hat Tigers 3–1 in the second all-WHL final (after 1989); other entrants were the OHL's Plymouth Whalers and the QMJHL's Lewiston Maineiacs. The four-team round-robin tournament set an attendance record of 121,561 fans over nine games, surpassing the 84,686 mark from the 2003 tournament in Quebec City.
