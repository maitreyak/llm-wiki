The document is a list of major opera composers, determined by appearance on a majority of compiled lists of significant opera composers (detailed in a "Lists Consulted" section). It spans from Jacopo Peri, author of the first ever opera in late 16th-century Italy, to John Adams, a leading figure in contemporary opera, and is organised by birthdate. Brief notes explain why each composer is considered major, and a separate section covers major women opera composers compiled from the same lists.
