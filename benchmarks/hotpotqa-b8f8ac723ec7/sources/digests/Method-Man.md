Clifford Smith (born April 1, 1971), known as Method Man, is an American rapper, record producer, and actor, and a member of the East Coast hip hop collective Wu-Tang Clan. He is also half of the duo Method Man & Redman and took his stage name from the 1979 film "Method Man". In 1996 he won a Grammy Award for Best Rap Performance by a Duo or Group for "I'll Be There for You/You're All I Need to Get By" with Mary J. Blige.
