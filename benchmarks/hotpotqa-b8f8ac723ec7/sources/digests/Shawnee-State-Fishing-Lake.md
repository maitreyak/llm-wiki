Shawnee State Fishing Lake is a 135-acre man-made freshwater lake about eight miles northwest of Topeka in northern Shawnee County, Kansas, built in the late 1960s with a maximum depth of 25 feet. It is sometimes confused with Lake Shawnee on Topeka's east side. The lake is widest at its dam on the south end; the only asphalted access road is at the southeast corner, with 94th Street to the north and Humphrey Road on the west.
