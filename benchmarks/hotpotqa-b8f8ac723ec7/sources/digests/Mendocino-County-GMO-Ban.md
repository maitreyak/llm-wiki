Mendocino County, California became the first U.S. jurisdiction to ban cultivation, production or distribution of GMOs when Measure H passed by referendum on March 2, 2004. The grassroots campaign was initiated by GMO Free Mendocino and opposed by biotechnology industry groups including the California Plant Health Association (now Western Plant Health Association) and CropLife America, whose clients include Monsanto, DuPont and Dow Chemical. Mendocino County was subsequently added to an international list of "GMO free zones," while pre-emptive statutes barring such local ordinances have been adopted in sixteen states.
