The 1995–96 season was Manchester United's fourth in the Premier League and their 21st consecutive season in England's top division. United became the first English club to win the Double (league title and FA Cup) twice, despite Alex Ferguson having sold Paul Ince, Mark Hughes and Andrei Kanchelskis before the season without major signings. Ferguson instead promoted young players including Nicky Butt, David Beckham, Paul Scholes and the Neville brothers, Gary and Phil.
