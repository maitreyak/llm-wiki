# Alex Seith

> Illinois attorney and Democratic nominee who narrowly lost the 1978 U.S. Senate election to Charles H. Percy.

## Key Facts

- Alex Seith was the Democratic nominee in the [[events/United-States-Senate-election-in-Illinois-1978]]. [src:United-States-Senate-election-in-Illinois-1978]
- Seith was a first-time candidate but made the 1978 race against [[people/Charles-H-Percy]] competitive. [src:United-States-Senate-election-in-Illinois-1978]
- Percy edged out Seith to win the 1978 election. [src:United-States-Senate-election-in-Illinois-1978]
