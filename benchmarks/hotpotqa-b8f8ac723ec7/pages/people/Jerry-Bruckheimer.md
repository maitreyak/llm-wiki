# Jerry Bruckheimer

> Film producer whose credits include Gone in 60 Seconds (2000), The Rock, Con Air, Armageddon, and the 2014 horror film Deliver Us from Evil.

## Key Facts

- Jerry Bruckheimer produced the 2014 supernatural horror film [[works/Deliver-Us-from-Evil]]. [src:Deliver-Us-from-Evil-2014-film]
- Bruckheimer produced Deliver Us from Evil, which was directed by [[people/Scott-Derrickson]]. [src:Deliver-Us-from-Evil-2014-film]
- Jerry Bruckheimer produced the 2000 action heist film [[media/Gone-in-60-Seconds]]. [src:Gone-in-60-Seconds-2000-film]
- Bruckheimer produced "The Rock" and "Con Air", both of which starred [[people/Nicolas-Cage]]. [src:Gone-in-60-Seconds-2000-film]
- Bruckheimer produced "Armageddon", which starred [[people/Will-Patton]]. [src:Gone-in-60-Seconds-2000-film]
