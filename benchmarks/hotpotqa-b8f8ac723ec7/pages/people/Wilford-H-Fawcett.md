# Wilford H. Fawcett

> American publisher known as "Captain Billy," founder of Fawcett Publications and developer of the Breezy Point resort property in Minnesota.

## Key Facts

- Wilford H. Fawcett was also known as "Captain Billy". [src:Wilford-H-Fawcett-House]
- He started the book [[works/Captain-Billys-Whiz-Bang]] as a joke book for soldiers during World War I. [src:Wilford-H-Fawcett-House]
- He formed [[organizations/Fawcett-Publications]] in 1919 in Robbinsdale, Minnesota. [src:Wilford-H-Fawcett-House]
- In 1920 he bought a tract of land where the present-day [[places/Breezy-Point-Resort]] is located, and began building cottages and a large log lodge. [src:Wilford-H-Fawcett-House]
- The large log lodge he built has since burnt down. [src:Wilford-H-Fawcett-House]
- He built the [[places/Wilford-H-Fawcett-House]] as a private residence within the resort property. [src:Wilford-H-Fawcett-House]
- He hired the Minneapolis architects [[organizations/Magney-and-Tusler]] to design his house in the Swiss Chateau style. [src:Wilford-H-Fawcett-House]
