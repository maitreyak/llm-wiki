# Richard Nixon

> 37th President of the United States (1969–1974) and the only U.S. president to resign from office.

## Key Facts

- Richard Milhous Nixon was born January 9, 1913 and died April 22, 1994. [src:Richard-Nixon]
- Nixon was the 37th President of the [[places/United-States]] from 1969 until 1974. [src:Richard-Nixon]
- Nixon resigned from the presidency in 1974, the only U.S. president to do so. [src:Richard-Nixon]
- Nixon served as the 36th Vice President of the United States from 1953 to 1961. [src:Richard-Nixon]
- Before becoming Vice President, Nixon served as a U.S. Representative and as a member of the [[organizations/United-States-Senate]] from [[places/California]]. [src:Richard-Nixon]
