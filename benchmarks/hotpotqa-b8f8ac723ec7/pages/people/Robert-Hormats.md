# Robert Hormats

> American economic diplomat and financier, Vice Chairman of Kissinger Associates and former U.S. Under Secretary of State for Economic Growth, Energy, and the Environment.

## Key Facts

- Robert D. "Bob" Hormats was born April 13, 1943, in [[places/Baltimore]], Maryland. [src:Robert-Hormats]
- Hormats is Vice Chairman of [[organizations/Kissinger-Associates]]. [src:Robert-Hormats]
- He was formerly Vice Chairman of [[organizations/Goldman-Sachs]] (International), which he joined in 1982. [src:Robert-Hormats]
- He was Ambassador and Deputy U.S. Trade Representative from 1979 to 1981. [src:Robert-Hormats]
- He served as a senior staff member for International Economic Affairs on the [[organizations/United-States-National-Security-Council]] from 1969 to 1977. [src:Robert-Hormats]
- At the National Security Council he was senior economic adviser to [[people/Henry-Kissinger]], General [[people/Brent-Scowcroft]] and [[people/Zbigniew-Brzezinski]]. [src:Robert-Hormats]
- He helped to manage the Nixon administration's opening of diplomatic relations with China's communist government. [src:Robert-Hormats]
- He received the [[concepts/Legion-of-Honour]] of France in 1982 and the [[concepts/Arthur-S-Flemming-Award]] in 1974. [src:Robert-Hormats]
