# Bezmialem Sultan

> Ottoman royal woman who was the adoptive daughter of Esma Sultan (1778–1848).

## Key Facts

- Bezmiâlem Sultan was the adoptive daughter of the Ottoman princess [[people/Esma-Sultan-daughter-of-Abdul-Hamid-I]]. [src:Esma-Sultan-daughter-of-Abdul-Hamid-I]
- She and [[people/Rahime-Perestu-Sultan]] were both adopted by that Esma Sultan. [src:Esma-Sultan-daughter-of-Abdul-Hamid-I]
