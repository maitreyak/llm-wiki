# Shirley Temple

> American actress, singer, dancer, businesswoman and diplomat (1928–2014) who was Hollywood's top box-office draw as a child actress from 1935 to 1938 and later served as a U.S. ambassador and Chief of Protocol.

## Key Facts

- Shirley Temple starred in [[works/A-Kiss-for-Corliss]] (1949) in what was her final starring role and her final film appearance. [src:A-Kiss-for-Corliss]
- Shirley Temple starred as [[topics/Corliss-Archer]] in the 1945 comedy film [[works/Kiss-and-Tell]]. [src:Kiss-and-Tell-1945-film]
- She was 17 years old at the time of Kiss and Tell. [src:Kiss-and-Tell-1945-film]
- Shirley Temple Black was born on April 23, 1928 and died on February 10, 2014. [src:Shirley-Temple]
- She was an American actress, singer, dancer, businesswoman, and diplomat. [src:Shirley-Temple]
- She was Hollywood's number one box-office draw as a child actress from 1935 to 1938. [src:Shirley-Temple]
- As an adult she was named United States ambassador to Ghana and to Czechoslovakia. [src:Shirley-Temple]
- She served as Chief of Protocol of the [[places/United-States]]. [src:Shirley-Temple]
