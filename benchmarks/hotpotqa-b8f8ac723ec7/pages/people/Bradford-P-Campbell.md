# Bradford P. Campbell

> Bradford P. Campbell was the U.S. Assistant Secretary for Employee Benefits Security, heading the Employee Benefits Security Administration from 2007 to 2009.

## Key Facts

- Bradford P. Campbell was the Assistant Secretary for Employee Benefits Security of the [[organizations/United-States-Department-of-Labor]], the official in charge of the [[organizations/Employee-Benefits-Security-Administration]]. [src:Bradford-P-Campbell]
- Campbell was nominated as Assistant Secretary by President [[people/George-W-Bush]] on May 3, 2007. [src:Bradford-P-Campbell]
- Campbell was unanimously confirmed by the [[organizations/United-States-Senate]] on August 3, 2007. [src:Bradford-P-Campbell]
- Campbell held the position of Assistant Secretary until January 20, 2009. [src:Bradford-P-Campbell]
- Before his confirmation, Campbell served as Acting Assistant Secretary beginning October 30, 2006. [src:Bradford-P-Campbell]
- Campbell served as EBSA's Deputy Assistant Secretary for Policy beginning March 5, 2004. [src:Bradford-P-Campbell]
