# Arnold Schwarzenegger

> Actor who has appeared in over 30 films, primarily action and comedy, and who has also directed and produced.

## Key Facts

- Arnold Schwarzenegger is an actor who has appeared in over 30 films. [src:Arnold-Schwarzenegger-filmography]
- Schwarzenegger has also ventured into directing and producing. [src:Arnold-Schwarzenegger-filmography]
- He began his acting career primarily with small roles in film and television. [src:Arnold-Schwarzenegger-filmography]
- For his first film role he was credited as "Arnold Strong", but was credited with his birth name thereafter. [src:Arnold-Schwarzenegger-filmography]
- He has appeared mainly in action and comedy films. [src:Arnold-Schwarzenegger-filmography]
- In addition to films and television, he has appeared in music videos for AC DC, [[organizations/Bon-Jovi]], and [[organizations/Guns-N-Roses]]. [src:Arnold-Schwarzenegger-filmography]
- Schwarzenegger starred as former NYPD detective Jericho Cane in the 1999 film [[works/End-of-Days]]. [src:End-of-Days-film]
- Schwarzenegger stars as [[concepts/Jack-Slater]], a Los Angeles police detective, in the 1993 film [[works/Last-Action-Hero]]. [src:Last-Action-Hero]
- In [[works/Last-Action-Hero]], Schwarzenegger also served as executive producer and plays himself as the actor portraying Jack Slater. [src:Last-Action-Hero]
- Schwarzenegger stars as U.S. government agent [[concepts/Harry-Tasker]] in the 1994 action film [[works/True-Lies]], directed by [[people/James-Cameron]]. [src:True-Lies]
