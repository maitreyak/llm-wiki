# Ernest Mangnall

> English football manager (1866–1932) who managed Manchester United from 1903 to 1912 and Manchester City from 1912 to 1924, the only man to have managed both clubs.

## Key Facts

- James Ernest Mangnall was born on 4 January 1866 and died on 13 January 1932. [src:Ernest-Mangnall]
- Ernest Mangnall was an English football manager. [src:Ernest-Mangnall]
- Mangnall started his managerial career with [[organizations/Burnley-F-C]]. [src:Ernest-Mangnall]
- Mangnall managed [[organizations/Manchester-United-F-C]] between 1903 and 1912. [src:Ernest-Mangnall]
- Mangnall managed [[organizations/Manchester-City-F-C]] from 1912 to 1924. [src:Ernest-Mangnall]
- Mangnall is the only man to date to have managed both Manchester United and Manchester City. [src:Ernest-Mangnall]
