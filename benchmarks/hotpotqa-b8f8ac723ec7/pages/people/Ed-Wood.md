# Ed Wood

> American filmmaker, actor, writer, producer and director (1924
> u20131978) whose films, including "Plan 9 from Outer Space", "Glen or Glenda" and "Jail Bait", featured actor Conrad Brooks; subject of Tim Burton's 1994 biographical film "Ed Wood".

## Key Facts

- Ed Wood's full name was Edward Davis Wood Jr. [src:Ed-Wood]
- Ed Wood was born on October 10, 1924 and died on December 10, 1978. [src:Ed-Wood]
- Ed Wood was an American filmmaker, actor, writer, producer, and director. [src:Ed-Wood]
- [[people/Conrad-Brooks]] got his start in movies appearing in Ed Wood films. [src:Conrad-Brooks]
- Ed Wood's films include [[works/Plan-9-from-Outer-Space]], [[works/Glen-or-Glenda]], and [[works/Jail-Bait]]. [src:Conrad-Brooks]
- Ongoing interest in the films of Ed Wood contributed to [[people/Conrad-Brooks]] reemerging as an actor in the 1980s. [src:Conrad-Brooks]
- Ed Wood is described as a cult filmmaker and is portrayed by [[people/Johnny-Depp]] in the 1994 film [[people/Ed-Wood]]. [src:Ed-Wood-film]
- The 1994 film [[people/Ed-Wood]] concerns the period in Wood's life when he made his best-known films and his relationship with actor [[people/Bela-Lugosi]]. [src:Ed-Wood-film]
- Ed Wood is a 1994 American biographical period comedy-drama film. [src:Ed-Wood-film]
- Ed Wood was directed and produced by [[people/Tim-Burton]]. [src:Ed-Wood-film]
- [[people/Johnny-Depp]] stars in the film as cult filmmaker [[people/Ed-Wood]]. [src:Ed-Wood-film]
- The film concerns the period in [[people/Ed-Wood]]'s life when he made his best-known films, as well as his relationship with actor [[people/Bela-Lugosi]]. [src:Ed-Wood-film]
- [[people/Martin-Landau]] plays [[people/Bela-Lugosi]] in the film. [src:Ed-Wood-film]
- The supporting cast includes [[people/Sarah-Jessica-Parker]], [[people/Patricia-Arquette]], [[people/Jeffrey-Jones]], [[people/Lisa-Marie]], and [[people/Bill-Murray]]. [src:Ed-Wood-film]
