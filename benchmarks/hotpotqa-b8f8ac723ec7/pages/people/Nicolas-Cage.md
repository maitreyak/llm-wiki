# Nicolas Cage

> Actor who starred as the angel in the 1998 film City of Angels, appeared in Deadfall as Eddie King, starred in the 2011 films Drive Angry and Season of the Witch, starred in the 2000 heist film Gone in 60 Seconds, starred in the 1994 romantic comedy-drama It Could Happen to You, starred in the 2000 film The Family Man, and starred in the 1989 Italian drama Time to Kill.

## Key Facts

- Nicolas Cage starred in the 1998 film [[media/City-of-Angels]]. [src:City-of-Angels-film]
- In City of Angels, Cage plays an angel who falls in love with a mortal woman and wishes to become human. [src:City-of-Angels-film]
- Nicolas Cage was among the stars of the 1993 crime drama [[media/Deadfall]]. [src:Deadfall-1993-film]
- Cage played the character Eddie King, reprising the role in the 2017 prequel/sequel [[media/Arsenal]]. [src:Deadfall-1993-film]
- Nicolas Cage starred in the 2011 fantasy action film [[media/Drive-Angry]] alongside [[people/Amber-Heard]]. [src:Drive-Angry]
- Nicolas Cage starred in the 2000 action heist film [[media/Gone-in-60-Seconds]]. [src:Gone-in-60-Seconds-2000-film]
- Cage starred in "The Rock" and "Con Air", both produced by [[people/Jerry-Bruckheimer]]. [src:Gone-in-60-Seconds-2000-film]
- Nicolas Cage starred in the 1994 romantic comedy-drama [[media/It-Could-Happen-to-You]] alongside [[people/Bridget-Fonda]]. [src:It-Could-Happen-to-You-1994-film]
- In It Could Happen to You, Cage plays a [[places/New-York-City]] police officer who wins the lottery and splits his winnings with a waitress. [src:It-Could-Happen-to-You-1994-film]
- Nicolas Cage starred in the 2011 historical fantasy adventure film [[media/Season-of-the-Witch]] alongside [[people/Ron-Perlman]]. [src:Season-of-the-Witch-2011-film]
- In Season of the Witch, Cage plays a Teutonic Knight returning from the Crusades. [src:Season-of-the-Witch-2011-film]
- Season of the Witch reunited Cage with director [[people/Dominic-Sena]], with whom he had previously worked on Gone in 60 Seconds. [src:Season-of-the-Witch-2011-film]
- Nicolas Cage starred in the 2000 romantic comedy-drama [[works/The-Family-Man]] alongside [[people/Tea-Leoni]]. [src:The-Family-Man]
- Cage's production company, [[organizations/Saturn-Films]], helped produce The Family Man. [src:The-Family-Man]
- Nicolas Cage starred in the 1989 Italian drama film [[media/Time-to-Kill]], alongside Italian actors [[people/Ricky-Tognazzi]] and [[people/Giancarlo-Giannini]]. [src:Time-to-Kill-1989-film]
