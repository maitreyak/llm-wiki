# Steven K. Galson

> American public health physician, retired USPHS rear admiral, acting Surgeon General of the United States (2007–2009), and Amgen executive.

## Key Facts

- Steven Kenneth Galson was born in 1956 and is an American public health physician. [src:Steven-K-Galson]
- He is Professor-at-Large at the [[organizations/Keck-Graduate-Institute-for-Applied-Life-Sciences]] in Claremont, California. [src:Steven-K-Galson]
- He served as the acting [[concepts/Surgeon-General-of-the-United-States]] from October 1, 2007 to October 1, 2009. [src:Steven-K-Galson]
- He served concurrently as acting Assistant Secretary for Health from January 22, 2009 to June 25, 2009. [src:Steven-K-Galson]
- He was Deputy Director and then Director of the [[organizations/Center-for-Drug-Evaluation-and-Research]] at the [[organizations/Food-and-Drug-Administration]] from 2001 to 2007. [src:Steven-K-Galson]
