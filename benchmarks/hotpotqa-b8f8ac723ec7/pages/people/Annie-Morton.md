# Annie Morton

> American model born in Pennsylvania, known for magazine covers and major fashion campaigns, and co-founder of Tsi-La Organics.

## Key Facts

- Annie Morton was born on October 8, 1970. [src:Annie-Morton]
- Annie Morton is an American model born in Pennsylvania. [src:Annie-Morton]
- Annie Morton has appeared on the covers of British Vogue, ID, Marie Claire, and other magazines. [src:Annie-Morton]
- Annie Morton has been photographed by Helmut Newton, Peter Lindbergh, Annie Leibovitz, Richard Avedon, Juergen Teller, Paul Jasmin, Mary Ellen Mark and [[people/Terry-Richardson]]. [src:Annie-Morton]
- Annie Morton has modeled for Donna Karan, Givenchy, Guerlain, Chanel, Harper's Bazaar, Sports Illustrated and Victoria's Secret. [src:Annie-Morton]
- Annie Morton is a long time vegetarian and an advocate for organic lifestyle choices and natural healthcare. [src:Annie-Morton]
- Annie Morton co-founded [[organizations/Tsi-La-Organics]], a "Green Luxury" company that creates and sells vegan, organic perfume and skin care products. [src:Annie-Morton]
