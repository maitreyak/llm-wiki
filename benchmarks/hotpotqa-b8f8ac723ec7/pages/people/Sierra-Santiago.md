# Sierra Santiago

> Afro-Boricua teenage protagonist of the novel Shadowshaper, living in Brooklyn.

## Key Facts

- Sierra Santiago is the protagonist of [[works/Shadowshaper]], an Afro-Boricua teenager living in [[places/Brooklyn]]. [src:Shadowshaper]
- She is the granddaughter of a shadowshaper, a person who infuses art with ancestral spirits. [src:Shadowshaper]
- Sierra must learn about her artistic and spiritual heritage to foil a killer who hunts the aging shadowshapers. [src:Shadowshaper]
