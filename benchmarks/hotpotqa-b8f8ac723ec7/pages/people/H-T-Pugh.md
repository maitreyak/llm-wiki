# H. T. Pugh

> Local stonemason near Jerome, Idaho, whose stone craftsmanship resembles that of the William H. Cook Water Tank House.

## Key Facts

- H. T. Pugh was a local stonemason whose work resembles the stone craftsmanship of the [[places/William-H-Cook-Water-Tank-House]]. [src:William-H-Cook-Water-Tank-House]
- Despite the similarity to H. T. Pugh's work, the builder of the William H. Cook Water Tank House has not been determined. [src:William-H-Cook-Water-Tank-House]
