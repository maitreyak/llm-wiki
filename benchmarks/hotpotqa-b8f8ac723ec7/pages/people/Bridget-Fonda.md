# Bridget Fonda

> Actress who starred opposite Nicolas Cage in the 1994 romantic comedy-drama It Could Happen to You.

## Key Facts

- Bridget Fonda starred in the 1994 American romantic comedy-drama [[media/It-Could-Happen-to-You]]. [src:It-Could-Happen-to-You-1994-film]
- In It Could Happen to You, Fonda plays a waitress with whom a police officer splits his lottery winnings. [src:It-Could-Happen-to-You-1994-film]
- Fonda co-starred with [[people/Nicolas-Cage]] in It Could Happen to You. [src:It-Could-Happen-to-You-1994-film]
