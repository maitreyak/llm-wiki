# Scott Derrickson

> Scott Derrickson (born July 16, 1966) is an American director, screenwriter and producer based in Los Angeles, best known for horror films such as "Sinister", "The Exorcism of Emily Rose" and "Deliver Us from Evil", and for the 2016 Marvel film "Doctor Strange".
