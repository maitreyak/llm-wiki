# Jacopo Peri

> Italian composer who wrote the first ever opera in late 16th century Italy, and the earliest composer in the list of major opera composers.

## Key Facts

- Jacopo Peri wrote the first ever opera, in late 16th century Italy. [src:List-of-major-opera-composers]
- Jacopo Peri is the earliest composer covered in the [[works/List-of-major-opera-composers]], which is organised by birthdate. [src:List-of-major-opera-composers]
