# Criss Angel

> American magician, illusionist and musician born Christopher Nicholas Sarantakos, known for "Criss Angel Mindfreak" and Las Vegas illusion shows.

## Key Facts

- Criss Angel is the stage name of Christopher Nicholas Sarantakos, born December 19, 1967. [src:Criss-Angel]
- Criss Angel is an American magician, illusionist and musician. [src:Criss-Angel]
- Criss Angel began his career in [[places/New-York-City]] before moving his base of operations to the [[places/Las-Vegas-Valley]]. [src:Criss-Angel]
- Criss Angel is known for starring in the television and stage show [[works/Criss-Angel-Mindfreak]]. [src:Criss-Angel]
- Criss Angel previously performed the live illusion show [[works/Criss-Angel-Believe]] in collaboration with [[organizations/Cirque-du-Soleil]] at the [[places/Luxor]] casino in Las Vegas. [src:Criss-Angel]
- Criss Angel starred in the television series [[works/Criss-Angel-Believe]] on [[organizations/Spike-TV]]. [src:Criss-Angel]
- Criss Angel starred in the reality-competition television show [[works/Phenomenon]] on [[organizations/NBC]]. [src:Criss-Angel]
- Criss Angel starred in the 2014 stage show [[works/Criss-Angel-Magicjam]]. [src:Criss-Angel]
