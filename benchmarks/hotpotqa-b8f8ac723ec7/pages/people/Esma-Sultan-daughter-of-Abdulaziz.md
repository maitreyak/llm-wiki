# Esma Sultan (daughter of Abdülaziz)

> Ottoman princess (1873–1899), daughter of Sultan Abdülaziz and Gevheri Kadın.

## Key Facts

- Esma Sultan was born on 21 March 1873 and died on 7 May 1899. [src:Esma-Sultan-daughter-of-Abdulaziz]
- She was an Ottoman princess, the daughter of Sultan [[people/Abdulaziz]] and his wife [[people/Gevheri-Kad-n]]. [src:Esma-Sultan-daughter-of-Abdulaziz]
- Her mother Gevheri Kadın was the daughter of Salih Bey Svatnba. [src:Esma-Sultan-daughter-of-Abdulaziz]
- She was the half-sister of [[people/Abdulmecid-II]], the last Caliph of the Muslim world. [src:Esma-Sultan-daughter-of-Abdulaziz]
- She and [[people/Sehzade-Mehmed-Seyfeddin]] were both children of Gevheri Kadın. [src:Gevheri-Kad-n]
