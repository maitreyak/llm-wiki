# Elizabeth Kay

> Author of The Divide trilogy, a fantasy young adult novel series.

## Key Facts

- Elizabeth Kay is the author of [[works/The-Divide-trilogy]], a fantasy young adult novel trilogy set in an alternate universe. [src:The-Divide-trilogy]
- Her trilogy comprises "The Divide" (2002), "Back to The Divide" (2005), and "Jinx on The Divide" (2006). [src:The-Divide-trilogy]
