# Dak Hamee

> A Hork-Bajir and companion of Aldrea who tried to save their world from the Yeerk invasion.

## Key Facts

- Dak Hamee is a [[concepts/Hork-Bajir]]. [src:The-Hork-Bajir-Chronicles]
- Dak Hamee was the companion of [[people/Aldrea]], and together they tried to save their world from the Yeerk invasion. [src:The-Hork-Bajir-Chronicles]
- Dak Hamee is one of the three points of view from which Jara Hamee's story is narrated in [[works/The-Hork-Bajir-Chronicles]]. [src:The-Hork-Bajir-Chronicles]
