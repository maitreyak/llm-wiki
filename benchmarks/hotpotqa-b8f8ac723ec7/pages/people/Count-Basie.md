# Count Basie

> Jazz musician who signed with Bennie Moten's Kansas City Orchestra in 1929 and brackets the beginning of the Kansas City jazz style.

## Key Facts

- Count Basie signed with [[organizations/Bennie-Motens-Kansas-City-Orchestra]] in 1929. [src:Kansas-City-jazz]
- Count Basie brackets one end of the hard-swinging, bluesy transition style known as [[concepts/Kansas-City-jazz]]. [src:Kansas-City-jazz]
