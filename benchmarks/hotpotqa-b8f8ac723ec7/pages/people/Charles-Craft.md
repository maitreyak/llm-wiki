# Charles Craft

> English-born American film and television editor (1902–1968) who edited 90 feature films and numerous television series including Racket Squad and Sea Hunt.

## Key Facts

- Charles Craft was an English-born American film and television editor. [src:Charles-Craft]
- Craft entered the film industry in Hollywood in 1927. [src:Charles-Craft]
- The first film Craft edited was the [[organizations/Universal-Pictures]] silent film [[works/Painting-the-Town]]. [src:Charles-Craft]
- Over 25 years Craft edited 90 feature-length films. [src:Charles-Craft]
- Craft's final film work was editing Flipper s New Adventure (1964). [src:Charles-Craft]
- Craft edited the first 28 episodes of the [[works/Flipper]] television series before retiring in 1966. [src:Charles-Craft]
