# John Becker

> John Becker is the head coach of the Vermont Catamounts men's basketball team, leading them to a 24–12 record, an America East title and an NCAA Tournament win in 2011–12, a 21–12 record with an America East final appearance in 2012–13, a 22–11 record with the 2013–14 America East regular season championship, a 23–14 record with a CBI semifinal run in 2015–16, and a 29–6 record with an unbeaten conference season and NCAA Tournament berth in 2016–17.

## Key Facts

- John Becker was the first year head coach of the [[events/2011-12-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]]. [src:2011-12-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]
- In Becker's first season, Vermont finished 24–12 overall and 13–3 in [[organizations/America-East-Conference]] play. [src:2011-12-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]
- Becker's team won the [[events/America-East-Basketball-Tournament]] and reached the [[events/2012-NCAA-Tournament]], beating [[organizations/Lamar-Cardinals]] before losing to [[organizations/North-Carolina-Tar-Heels]]. [src:2011-12-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]
- Becker was in his second year as head coach with the [[events/2012-13-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]]. [src:2012-13-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]
- In Becker's second season, Vermont finished 21–12 overall and 11–5 in America East play for a tie for second place. [src:2012-13-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]
- Becker's 2012–13 team lost the America East championship game to [[organizations/Albany-Great-Danes]] and then lost to [[organizations/Santa-Clara-Broncos]] in the first round of the [[events/2013-College-Basketball-Invitational]]. [src:2012-13-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]
- Becker was in his third year as head coach of the [[events/2013-14-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]]. [src:2013-14-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]
- In Becker's third season, Vermont finished 22–11 overall and 15–1 in America East play to win the regular season championship. [src:2013-14-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]
- Becker's 2013–14 team lost in the America East Tournament semifinals to Albany and then lost to [[organizations/Georgia-Bulldogs]] in the first round of the [[events/National-Invitation-Tournament]]. [src:2013-14-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]
- Becker was in his fifth year as head coach of the [[events/2015-16-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]]. [src:2015-16-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]
- In Becker's fifth season, Vermont finished 23–14 overall and 11–5 in America East play to tie for third place. [src:2015-16-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]
- Becker's 2015–16 team lost the America East championship game to [[organizations/Stony-Brook-Seawolves]] and reached the semifinals of the [[events/2016-College-Basketball-Invitational]], where it lost to [[organizations/Nevada-Wolf-Pack]]. [src:2015-16-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]
- Becker was in his sixth year as head coach of the [[events/2016-17-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]]. [src:2016-17-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]
- In Becker's sixth season, Vermont finished 29–6 overall and 16–0 in America East play to win the regular season championship. [src:2016-17-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]
- Becker's 2016–17 team won the America East Tournament and lost to [[organizations/Purdue-Boilermakers]] in the first round of the [[events/2017-NCAA-Tournament]]. [src:2016-17-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]
- Becker is the coach of the [[organizations/Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball]] team. [src:Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball]
