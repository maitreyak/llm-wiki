# Muhammed Ali

> Boxer captured in one of the century's most famous sports photographs, standing over Sonny Liston at the 1965 World Heavyweight Title fight in Lewiston, Maine.

## Key Facts

- Muhammed Ali was photographed standing over [[people/Sonny-Liston]] during the 1965 World Heavyweight Title fight. [src:Androscoggin-Bank-Colisee]
- The photograph of Ali over Liston, taken at the [[places/Androscoggin-Bank-Colisee]] in [[places/Lewiston]], Maine, is described as one of the most famous sports photographs of the century. [src:Androscoggin-Bank-Colisee]
