# Victoria Hanley

> American young adult fantasy novelist, author of The Seer And The Sword and companion books, plus non-fiction writing guides for teens and adults.

## Key Facts

- Victoria Hanley is an American young adult fantasy novelist. [src:Victoria-Hanley]
- Her first three books, [[works/The-Seer-And-The-Sword]], [[works/The-Healers-Keep]] and [[works/The-Light-Of-The-Oracle]], are companion books to one another. [src:Victoria-Hanley]
- Her newest book, released March 2012, is [[works/Indigo-Magic]], the sequel of a series, published by [[organizations/Egmont-USA]]. [src:Victoria-Hanley]
- She has published two non-fiction books through [[organizations/Cotton-Wood-Press]]: [[works/Seize-the-Story]] and [[works/Wild-Ink]]. [src:Victoria-Hanley]
