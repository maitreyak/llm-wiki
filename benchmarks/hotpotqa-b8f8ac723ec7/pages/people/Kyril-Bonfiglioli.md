# Kyril Bonfiglioli

> Editor hired by Roberts & Vinter for Science Fantasy, who renamed the magazine Impulse in 1966.

## Key Facts

- Kyril Bonfiglioli was hired by [[organizations/Roberts-Vinter]] to edit [[media/Science-Fantasy]]. [src:Science-Fantasy-magazine]
- He changed the magazine's title to Impulse in early 1966, a change that confused distributors and caused sales to fall, though the magazine remained profitable. [src:Science-Fantasy-magazine]
