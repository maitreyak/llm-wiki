# Robert Caesar Childers

> British Orientalist scholar (1838–1876) who compiled the first Pāli-English dictionary and was the father of Robert Erskine Childers.

## Key Facts

- Robert Caesar Childers was born in 1838 and died on 25 July 1876. [src:Robert-Caesar-Childers]
- He was a British Orientalist scholar. [src:Robert-Caesar-Childers]
- He compiled the first [[works/Pali-English-Dictionary]], the first dictionary of Pāli into English. [src:Robert-Caesar-Childers]
- He was the husband of [[people/Anna-Barton]] of Ireland. [src:Robert-Caesar-Childers]
- He was the father of the Irish nationalist [[people/Erskine-Childers]]. [src:Robert-Caesar-Childers]
- He was the grandfather of [[people/Erskine-Hamilton-Childers]], the fourth President of Ireland. [src:Robert-Caesar-Childers]
- He was described as a British Orientalist scholar and the father of the writer and revolutionary [[people/Erskine-Childers]]. [src:Robert-Erskine-Childers]
