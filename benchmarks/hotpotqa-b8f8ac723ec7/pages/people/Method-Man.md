# Method Man

> American rapper, record producer, and actor, member of the Wu-Tang Clan and half of the duo Method Man & Redman.

## Key Facts

- Method Man was born Clifford Smith on April 1, 1971. [src:Method-Man]
- Method Man is an American rapper, record producer, and actor. [src:Method-Man]
- He is known as a member of the East Coast hip hop collective [[organizations/Wu-Tang-Clan]]. [src:Method-Man]
- He is one half of the hip hop duo [[organizations/Method-Man-Redman]]. [src:Method-Man]
- He took his stage name from the 1979 film "Method Man". [src:Method-Man]
- In 1996, he won a Grammy Award for Best Rap Performance by a Duo or Group for "I'll Be There for You/You're All I Need to Get By" with [[people/Mary-J-Blige]]. [src:Method-Man]
