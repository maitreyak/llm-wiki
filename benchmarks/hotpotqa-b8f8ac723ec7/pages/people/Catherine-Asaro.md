# Catherine Asaro

> Author who, as SFWA president, established the Andre Norton Award in 2005.

## Key Facts

- Catherine Asaro was president of the [[organizations/Science-Fiction-and-Fantasy-Writers-of-America]] when the [[events/Andre-Norton-Award]] was established. [src:Andre-Norton-Award]
- She established the award together with the SFWA Young Adult Fiction committee, and it was announced on February 20, 2005. [src:Andre-Norton-Award]
