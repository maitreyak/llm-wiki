# Conrad Brooks

> American actor born Conrad Biedrzycki in 1931, known for appearing in Ed Wood's films and for a prolific later career as actor, writer, producer and director.

## Key Facts

- Conrad Brooks was born Conrad Biedrzycki on January 3, 1931 in [[places/Baltimore]], Maryland. [src:Conrad-Brooks]
- Conrad Brooks is an American actor. [src:Conrad-Brooks]
- He moved to [[places/Hollywood]], California in 1948 to pursue a career in acting. [src:Conrad-Brooks]
- He got his start in movies appearing in [[people/Ed-Wood]] films such as [[works/Plan-9-from-Outer-Space]], [[works/Glen-or-Glenda]], and [[works/Jail-Bait]]. [src:Conrad-Brooks]
- He took a break from acting during the 1960s and 1970s. [src:Conrad-Brooks]
- Due to ongoing interest in the films of [[people/Ed-Wood]], he reemerged in the 1980s and has become a prolific actor. [src:Conrad-Brooks]
- He has also gone on to write, produce and direct several films. [src:Conrad-Brooks]
