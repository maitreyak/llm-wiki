# Ahmed III

> Ottoman Sultan, father of Esma Sultan (1726–1788) by his consort Zeynep Kadın.

## Key Facts

- Ahmed III was an Ottoman Sultan. [src:Esma-Sultan-daughter-of-Ahmed-III]
- He was the father of the Ottoman princess [[people/Esma-Sultan-daughter-of-Ahmed-III]] (1726–1788). [src:Esma-Sultan-daughter-of-Ahmed-III]
- [[people/Zeynep-Kad-n]] was his consort. [src:Esma-Sultan-daughter-of-Ahmed-III]
- His daughter Esma Sultan was the half-sister of Sultans [[people/Mustafa-III]] and [[people/Abdul-Hamid-I]]. [src:Esma-Sultan-daughter-of-Ahmed-III]
