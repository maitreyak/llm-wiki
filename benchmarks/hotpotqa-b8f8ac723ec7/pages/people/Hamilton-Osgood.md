# Hamilton Osgood

> Boston doctor of Beacon Hill, father of Gretchen Osgood Warren and Mary Alden (Molly) Childers.

## Key Facts

- Dr. Hamilton Osgood lived at [[places/Beacon-Hill]], Boston, Massachusetts, with his wife [[people/Margaret-Cushing-Osgood]]. [src:Gretchen-Osgood-Warren]
- He was the father of [[people/Gretchen-Osgood-Warren]] and of her younger sister Mary Alden Childers ([[people/Molly-Childers]]). [src:Gretchen-Osgood-Warren]
- Dr Hamilton Osgood of Beacon Hill, Boston, was the father of Mary Alden Osgood Childers ([[people/Molly-Childers]]). [src:Molly-Childers]
