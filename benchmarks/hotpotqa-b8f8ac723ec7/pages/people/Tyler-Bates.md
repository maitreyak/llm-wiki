# Tyler Bates

> Tyler Bates (born June 5, 1965) is an American musician, music producer, and composer for films, television, and video games, best known for action and horror scores and as lead guitarist of Marilyn Manson.

## Key Facts

- Tyler Bates was born on June 5, 1965. [src:Tyler-Bates]
- Tyler Bates is an American musician, music producer, and composer for films, television, and video games. [src:Tyler-Bates]
- Much of Tyler Bates's work is in the action and horror film genres. [src:Tyler-Bates]
- Tyler Bates's film work includes [[works/Dawn-of-the-Dead]], [[works/300]], [[works/Sucker-Punch]], and [[works/John-Wick]]. [src:Tyler-Bates]
- Tyler Bates has collaborated with directors including [[people/Zack-Snyder]], [[people/Rob-Zombie]], [[people/Neil-Marshall]], [[people/William-Friedkin]], [[people/Scott-Derrickson]], and [[people/James-Gunn]]. [src:Tyler-Bates]
- Tyler Bates has scored every one of [[people/James-Gunn]]'s films. [src:Tyler-Bates]
- Tyler Bates scored [[works/Guardians-of-the-Galaxy]], which became one of the highest grossing domestic movies of 2014, and its 2017 sequel. [src:Tyler-Bates]
- Tyler Bates is the lead guitarist of the American rock band [[organizations/Marilyn-Manson]]. [src:Tyler-Bates]
- Tyler Bates produced the Marilyn Manson albums [[works/The-Pale-Emperor]] and [[works/Heaven-Upside-Down]]. [src:Tyler-Bates]
