# Roger O. Egeberg

> American medical educator, administrator and public health advocate who was Douglas MacArthur's personal physician in World War II and later a U.S. health official.

## Key Facts

- Egeberg was an American medical educator, administrator and advocate of public health. [src:Roger-O-Egeberg]
- He served as General [[people/Douglas-MacArthur]]'s personal physician during [[events/World-War-II]] in the Pacific theater. [src:Roger-O-Egeberg]
- He was Assistant Secretary for Health and Scientific Affairs in the Department of Health, Education, and Welfare (now the [[organizations/United-States-Department-of-Health-and-Human-Services]]) during the Nixon administration. [src:Roger-O-Egeberg]
- He served as Dean of the [[organizations/University-of-Southern-California]]'s medical school. [src:Roger-O-Egeberg]
