# Giuseppe Verdi

> Italian opera composer (1813–1901), whose works include Don Carlos and Rigoletto.

## Key Facts

- Verdi's full name was Giuseppe Fortunino Francesco Verdi. [src:Giuseppe-Verdi]
- He was born on 9 or 10 October 1813 and died on 27 January 1901. [src:Giuseppe-Verdi]
- He was an Italian opera composer. [src:Giuseppe-Verdi]
- Verdi composed the opera [[works/Don-Carlos]]. [src:Armand-Castelmary]
- His opera Don Carlos premiered at the [[organizations/Paris-Opera]] in 1867, with [[people/Armand-Castelmary]] creating the role of the Monk. [src:Armand-Castelmary]
- [[people/Ferdinando-Provesi]] was an early tutor of Verdi, beginning to teach him in 1824 when Verdi was 11 years old. [src:Ferdinando-Provesi]
- His life was portrayed in the 1938 Italian biographical film [[people/Giuseppe-Verdi]], directed by [[people/Carmine-Gallone]]. [src:Giuseppe-Verdi-film]
- In that film Verdi was played by [[people/Fosco-Giachetti]], whose casting was intended to emphasise the composer's patriotism. [src:Giuseppe-Verdi-film]
- Giuseppe Verdi is a 1938 Italian biographical film. [src:Giuseppe-Verdi-film]
- The film was directed by [[people/Carmine-Gallone]]. [src:Giuseppe-Verdi-film]
- It stars [[people/Fosco-Giachetti]], [[people/Gaby-Morlay]] and [[people/Germana-Paolieri]]. [src:Giuseppe-Verdi-film]
- The film portrays the life of the composer [[people/Giuseppe-Verdi]] (1813-1901). [src:Giuseppe-Verdi-film]
- The casting of Giachetti as Verdi was intended to emphasise the composer's patriotism, as he had recently played patriotic roles in films such as "The White Squadron". [src:Giuseppe-Verdi-film]
- The film was made at the [[places/Cinecitta-Studios]] in Rome. [src:Giuseppe-Verdi-film]
- The film is also known by the alternative title The Life of Giuseppe Verdi. [src:Giuseppe-Verdi-film]
- [[places/Parma-Airport]] is also known as Giuseppe Verdi Airport, named after him. [src:Parma-Airport]
- The town of [[places/Busseto]] is closely associated with Verdi's life, and its small opera house, the [[places/Teatro-Giuseppe-Verdi]], is named after him. [src:Teatro-Giuseppe-Verdi]
- His adult life events were also the basis of the 1953 Italian biographical film [[media/Giuseppe-Verdi-1953-film]], directed by [[people/Raffaello-Matarazzo]] and starring [[people/Pierre-Cressoy]]. [src:Verdi-the-King-of-Melody]
- The 1953 film about him was released theatrically in the USA as The Life and Music of Giuseppe Verdi and on video as Verdi, the King of Melody, grossing over 957 million lire at the Italian box office. [src:Verdi-the-King-of-Melody]
