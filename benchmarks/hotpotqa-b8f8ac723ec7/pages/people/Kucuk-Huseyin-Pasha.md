# Küçük Hüseyin Pasha

> Ottoman statesman and admiral (1757–1803) who served as Kapudan Pasha of the Ottoman Navy from 1792 until his death, and was a "damat" of the Ottoman dynasty through his marriage to Esma Sultan.

## Key Facts

- Küçük Hüseyin Pasha was born in 1757 and died on 7 December 1803. [src:Kucuk-Huseyin-Pasha]
- He was also known as Tayazade Damat Küçük Hüseyin Pasha. [src:Kucuk-Huseyin-Pasha]
- He was an Ottoman statesman and admiral. [src:Kucuk-Huseyin-Pasha]
- He served as [[concepts/Kapudan-Pasha]], Grand Admiral of the [[organizations/Ottoman-Navy]], from 11 March 1792 to 7 December 1803. [src:Kucuk-Huseyin-Pasha]
- He was a [[concepts/Damat]] ("bridegroom") to the Ottoman dynasty. [src:Kucuk-Huseyin-Pasha]
- He acquired the damat status after marrying an Ottoman princess, [[topics/Esma-Sultan]]. [src:Kucuk-Huseyin-Pasha]
