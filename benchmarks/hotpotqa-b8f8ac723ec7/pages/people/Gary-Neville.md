# Gary Neville

> Young Manchester United defender, brother of Phil Neville, drafted into the team by Alex Ferguson for the 1995–96 season and featured in The Class of '92.

## Key Facts

- Gary Neville was one of the young players drafted in by [[people/Alex-Ferguson]] for the 1995–96 season. [src:1995-96-Manchester-United-F-C-season]
- Gary Neville is the brother of [[people/Phil-Neville]]. [src:1995-96-Manchester-United-F-C-season]
- Gary Neville is one of the six young [[organizations/Manchester-United-F-C]] footballers featured in the 2013 documentary [[media/The-Class-of-92]]. [src:The-Class-of-92]
