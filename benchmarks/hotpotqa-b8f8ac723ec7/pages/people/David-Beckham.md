# David Beckham

> Manchester United player, drafted into the team by Alex Ferguson for the 1995–96 season, who scored in the 1996 FA Charity Shield, later founded the David Beckham Academy and moved to Los Angeles Galaxy; one of the six players featured in The Class of '92.

## Key Facts

- David Beckham was one of the young players drafted in by [[people/Alex-Ferguson]] for the 1995–96 season. [src:1995-96-Manchester-United-F-C-season]
- David Beckham scored for [[organizations/Manchester-United-F-C]] in the [[events/1996-FA-Charity-Shield]]. [src:1996-FA-Charity-Shield]
- David Beckham, an England international, founded the [[organizations/David-Beckham-Academy]] football school in 2005. [src:David-Beckham-Academy]
- David Beckham moved to [[organizations/Los-Angeles-Galaxy]], and the show [[media/David-Beckhams-Soccer-USA]] began following that move. [src:David-Beckhams-Soccer-USA]
- David Beckham is one of the six young [[organizations/Manchester-United-F-C]] footballers featured in the 2013 documentary [[media/The-Class-of-92]]. [src:The-Class-of-92]
