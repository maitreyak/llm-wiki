# Edward D. Martin

> Rear Admiral Edward Dana Martin (MD), former Acting Assistant Secretary of Defense (Health Affairs), appointed to two terms in 1993 and 1997.

## Key Facts

- Edward Dana Martin holds the rank of Rear Admiral and the MD credential. [src:Edward-D-Martin]
- Edward D. Martin is the former Acting Assistant Secretary of Defense (Health Affairs). [src:Edward-D-Martin]
- Martin was appointed to two terms, originally in 1993 and once again in 1997. [src:Edward-D-Martin]
- Martin served as the Secretary's principal advisor on matters related to the [[concepts/Military-Health-System]], health.mil. [src:Edward-D-Martin]
