# Adam Collis

> Adam Collis is an American filmmaker and actor educated at Duke University, UCLA, and the University of Southern California, whose early credits include assistant director on the short film "Love in the Ruins" and an acting role in "Hundred Percent".

## Key Facts

- Adam Collis is an American filmmaker and actor. [src:Adam-Collis]
- Adam Collis attended [[organizations/Duke-University]] from 1986 to 1990. [src:Adam-Collis]
- Adam Collis attended the [[organizations/University-of-California-Los-Angeles]] from 2007 to 2010. [src:Adam-Collis]
- Adam Collis studied cinema at the [[organizations/University-of-Southern-California]] from 1991 to 1997. [src:Adam-Collis]
- Adam Collis's first work was as assistant director for [[people/Scott-Derrickson]]'s short film [[works/Love-in-the-Ruins]] (1995). [src:Adam-Collis]
- In 1998, Adam Collis played "Crankshaft" in [[people/Eric-Koyanagi]]'s [[works/Hundred-Percent]]. [src:Adam-Collis]
