# Cho Kyuhyun

> South Korean singer and musical theatre actor, best known as a member of the boy group Super Junior.

## Key Facts

- Cho Kyu-hyun was born on February 3, 1988. [src:Cho-Kyuhyun]
- He is better known mononymously as Kyuhyun. [src:Cho-Kyuhyun]
- He is a South Korean singer and musical theatre actor. [src:Cho-Kyuhyun]
- He is best known as a member of the South Korean boy group [[organizations/Super-Junior]]. [src:Cho-Kyuhyun]
- He is a member of the Super Junior sub-groups [[organizations/Super-Junior-K-R-Y]] and [[organizations/Super-Junior-M]]. [src:Cho-Kyuhyun]
- He is a former member of the South Korean ballad group [[organizations/S-M-the-Ballad]]. [src:Cho-Kyuhyun]
- He is one of the first four Korean artists to appear on Chinese postage stamps. [src:Cho-Kyuhyun]
