# Ahmed I

> Ruler during whose reign the Sultan Ahmed Mosque was constructed in Istanbul.

## Key Facts

- The [[places/Sultan-Ahmed-Mosque]] was constructed between 1609 and 1616 during the rule of Ahmed I. [src:Sultan-Ahmed-Mosque]
- The mosque's külliye contains Ahmed's tomb, along with a madrasah and a hospice. [src:Sultan-Ahmed-Mosque]
