# F. Hugh Herbert

> Writer whose series of short stories was the basis for the radio and television versions of Meet Corliss Archer.

## Key Facts

- F. Hugh Herbert wrote the series of short stories on which the radio program [[works/Meet-Corliss-Archer]] was based. [src:Meet-Corliss-Archer-TV-series]
- The television sitcom [[works/Meet-Corliss-Archer-TV-series]] was an adaptation of the radio series derived from F. Hugh Herbert's short stories. [src:Meet-Corliss-Archer-TV-series]
