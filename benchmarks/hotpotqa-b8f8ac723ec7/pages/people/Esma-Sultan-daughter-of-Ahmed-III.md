# Esma Sultan (daughter of Ahmed III)

> Ottoman princess (1726–1788), daughter of Sultan Ahmed III and Zeynep Kadın.

## Key Facts

- Esma Sultan was born on 14 March 1726 and died on 13 August 1788. [src:Esma-Sultan-daughter-of-Ahmed-III]
- She was an Ottoman princess, the daughter of Sultan [[people/Ahmed-III]] and his consort [[people/Zeynep-Kad-n]]. [src:Esma-Sultan-daughter-of-Ahmed-III]
- She was the half-sister of Sultan [[people/Mustafa-III]] and Sultan [[people/Abdul-Hamid-I]]. [src:Esma-Sultan-daughter-of-Ahmed-III]
