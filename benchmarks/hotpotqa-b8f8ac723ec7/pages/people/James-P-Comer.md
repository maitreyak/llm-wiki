# James P. Comer

> American child psychiatrist, Yale professor, and founder of the Comer School Development Program, known for work on improving school performance of lower-income and minority children.

## Key Facts

- He has been the Maurice Falk Professor of Child Psychiatry at the [[organizations/Yale-Child-Study-Center]] since 1976. [src:James-P-Comer]
- He is an associate dean at the [[organizations/Yale-School-of-Medicine]]. [src:James-P-Comer]
- He is regarded as one of the world's leading child psychiatrists and is best known for efforts to improve the scholastic performance of children from lower-income and minority backgrounds. [src:James-P-Comer]
- His work led to the founding of the [[concepts/Comer-School-Development-Program]] in 1968. [src:James-P-Comer]
- He has been awarded 47 honorary degrees and has been recognized by numerous organizations. [src:James-P-Comer]
