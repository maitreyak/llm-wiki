# Armand Castelmary

> French operatic bass (1834–1897) who created roles in three major Paris Opera premieres and died onstage at the Metropolitan Opera House.

## Key Facts

- Armand Castelmary's real name was Comte Armand de Castan. [src:Armand-Castelmary]
- He was born in [[places/Toulouse]] on 16 August 1834. [src:Armand-Castelmary]
- He died in [[places/New-York-City]] on 10 February 1897. [src:Armand-Castelmary]
- He was a French operatic bass. [src:Armand-Castelmary]
- He created roles in three major premieres at the [[organizations/Paris-Opera]]. [src:Armand-Castelmary]
- He created the role of Don Diego in L Africaine by [[people/Giacomo-Meyerbeer]] in 1865. [src:Armand-Castelmary]
- He created the role of the Monk in [[works/Don-Carlos]] by [[people/Giuseppe-Verdi]] in 1867. [src:Armand-Castelmary]
- He created the role of Horatio in [[works/Hamlet]] by [[people/Ambroise-Thomas]] in 1868. [src:Armand-Castelmary]
- He also appeared at opera houses in England and the United States. [src:Armand-Castelmary]
- He died onstage at the [[organizations/Metropolitan-Opera-House]] in New York during a performance of [[works/Martha]] by [[people/Friedrich-von-Flotow]]. [src:Armand-Castelmary]
