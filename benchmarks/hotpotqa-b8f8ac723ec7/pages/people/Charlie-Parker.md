# Charlie Parker

> Kansas City native jazz musician who ushered in the Bebop style in the 1940s.

- Charlie Parker was a native of Kansas City. [src:Kansas-City-jazz]
- Charlie Parker was to usher in the [[concepts/Bebop]] style in the 1940s. [src:Kansas-City-jazz]
- Charlie Parker brackets one end of the [[concepts/Kansas-City-jazz]] transition style, with [[people/Count-Basie]] at the other. [src:Kansas-City-jazz]
