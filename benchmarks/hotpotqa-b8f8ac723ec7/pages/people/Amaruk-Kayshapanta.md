# Amaruk Kayshapanta

> Ecuadorian multidisciplinary artist born in Quito on January 30, 1970, whose stage name is Amaruk Kayshapanta. He is known in Spain and Ecuador for his artistic and humanistic work in favor of the human rights of immigrants in Spain, has received several cultural awards, acted in the TV series Hospital Central, and developed the philosophy "Amawtica Amarukiana Desestructuration".

## Key Facts

- Amaruk Caizapanta Anchapacxi was born in [[places/Quito]] on January 30, 1970, and uses the stage name Amaruk Kayshapanta. [src:Amaruk-Kayshapanta]
- He is an Ecuadorian multidisciplinary artist known in Spain and Ecuador for his artistic and humanistic trajectory in favor of the human rights of immigration in Spain. [src:Amaruk-Kayshapanta]
- He is known for his role in the TV series [[media/Hospital-Central]], where he played the character "Edgar". [src:Amaruk-Kayshapanta]
- He created the philosophy [[concepts/Amawtica-Amarukiana-Desestructuration]], which brings the philosophical study of the Andean Worldview to the contemporary world. [src:Amaruk-Kayshapanta]
