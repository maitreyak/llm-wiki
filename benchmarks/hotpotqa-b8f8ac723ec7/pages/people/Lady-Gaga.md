# Lady Gaga

> American singer who co-created the 2011 photo-book Lady Gaga x Terry Richardson with photographer Terry Richardson.

## Key Facts

- Lady Gaga wrote a foreword for [[works/Lady-Gaga-x-Terry-Richardson]] about her relationship with [[people/Terry-Richardson]]. [src:Lady-Gaga-x-Terry-Richardson]
- Gaga was photographed by Richardson over a ten-month period spanning from her [[events/The-Monster-Ball-Tour]] performance to the 2011 Grammy Awards. [src:Lady-Gaga-x-Terry-Richardson]
- Gaga and Richardson had collaborated on other projects prior to the shooting of the book. [src:Lady-Gaga-x-Terry-Richardson]
