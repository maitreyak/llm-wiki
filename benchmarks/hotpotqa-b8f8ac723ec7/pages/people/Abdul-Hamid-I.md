# Abdul Hamid I

> Ottoman Sultan, half-brother of Esma Sultan (1726–1788) and father of Esma Sultan (1778–1848).

## Key Facts

- Abdul Hamid I was an Ottoman Sultan. [src:Esma-Sultan-daughter-of-Ahmed-III]
- He was the half-brother of the Ottoman princess [[people/Esma-Sultan-daughter-of-Ahmed-III]]. [src:Esma-Sultan-daughter-of-Ahmed-III]
- His fellow Sultan [[people/Mustafa-III]] was also a half-brother of Esma Sultan. [src:Esma-Sultan-daughter-of-Ahmed-III]
- He was the father of the Ottoman princess [[people/Esma-Sultan-daughter-of-Abdul-Hamid-I]] (1778–1848). [src:Esma-Sultan-daughter-of-Abdul-Hamid-I]
- Sultans [[people/Mustafa-IV]] and [[people/Mahmud-II]] were brothers of his daughter Esma Sultan. [src:Esma-Sultan-daughter-of-Abdul-Hamid-I]
