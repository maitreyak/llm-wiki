# Bernhard Bötel

> German operatic tenor and actor (1883–1953) active in Germany and Austria during the first half of the 20th century.

## Key Facts

- Bernhard Bötel lived from 1883 to 1953. [src:Bernhard-Botel]
- Bernhard Bötel was a German operatic tenor and actor. [src:Bernhard-Botel]
- He had an active career in Germany and Austria during the first half of the 20th century. [src:Bernhard-Botel]
- He made recordings for several record labels during the early years of the recording industry, including His Master's Voice, Odeon Records, Polydor Records, Tri-Ergon, and Vox Records. [src:Bernhard-Botel]
- On stage he sang a variety of roles in operas and operettas, from leading parts to comprimario roles. [src:Bernhard-Botel]
- His repertoire included Belmonte in [[people/Wolfgang-Amadeus-Mozart]]'s "Die Entführung aus dem Serail". [src:Bernhard-Botel]
- His repertoire included Chapelou in [[people/Adolphe-Adam]]'s "Le postillon de Lonjumeau". [src:Bernhard-Botel]
- His repertoire included Count Almaviva in [[people/Gioachino-Rossini]]'s "The Barber of Seville". [src:Bernhard-Botel]
- His repertoire included Daniel in [[people/Franz-Lehar]]'s "The Merry Widow". [src:Bernhard-Botel]
- His repertoire included the Duke of Mantua in [[people/Giuseppe-Verdi]]'s "Rigoletto". [src:Bernhard-Botel]
- His repertoire included Gabriel von Eisenstein in [[people/Johann-Strauss-II]]'s "Die Fledermaus" and Indigo in Strauss' "Indigo und die vierzig Räuber". [src:Bernhard-Botel]
- His repertoire included Jeník in [[people/Bedrich-Smetana]]'s "The Bartered Bride". [src:Bernhard-Botel]
- His repertoire included Paolino in [[people/Domenico-Cimarosa]]'s "Il matrimonio segreto". [src:Bernhard-Botel]
- His repertoire included Pâris in [[people/Jacques-Offenbach]]'s "La belle Hélène". [src:Bernhard-Botel]
- His repertoire included Pietro in [[people/Franz-von-Suppe]]'s "Boccaccio". [src:Bernhard-Botel]
- His repertoire included Wilhelm Meister in [[people/Ambroise-Thomas]]' "Mignon". [src:Bernhard-Botel]
