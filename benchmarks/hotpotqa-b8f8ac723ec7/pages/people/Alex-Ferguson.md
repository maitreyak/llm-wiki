# Alex Ferguson

> Scottish former football manager and player who managed Manchester United from 1986 to 2013, widely regarded as one of the greatest managers of all time; before the 1995–96 season he sold several experienced players without making major signings and instead promoted a group of young players, going on to win a second Double.

## Key Facts

- Sir Alexander Chapman Ferguson, CBE, was born on 31 December 1941. [src:Alex-Ferguson]
- Alex Ferguson is a Scottish former football manager and player. [src:Alex-Ferguson]
- Ferguson managed [[organizations/Manchester-United-F-C]] from 1986 to 2013. [src:Alex-Ferguson]
- Ferguson is regarded by many players, managers and analysts as one of the greatest and most successful managers of all time. [src:Alex-Ferguson]
- Alex Ferguson sold [[people/Paul-Ince]], [[people/Mark-Hughes]] and [[people/Andrei-Kanchelskis]] before the start of the 1995–96 season. [src:1995-96-Manchester-United-F-C-season]
- Ferguson made no major signings ahead of the 1995–96 season, instead drafting in young players including [[people/Nicky-Butt]], [[people/David-Beckham]], [[people/Paul-Scholes]], [[people/Gary-Neville]] and [[people/Phil-Neville]]. [src:1995-96-Manchester-United-F-C-season]
- Ferguson led [[organizations/Manchester-United-F-C]] to the [[concepts/The-Double]] in [[events/1995-96-Manchester-United-F-C-season]]. [src:1995-96-Manchester-United-F-C-season]
- Ferguson is the only Manchester United manager to surpass [[people/Matt-Busby]]'s managerial records and longevity at the club. [src:Matt-Busby]
