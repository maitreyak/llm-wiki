# Margaret Cushing Osgood

> Wife of Dr. Hamilton Osgood of Beacon Hill and mother of Gretchen Osgood Warren and Molly Childers.

## Key Facts

- Margaret Cushing Osgood of [[places/Beacon-Hill]], Boston, Massachusetts, was the wife of [[people/Hamilton-Osgood]]. [src:Gretchen-Osgood-Warren]
- She was the mother of [[people/Gretchen-Osgood-Warren]] and of her younger sister Mary Alden Childers ([[people/Molly-Childers]]). [src:Gretchen-Osgood-Warren]
- Margaret Cushing Osgood of Beacon Hill, Boston, was the mother of Mary Alden Osgood Childers ([[people/Molly-Childers]]). [src:Molly-Childers]
