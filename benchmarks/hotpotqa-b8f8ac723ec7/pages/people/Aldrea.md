# Aldrea

> An Andalite who, with Dak Hamee, tried to save the Hork-Bajir world from the Yeerk invasion.

## Key Facts

- Aldrea is an [[concepts/Andalite]]. [src:The-Hork-Bajir-Chronicles]
- Aldrea and her companion [[people/Dak-Hamee]] tried to save their world from the Yeerk invasion. [src:The-Hork-Bajir-Chronicles]
- Aldrea is one of the three points of view from which Jara Hamee's story is narrated in [[works/The-Hork-Bajir-Chronicles]]. [src:The-Hork-Bajir-Chronicles]
