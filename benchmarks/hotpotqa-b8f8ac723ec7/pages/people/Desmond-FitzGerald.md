# Desmond FitzGerald

> First editor of the Irish Bulletin, the official gazette of the Irish Republic's government, until his arrest.

## Key Facts

- Desmond FitzGerald was the first editor of the [[media/Irish-Bulletin]]. [src:Irish-Bulletin]
- He served as editor until his arrest, when he was replaced by [[people/Erskine-Childers]]. [src:Irish-Bulletin]
