# William H. Cook

> Farm owner near Jerome, Idaho, for whose farm the William H. Cook Water Tank House was built around 1915.

## Key Facts

- William H. Cook owned a farm for which the [[places/William-H-Cook-Water-Tank-House]] stored water. [src:William-H-Cook-Water-Tank-House]
- The water tank house associated with William H. Cook's farm was constructed circa 1915 southeast of [[places/Jerome-Idaho]]. [src:William-H-Cook-Water-Tank-House]
