# Erskine Barton Childers

> Irish writer, BBC correspondent and senior United Nations civil servant (1929–1996), grandson of Robert Erskine Childers.

## Key Facts

- Erskine Barton Childers was an Irish writer, BBC correspondent and United Nations senior civil servant. [src:Erskine-Barton-Childers]
- He was the eldest son of [[people/Erskine-Hamilton-Childers]], Ireland's fourth President, and [[people/Ruth-Ellen-Dow-Childers]]. [src:Erskine-Barton-Childers]
- His grandparents were [[people/Molly-Childers]] (Mary Alden Childers) and [[people/Erskine-Childers]]. [src:Erskine-Barton-Childers]
- His grandparents and [[people/Robert-Barton]], a double first cousin of Robert Erskine Childers, were all Irish nationalists heavily involved with the negotiation of Irish independence. [src:Erskine-Barton-Childers]
- His grandfather's involvement in the negotiation of Irish independence ultimately led to his execution during the [[events/Irish-Civil-War]]. [src:Erskine-Barton-Childers]
- His great aunt was [[people/Gretchen-Osgood-Warren]]. [src:Erskine-Barton-Childers]
