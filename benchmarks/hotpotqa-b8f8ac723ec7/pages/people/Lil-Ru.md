# Lil Ru

> American rapper born Sylvester Samuels, from Ridgeway, South Carolina, signed to Def Jam Recordings and known for his debut album 21 & Up.

## Key Facts

- Lil Ru's real name is Sylvester Samuels. [src:Lil-Ru]
- Lil Ru is an American rapper from [[places/Ridgeway-South-Carolina]]. [src:Lil-Ru]
- Lil Ru is currently signed to [[organizations/Def-Jam-Recordings]]. [src:Lil-Ru]
- His debut album, [[works/21-Up]], was released on August 25, 2009. [src:Lil-Ru]
- He was 16 when he made his professional foray into the music business. [src:Lil-Ru]
- He was inspired by New Orleans' innovative [[organizations/Cash-Money-Crew]]. [src:Lil-Ru]
- [[people/Angie-Stone]] helped him secure a deal with [[organizations/Elektra-Records]], but he became unsigned again among the artists lost in the shuffle after the label merged with [[organizations/Atlantic-Records]]. [src:Lil-Ru]
- Music fans first heard him on his 2001 debut single [[works/Will-Destroy]]. [src:Lil-Ru]
- He released the 2002 follow-up single [[works/Shawty-What-You-Doin]]. [src:Lil-Ru]
- Both "Will Destroy" and "Shawty What You Doin'" reached the Billboard R&B/Hip-Hop charts and helped land him at his next label home, [[organizations/Capitol-Records]]. [src:Lil-Ru]
