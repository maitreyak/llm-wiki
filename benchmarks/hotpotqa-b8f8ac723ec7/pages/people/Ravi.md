# Ravi

> South Korean rapper, songwriter and producer signed to Jellyfish Entertainment, known as a member of VIXX and the sub-unit VIXX LR.

## Key Facts

- Ravi is a South Korean rapper, songwriter and producer signed under [[organizations/Jellyfish-Entertainment]]. [src:List-of-songs-written-by-Ravi]
- Ravi began his career as a rapper in 2012 in the South Korean boy group [[organizations/VIXX]]. [src:List-of-songs-written-by-Ravi]
- Ravi's songwriting career began with his participation in co-writing VIXX's debut single "[[works/Super-Hero]]". [src:List-of-songs-written-by-Ravi]
- As of November 2016, with the release of "VIXX 2016 Conception Ker", Ravi had contributed to the writing and composing of over 46 songs recorded by VIXX. [src:List-of-songs-written-by-Ravi]
- Ravi is widely known for composing and songwriting rap portions for VIXX, as well as lyrics and music. [src:List-of-songs-written-by-Ravi]
