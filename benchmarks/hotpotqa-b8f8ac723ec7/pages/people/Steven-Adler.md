# Steven Adler

> American musician best known as the former drummer and co-songwriter of Guns N' Roses, later of Adler's Appetite and Adler.

## Key Facts

- Steven Adler was born Michael Coletti on January 22, 1965, and is an American musician. [src:Steven-Adler]
- Steven Adler is best known as the former drummer and co-songwriter of the hard rock band [[organizations/Guns-N-Roses]], with whom he achieved worldwide success in the late 1980s. [src:Steven-Adler]
- Adler was fired from [[organizations/Guns-N-Roses]] over his heroin addiction in 1990. [src:Steven-Adler]
- During the 2000s, Adler was the drummer of the band Adler's Appetite, and from 2012 he held the same position in the band Adler. [src:Steven-Adler]
- Adler appeared on the second and fifth seasons of the reality TV show "Celebrity Rehab with Dr. Drew", as well as on the first season of its spin-off "Sober House". [src:Steven-Adler]
- Steven Adler was inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame in 2012 as a member of [[organizations/Guns-N-Roses]]. [src:Steven-Adler]
