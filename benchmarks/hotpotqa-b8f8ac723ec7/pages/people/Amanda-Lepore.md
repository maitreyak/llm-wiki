# Amanda Lepore

> American transgender model, celebutante, singer and performance artist, a former Club Kid known as photographer David LaChapelle's muse and for her music releases with Cazwell.

## Key Facts

- Amanda Lepore was born on November 21, 1967. [src:Amanda-Lepore]
- Amanda Lepore is an American transgender model, celebutante, singer, and performance artist. [src:Amanda-Lepore]
- A former Club Kid, Lepore has appeared in advertising for numerous companies. [src:Amanda-Lepore]
- Lepore is noted as a regular subject and muse in the work of photographer [[people/David-LaChapelle]]. [src:Amanda-Lepore]
- Lepore has also been photographed by many other photographers, such as [[people/Terry-Richardson]]. [src:Amanda-Lepore]
- Lepore participated in LaChapelle's exhibit [[events/Artists-and-Prostitutes-1985-2005]] in [[places/New-York-City]], where she "lived" in a voyeuristic life-sized set. [src:Amanda-Lepore]
- Lepore has released several singles, many of them written by and/or recorded with [[people/Cazwell]]. [src:Amanda-Lepore]
- In 2011, Lepore released her debut studio album, [[works/I-Amanda-Lepore]], on [[organizations/Peace-Bisquit]]. [src:Amanda-Lepore]
