# Douglas J. Bennet

> American political official and college president who led Wesleyan University (1995–2007) and NPR (1983–93), and held State Department and USAID posts under Presidents Carter and Clinton.

## Key Facts

- Douglas Joseph "Doug" Bennet, Jr. was born June 23, 1938. [src:Douglas-J-Bennet]
- He is a former national political official and college president. [src:Douglas-J-Bennet]
- He was the fifteenth president of [[organizations/Wesleyan-University]] from 1995 to 2007. [src:Douglas-J-Bennet]
- He served as Assistant Secretary of State for International Organization Affairs in the Clinton Administration from 1993 to 1995. [src:Douglas-J-Bennet]
- He served as Assistant Secretary of State for Legislative Affairs in the Carter administration from 1977 to 1979. [src:Douglas-J-Bennet]
- He was President and CEO of [[organizations/National-Public-Radio]] from 1983 to 1993. [src:Douglas-J-Bennet]
- He ran the [[organizations/U-S-Agency-for-International-Development]] under President Carter from 1979 to 1981. [src:Douglas-J-Bennet]
