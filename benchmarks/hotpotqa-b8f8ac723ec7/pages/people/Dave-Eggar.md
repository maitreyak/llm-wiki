# Dave Eggar

> Musician whose fourth solo release was Kingston Morning.

## Key Facts

- [[works/Kingston-Morning]] is Dave Eggar's 4th solo release. [src:Kingston-Morning]
- Dave Eggar's Kingston Morning was recorded in Brooklyn, [[places/New-York-City]]; [[places/Kingston-Jamaica]]; and [[places/Big-Stone-Gap]], and released by [[organizations/Domo-Records]]. [src:Kingston-Morning]
- A track from Eggar's Kingston Morning, [[works/Itsbynne-Reel]], was nominated at the [[events/53rd-Grammy-Awards]] for "Best Instrumental Arrangement". [src:Kingston-Morning]
