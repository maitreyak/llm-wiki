# eLDee

> Lanre Dabiri, known by his stage name eLDee, is a former Nigerian-American rapper, singer and record producer who now works as an IT consultant in the United States.

## Key Facts

- Lanre Dabiri was born on May 23, 1977. [src:ELDee]
- He is better known by his stage name Eldee, stylized as eLDee. [src:ELDee]
- He is a former Nigerian-American rapper, singer, and record producer. [src:ELDee]
- He is now an IT consultant based in the [[places/United-States]]. [src:ELDee]
- "Lanre" is a diminutive for the Yoruba name "Olanrewaju", which translates to "Wealth is moving forward". [src:ELDee]
- He has a master's degree in Architecture from the [[organizations/University-of-Lagos]] in Nigeria. [src:ELDee]
- He is an original member of the band [[organizations/Trybesmen]]. [src:ELDee]
- He hails from [[places/Lagos-Island]] in Lagos State, Nigeria. [src:ELDee]
