# Molly Childers

> American-born Irish writer and Irish nationalist (1875–1964), wife of Erskine Childers, co-owner of the yacht Asgard, sister of Gretchen Osgood Warren, and mother of Ireland's fourth President.

## Key Facts

- Mary Alden Osgood Childers, MBE, was born 14 December 1875 and died 1 January 1964. [src:Molly-Childers]
- She was an American-born Irish writer and Irish nationalist. [src:Molly-Childers]
- She was awarded the MBE. [src:Molly-Childers]
- Molly married the writer and Irish nationalist Robert Erskine Childers, i.e. [[people/Erskine-Childers]]. [src:Molly-Childers]
- Her son [[people/Erskine-Hamilton-Childers]] became the fourth President of Ireland. [src:Molly-Childers]
- Molly Childers was the wife of [[people/Erskine-Childers]]. [src:Asgard-yacht]
- Mary Alden Childers was a grandmother of [[people/Erskine-Barton-Childers]]. [src:Erskine-Barton-Childers]
- She was an Irish nationalist heavily involved with the negotiation of Irish independence. [src:Erskine-Barton-Childers]
- Mary Alden Childers was the younger sister of [[people/Gretchen-Osgood-Warren]]. [src:Gretchen-Osgood-Warren]
- She was a daughter of Dr. [[people/Hamilton-Osgood]] and [[people/Margaret-Cushing-Osgood]] of [[places/Beacon-Hill]], Boston, Massachusetts. [src:Gretchen-Osgood-Warren]
- Her older sister was [[people/Gretchen-Osgood-Warren]]. [src:Molly-Childers]
