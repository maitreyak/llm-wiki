# Ferdinando Provesi

> Italian composer (1770–1833) from Parma, Maestro di cappella in Busseto and early tutor of Giuseppe Verdi.

## Key Facts

- Ferdinando Angelo Maria Provesi lived from 1770 to 1833 and was a native of [[places/Parma]], Italy. [src:Ferdinando-Provesi]
- He was regarded as one of the greatest Italian opera composers of the era. [src:Ferdinando-Provesi]
- Provesi is best known as an early tutor of [[people/Giuseppe-Verdi]]. [src:Ferdinando-Provesi]
- He served as "Maestro di cappella" (master of music) at the St. Bartolomeo cathedral in [[places/Busseto]]. [src:Ferdinando-Provesi]
- He was also director of the municipal music school and the local Philharmonic Society. [src:Ferdinando-Provesi]
- He began teaching Verdi in 1824, when the future composer was 11 years old. [src:Ferdinando-Provesi]
