# Robert Barton

> Irish nationalist, politician and farmer (1881–1975) who participated in the Anglo-Irish Treaty negotiations and was the cousin of Robert Erskine Childers.

## Key Facts

- Robert Barton was a double first cousin of [[people/Erskine-Childers]]. [src:Erskine-Barton-Childers]
- Robert Barton was an Irish nationalist heavily involved with the negotiation of Irish independence. [src:Erskine-Barton-Childers]
- He was a relative of [[people/Erskine-Barton-Childers]]. [src:Erskine-Barton-Childers]
- His full name was Robert Childers Barton and he was born on 4 March 1881 and died on 10 August 1975. [src:Robert-Barton]
- He was an Irish nationalist, politician and farmer. [src:Robert-Barton]
- He participated in the negotiations leading up to the signature of the [[events/Anglo-Irish-Treaty]]. [src:Robert-Barton]
- His father was [[people/Charles-William-Barton]] and his mother was [[people/Agnes-Childers]]. [src:Robert-Barton]
- His wife was [[people/Rachel-Warren]] of Boston, daughter of [[people/Fiske-Warren]]. [src:Robert-Barton]
- [[people/Erskine-Childers]] was his double first cousin and close friend. [src:Robert-Barton]
- He was named as a cousin of [[people/Erskine-Childers]], alongside [[people/Hugh-Childers]]. [src:Robert-Erskine-Childers]
