# Indriati Iskak

> Indonesian actress turned psychologist and marketing consultant, best known for her role in the 1957 film Tiga Dara.

## Key Facts

- Indriati Gerald Bernardina was born on 9 June 1942 in [[places/Surabaya]]. [src:Indriati-Iskak]
- She is known by her stage name Indriati Iskak and, after marriage, as Indri Makki. [src:Indriati-Iskak]
- She is an Indonesian actress who later became a psychologist and marketer. [src:Indriati-Iskak]
- She soared to popularity in the Indonesian film industry with [[works/Tiga-Dara]] (1957), directed by [[people/Usmar-Ismail]]. [src:Indriati-Iskak]
- She appeared in eight further films and established her own girl group before retiring from cinema in 1963. [src:Indriati-Iskak]
- She worked with [[organizations/Unilever]] for twenty-six years. [src:Indriati-Iskak]
- Since 1994 she has been a marketing consultant with [[organizations/Makki-Makki]]. [src:Indriati-Iskak]
