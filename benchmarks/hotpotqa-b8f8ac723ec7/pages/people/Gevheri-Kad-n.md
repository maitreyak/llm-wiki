# Gevheri Kadın

> Fifth wife of Sultan Abdülaziz (1856–1884), mother of Şehzade Mehmed Seyfeddin and Esma Sultan.

## Key Facts

- Gevheri Kadın was a wife of Sultan [[people/Abdulaziz]]. [src:Esma-Sultan-daughter-of-Abdulaziz]
- She was the daughter of Salih Bey Svatnba. [src:Esma-Sultan-daughter-of-Abdulaziz]
- She was the mother of Esma Sultan daughter of Abdülaziz. [src:Esma-Sultan-daughter-of-Abdulaziz]
- Gevheri Kadın was born on 8 July 1856 and died on 6 September 1884. [src:Gevheri-Kad-n]
- She was the fifth wife of [[people/Abdulaziz]], the 32nd Ottoman Sultan. [src:Gevheri-Kad-n]
- She was the mother of [[people/Sehzade-Mehmed-Seyfeddin]] and of [[people/Esma-Sultan-daughter-of-Abdulaziz]] of the Ottoman Empire. [src:Gevheri-Kad-n]
