# Adriana Trigiani

> Adriana Trigiani is an Italian American best-selling author of sixteen books, television writer, film director, and entrepreneur based in Greenwich Village, New York City. She has published a novel a year since 2000, and she wrote and directed the 2014 film Big Stone Gap, based on her own novel.
