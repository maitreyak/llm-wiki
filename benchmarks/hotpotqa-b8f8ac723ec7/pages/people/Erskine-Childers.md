# Erskine Childers

> English-born writer and Irish nationalist (1870–1922) who wrote The Riddle of the Sands, smuggled guns to Ireland in the yacht Asgard, and was executed during the Irish Civil War.

## Key Facts

- Erskine Childers was an English-born writer and Irish nationalist. [src:Asgard-yacht]
- Erskine Childers and his wife [[people/Molly-Childers]] owned the yacht [[works/Asgard]]. [src:Asgard-yacht]
- Robert Erskine Childers was a grandfather of [[people/Erskine-Barton-Childers]]. [src:Erskine-Barton-Childers]
- He was heavily involved with the negotiation of Irish independence, which ultimately led to his execution during the [[events/Irish-Civil-War]]. [src:Erskine-Barton-Childers]
- [[people/Robert-Barton]] was his double first cousin and a fellow Irish nationalist. [src:Erskine-Barton-Childers]
- Robert Erskine Childers became editor of the [[media/Irish-Bulletin]] after the arrest of its first editor, [[people/Desmond-FitzGerald]]. [src:Irish-Bulletin]
- The [[places/Childers-Road]] in Limerick is named after Erskine Childers. [src:R509-road-Ireland]
- He was the double first cousin and close friend of [[people/Robert-Barton]]. [src:Robert-Barton]
- He was the son of the British Orientalist scholar [[people/Robert-Caesar-Childers]] and [[people/Anna-Barton]] of Ireland. [src:Robert-Caesar-Childers]
- He was born on 25 June 1870 and died on 24 November 1922, and held the DSC. [src:Robert-Erskine-Childers]
- He was universally known as Erskine Childers. [src:Robert-Erskine-Childers]
- He was a Fenian revolutionary who smuggled guns to Ireland in his sailing yacht [[works/Asgard]]. [src:Robert-Erskine-Childers]
- He was executed by the authorities of the nascent [[organizations/Irish-Free-State]] during the [[events/Irish-Civil-War]]. [src:Robert-Erskine-Childers]
- He was a cousin of [[people/Hugh-Childers]] and of [[people/Robert-Barton]]. [src:Robert-Erskine-Childers]
- He was the father of the fourth President of Ireland, [[people/Erskine-Hamilton-Childers]]. [src:Robert-Erskine-Childers]
