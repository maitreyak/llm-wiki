# Fosco Giachetti

> Italian actor who played the title role in the 1938 film Giuseppe Verdi.

## Key Facts

- Fosco Giachetti starred as the composer Verdi in the 1938 film [[people/Giuseppe-Verdi]]. [src:Giuseppe-Verdi-film]
- His casting as Verdi was intended to emphasise the composer's patriotism, as he had recently played patriotic roles in films such as "The White Squadron". [src:Giuseppe-Verdi-film]
