# Terry Richardson

> American fashion and portrait photographer known for advertising campaigns and magazine work; also a writer for Today's Zaman and a figure featured in Snoecks.

## Key Facts

- Terrence "Uncle Terry" Richardson was born on August 14, 1965. [src:Terry-Richardson]
- Terry Richardson is an American fashion and portrait photographer. [src:Terry-Richardson]
- Richardson has shot advertising campaigns for [[people/Marc-Jacobs]], Aldo, Supreme, Sisley, Tom Ford, and Yves Saint Laurent, among others. [src:Terry-Richardson]
- Richardson has done work for magazines such as "Rolling Stone", "GQ", "Vogue", "Vanity Fair", "Harper's Bazaar", "i-D", and "Vice". [src:Terry-Richardson]
- Terry Richardson wrote for [[organizations/Todays-Zaman]]. [src:Piano-Girl]
- Terry Richardson is one of the many photographers, besides [[people/David-LaChapelle]], who have photographed [[people/Amanda-Lepore]]. [src:Amanda-Lepore]
- Terry Richardson is among the photographers who have photographed model [[people/Annie-Morton]]. [src:Annie-Morton]
- Richardson photographed Lady Gaga over a ten-month period, from her [[events/The-Monster-Ball-Tour]] performance to the 2011 Grammy Awards, producing more than 350 pictures for the book. [src:Lady-Gaga-x-Terry-Richardson]
- Richardson and Lady Gaga had collaborated on other projects prior to the shooting of the book. [src:Lady-Gaga-x-Terry-Richardson]
- Terry Richardson is among the artists featured in recent editions of the Belgian magazine [[media/Snoecks]]. [src:Snoecks]
