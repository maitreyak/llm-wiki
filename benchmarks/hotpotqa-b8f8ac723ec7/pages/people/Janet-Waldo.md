# Janet Waldo

> American radio and voice actress (1920–2016) best known for voicing Judy Jetson, Penelope Pitstop, and Josie, and for playing the title role on radio's Meet Corliss Archer.

## Key Facts

- Janet Marie Waldo was an American radio and voice actress. [src:Janet-Waldo]
- Janet Waldo was born on February 4, 1920. [src:Janet-Waldo]
- Janet Waldo died on June 12, 2016. [src:Janet-Waldo]
- In animation she is best known for voicing Judy Jetson. [src:Janet-Waldo]
- She voiced Nancy in [[works/Shazzan]]. [src:Janet-Waldo]
- She voiced Penelope Pitstop. [src:Janet-Waldo]
- She voiced Josie in [[works/Josie-and-the-Pussycats]]. [src:Janet-Waldo]
