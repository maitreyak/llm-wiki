# Charles H. Percy

> Republican U.S. Senator from Illinois who won a third and final term in the 1978 Senate election.

## Key Facts

- Charles H. Percy was the incumbent Republican United States Senator who ran for re-election to a third term in 1978. [src:United-States-Senate-election-in-Illinois-1978]
- Percy was expected to coast to re-election over first-time candidate [[people/Alex-Seith]], but the race became competitive. [src:United-States-Senate-election-in-Illinois-1978]
- In the final days of the 1978 campaign, Percy ran a television advertisement apologizing and saying, "I got your message and you're right." [src:United-States-Senate-election-in-Illinois-1978]
- Percy edged out Seith in the [[events/United-States-Senate-election-in-Illinois-1978]] to win his third and final term in the [[organizations/United-States-Senate]]. [src:United-States-Senate-election-in-Illinois-1978]
