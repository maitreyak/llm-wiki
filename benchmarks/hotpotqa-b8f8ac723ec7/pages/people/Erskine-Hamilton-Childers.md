# Erskine Hamilton Childers

> Fourth President of Ireland (1973–74), son of Erskine Childers and Molly Childers, father of Erskine Barton Childers.

## Key Facts

- Erskine Hamilton Childers was Ireland's fourth President. [src:Erskine-Barton-Childers]
- He served as the fourth President of Ireland from 1973 to 1974. [src:Gretchen-Osgood-Warren]
- He was the son of [[people/Molly-Childers]] and Robert Erskine Childers, and became the fourth President of Ireland. [src:Molly-Childers]
- He was the father of [[people/Erskine-Barton-Childers]], his eldest son. [src:Erskine-Barton-Childers]
- His wife was [[people/Ruth-Ellen-Dow-Childers]]. [src:Erskine-Barton-Childers]
- He was a son of [[people/Erskine-Childers]] and [[people/Molly-Childers]]. [src:Erskine-Barton-Childers]
- He was the grandson of the Orientalist scholar [[people/Robert-Caesar-Childers]]. [src:Robert-Caesar-Childers]
- He was the son of the writer and revolutionary [[people/Erskine-Childers]] and became the fourth President of Ireland. [src:Robert-Erskine-Childers]
