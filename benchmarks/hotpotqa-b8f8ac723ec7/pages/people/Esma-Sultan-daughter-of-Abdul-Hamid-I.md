# Esma Sultan (daughter of Abdul Hamid I)

> Ottoman princess (1778–1848), daughter of Sultan Abdul Hamid I and adoptive mother of Bezmiâlem Sultan and Rahime Perestu Sultan.

## Key Facts

- Esma Sultan was born on 17 July 1778 and died on 4 June 1848. [src:Esma-Sultan-daughter-of-Abdul-Hamid-I]
- She was an Ottoman princess, the daughter of Sultan [[people/Abdul-Hamid-I]]. [src:Esma-Sultan-daughter-of-Abdul-Hamid-I]
- She was the sister of Sultan [[people/Mustafa-IV]] and Sultan [[people/Mahmud-II]]. [src:Esma-Sultan-daughter-of-Abdul-Hamid-I]
- She was the adoptive mother of [[people/Bezmialem-Sultan]] and [[people/Rahime-Perestu-Sultan]]. [src:Esma-Sultan-daughter-of-Abdul-Hamid-I]
