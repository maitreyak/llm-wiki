# Robert R. Hood

> American government official serving as Assistant Secretary of Defense for Legislative Affairs, with prior roles at the White House, the Department of Defense, the National Nuclear Security Administration, and CH2M.

## Key Facts

- Robert R. Hood is an American government official who currently serves as Assistant Secretary of Defense for Legislative Affairs. [src:Robert-R-Hood]
- Hood was previously vice president for government affairs for [[organizations/CH2M]]. [src:Robert-R-Hood]
- Hood served at the White House as Special Assistant to the President in the Office of Legislative Affairs. [src:Robert-R-Hood]
- At the [[organizations/United-States-Department-of-Defense]], Hood served as principal deputy assistant secretary for legislative affairs and as deputy under secretary of defense for budget and appropriations affairs. [src:Robert-R-Hood]
- Hood was director of congressional affairs at the [[organizations/National-Nuclear-Security-Administration]]. [src:Robert-R-Hood]
