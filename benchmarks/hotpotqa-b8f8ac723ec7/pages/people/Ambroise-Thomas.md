# Ambroise Thomas

> French composer (1811–1896), celebrated for his operas Mignon and Hamlet, and Director of the Conservatoire de Paris from 1871 until his death.

## Key Facts

- Charles Louis Ambroise Thomas was born on 5 August 1811 and died on 12 February 1896. [src:Ambroise-Thomas]
- Ambroise Thomas was a French composer. [src:Ambroise-Thomas]
- He is best known for his operas [[works/Mignon]] (1866) and [[works/Hamlet]] (1868). [src:Ambroise-Thomas]
- His opera Hamlet (1868) was based on a work by Shakespeare. [src:Ambroise-Thomas]
- He served as Director of the [[organizations/Conservatoire-de-Paris]] from 1871 until his death. [src:Ambroise-Thomas]
- His opera Hamlet was premiered at the [[organizations/Paris-Opera]] in 1868, with [[people/Armand-Castelmary]] creating the role of Horatio. [src:Armand-Castelmary]
- The tenor [[people/Bernhard-Botel]] sang Wilhelm Meister in his opera Mignon. [src:Bernhard-Botel]
