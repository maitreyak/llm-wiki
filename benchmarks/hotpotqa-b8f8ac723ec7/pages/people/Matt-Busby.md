# Matt Busby

> Scottish football player and manager (1909–1994), born in Bellshill, who managed Manchester United from 1945 to 1969 and again during the second half of the 1970–71 season, widely regarded as one of the greatest managers of all time.

## Key Facts

- Matt Busby was a Scottish football player and manager. [src:Matt-Busby]
- Busby managed [[organizations/Manchester-United-F-C]] between 1945 and 1969. [src:Matt-Busby]
- Busby returned to manage [[organizations/Manchester-United-F-C]] for the second half of the 1970–71 season. [src:Matt-Busby]
- Busby is widely regarded as one of the greatest managers of all time. [src:Matt-Busby]
- Busby's managerial records and longevity at the helm of [[organizations/Manchester-United-F-C]] are surpassed only by [[people/Alex-Ferguson]]. [src:Matt-Busby]
- Busby was born in [[places/Bellshill]] in 1909 and died in 1994. [src:Sir-Matt-Busby-Sports-Complex]
- The [[places/Sir-Matt-Busby-Sports-Complex]], a public leisure centre in Bellshill, is named in his honour. [src:Sir-Matt-Busby-Sports-Complex]
