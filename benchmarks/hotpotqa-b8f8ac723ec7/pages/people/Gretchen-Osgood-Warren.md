# Gretchen Osgood Warren

> American actress, singer and poet (1868–1961), wife of Fiske Warren, older sister of Molly Childers and great aunt of Erskine Barton Childers.

## Key Facts

- Gretchen Osgood Warren was born 19 March 1868 and died in September 1961. [src:Gretchen-Osgood-Warren]
- She was an actress, singer and poet. [src:Gretchen-Osgood-Warren]
- She was the wife of [[people/Fiske-Warren]]. [src:Gretchen-Osgood-Warren]
- She was the daughter of [[people/Hamilton-Osgood]] and [[people/Margaret-Cushing-Osgood]] of [[places/Beacon-Hill]], Boston, Massachusetts. [src:Gretchen-Osgood-Warren]
- Her younger sister was Mary Alden Childers, i.e. [[people/Molly-Childers]], the wife of writer and Irish nationalist [[people/Erskine-Childers]]. [src:Gretchen-Osgood-Warren]
- She was the older sister of [[people/Molly-Childers]]. [src:Molly-Childers]
- Her nephew [[people/Erskine-Hamilton-Childers]] served as the fourth President of Ireland from 1973 to 1974. [src:Gretchen-Osgood-Warren]
- Gretchen Osgood Warren was the great aunt of [[people/Erskine-Barton-Childers]]. [src:Erskine-Barton-Childers]
