# Nicky Butt

> Manchester United midfielder, drafted into the team by Alex Ferguson for the 1995–96 season, who scored in the 1996 FA Charity Shield and features in The Class of '92.

## Key Facts

- Nicky Butt was one of the young players drafted in by [[people/Alex-Ferguson]] for the 1995–96 season. [src:1995-96-Manchester-United-F-C-season]
- Nicky Butt scored for [[organizations/Manchester-United-F-C]] in the [[events/1996-FA-Charity-Shield]]. [src:1996-FA-Charity-Shield]
- Nicky Butt is one of the six young [[organizations/Manchester-United-F-C]] footballers featured in the 2013 documentary [[media/The-Class-of-92]]. [src:The-Class-of-92]
