# PJ Morton

> American singer-songwriter who released and entirely produced the 2017 album Gumbo.

## Key Facts

- [[works/Gumbo]] is PJ Morton's fourth studio album and his first self-released album. [src:Gumbo-PJ-Morton-album]
- PJ Morton's third studio album was New Orleans, released in 2013. [src:Gumbo-PJ-Morton-album]
- PJ Morton produced the album Gumbo entirely himself. [src:Gumbo-PJ-Morton-album]
- Gumbo was released through [[organizations/Morton-Records]] on April 14, 2017. [src:Gumbo-PJ-Morton-album]
