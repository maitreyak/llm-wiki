# United States Senate election in Illinois, 1978

> The November 7, 1978 U.S. Senate election in Illinois, in which Republican incumbent Charles H. Percy narrowly defeated Democrat Alex Seith.

## Key Facts

- The 1978 United States Senate election in Illinois took place on November 7, 1978. [src:United-States-Senate-election-in-Illinois-1978]
- Incumbent Republican Senator [[people/Charles-H-Percy]] ran for re-election to a third term in the [[organizations/United-States-Senate]]. [src:United-States-Senate-election-in-Illinois-1978]
- Though Percy had been expected to coast to re-election over Seith, a first-time candidate, the election quickly became competitive. [src:United-States-Senate-election-in-Illinois-1978]
- In the last few days of the campaign, a desperate Percy ran a television advertisement in which he apologized and acknowledged, "I got your message and you're right." [src:United-States-Senate-election-in-Illinois-1978]
- Percy's last-ditch effort appeared to have paid off, as he edged out Seith to win what would be his third and final Senate term. [src:United-States-Senate-election-in-Illinois-1978]
- The election was held in [[places/Illinois]]. [src:United-States-Senate-election-in-Illinois-1978]
