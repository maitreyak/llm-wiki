# 2007 Memorial Cup

> The 89th Memorial Cup tournament, played in May 2007 at the Pacific Coliseum in Vancouver, won by the host Vancouver Giants over the Medicine Hat Tigers 3–1 in an all-WHL final, and setting a new attendance record of 121,561.

## Key Facts

- The 2007 MasterCard Memorial Cup was played in May 2007 in [[places/Vancouver]], British Columbia, at the [[places/Pacific-Coliseum]]. [src:2007-Memorial-Cup]
- It was the 89th annual [[concepts/Memorial-Cup]] competition and determined the major junior ice hockey champion of the [[organizations/Canadian-Hockey-League]]. [src:2007-Memorial-Cup]
- The tournament was contested by the [[organizations/Western-Hockey-League]] champion [[organizations/Medicine-Hat-Tigers]], the [[organizations/Ontario-Hockey-League]] champion [[organizations/Plymouth-Whalers]], the [[organizations/Quebec-Major-Junior-Hockey-League]] champion [[organizations/Lewiston-Maineiacs]], and host team the [[organizations/Vancouver-Giants]]. [src:2007-Memorial-Cup]
- The tournament used a four-team round-robin format. [src:2007-Memorial-Cup]
- The Giants won their first Memorial Cup by defeating Medicine Hat 3–1 in the final. [src:2007-Memorial-Cup]
- The 2007 final was only the second all-WHL final in Memorial Cup history, the first having been in 1989. [src:2007-Memorial-Cup]
- The tournament set a new Memorial Cup attendance record with 121,561 fans attending its nine games. [src:2007-Memorial-Cup]
- The previous attendance record of 84,686 had been set at the 2003 tournament in Quebec City. [src:2007-Memorial-Cup]
