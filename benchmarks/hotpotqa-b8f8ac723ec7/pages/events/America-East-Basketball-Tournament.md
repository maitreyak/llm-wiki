# America East Basketball Tournament

> The America East Basketball Tournament is the conference postseason tournament whose champion receives an automatic bid to the NCAA Tournament; Vermont won it in 2011–12 and 2016–17.

## Key Facts

- The [[events/2011-12-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]] were champions of the America East Basketball Tournament. [src:2011-12-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]
- The champion of the America East Basketball Tournament earns the [[organizations/America-East-Conference]]'s automatic bid into the NCAA Tournament. [src:2011-12-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]
- In 2011, [[organizations/Stony-Brook-Seawolves]] defeated Vermont in the America East Conference tournament semifinals. [src:2010-11-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]
- The [[events/2016-17-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]] won the tournament by defeating [[organizations/Maine-Black-Bears]], [[organizations/New-Hampshire-Wildcats]] and [[organizations/Albany-Great-Danes]]. [src:2016-17-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]
