# 2015-16 Vermont Catamounts men's basketball team

> The 2015–16 Vermont Catamounts men's basketball team, coached by fifth-year head coach John Becker, went 23–14 overall and 11–5 in America East play, lost the conference tournament final to Stony Brook, and reached the College Basketball Invitational semifinals.

## Key Facts

- The 2015–16 Vermont Catamounts men's basketball team represented the [[organizations/University-of-Vermont]] during the 2015–16 NCAA Division I men's basketball season. [src:2015-16-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]
- The team was led by fifth year head coach [[people/John-Becker]]. [src:2015-16-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]
- The Catamounts played their home games at [[places/Patrick-Gym]] and were members of the [[organizations/America-East-Conference]]. [src:2015-16-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]
- Vermont finished the season 23–14 overall and 11–5 in America East play, tying for third place. [src:2015-16-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]
- Vermont beat [[organizations/Maine-Black-Bears]] and [[organizations/New-Hampshire-Wildcats]] to advance to the championship game of the [[events/America-East-Basketball-Tournament]], where it lost to [[organizations/Stony-Brook-Seawolves]]. [src:2015-16-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]
- Vermont was invited to the [[events/2016-College-Basketball-Invitational]], defeating [[organizations/Western-Carolina-Catamounts]] and [[organizations/Seattle-Redhawks]] before losing to [[organizations/Nevada-Wolf-Pack]] in the semifinals. [src:2015-16-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]
