# 2012 NCAA Tournament

> The 2012 NCAA Division I men's basketball tournament featured Vermont as the America East automatic qualifier; the Catamounts won a First Four game over Lamar and lost to North Carolina in the second round.

- The [[events/2011-12-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]] earned the [[organizations/America-East-Conference]]'s automatic bid into the 2012 NCAA Tournament. [src:2011-12-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]
- The 2012 NCAA Tournament included a "First Four" round, in which Vermont defeated [[organizations/Lamar-Cardinals]]. [src:2011-12-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]
- Vermont fell to [[organizations/North-Carolina-Tar-Heels]] in the second round of the 2012 NCAA Tournament. [src:2011-12-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]
