# 2012–13 Vermont Catamounts men's basketball team

> The 2012–13 Vermont Catamounts men's basketball team, coached by second-year head coach John Becker, went 21–12 (11–5 America East) to tie for second place, lost the America East Tournament final to Albany, and fell to Santa Clara in the first round of the 2013 College Basketball Invitational.

## Key Facts

- The 2012–13 Vermont Catamounts men's basketball team represented the [[organizations/University-of-Vermont]] during the 2012–13 NCAA Division I men's basketball season. [src:2012-13-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]
- The team was led by second year head coach [[people/John-Becker]]. [src:2012-13-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]
- The Catamounts played their home games at [[places/Patrick-Gym]] and were members of the [[organizations/America-East-Conference]]. [src:2012-13-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]
- Vermont finished the season 21–12 overall and 11–5 in America East play, tying for second place in the conference. [src:2012-13-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]
- The Catamounts advanced to the championship game of the [[events/America-East-Basketball-Tournament]], where they lost to [[organizations/Albany-Great-Danes]]. [src:2012-13-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]
- Vermont was invited to the [[events/2013-College-Basketball-Invitational]], losing in the first round to [[organizations/Santa-Clara-Broncos]]. [src:2012-13-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]
