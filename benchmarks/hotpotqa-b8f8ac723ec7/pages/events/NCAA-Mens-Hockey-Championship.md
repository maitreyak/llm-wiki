# NCAA Men's Hockey Championship

> The NCAA Men's Hockey Championship is the national college ice hockey tournament, whose late rounds are known as the Frozen Four.

## Key Facts

- The [[organizations/Vermont-Catamounts-mens-ice-hockey]] team has appeared in the NCAA Men's Hockey Championship five times since moving to Division I in 1974–75. [src:Vermont-Catamounts-mens-ice-hockey]
- Vermont reached the championship's Frozen Four in 1996 and 2009. [src:Vermont-Catamounts-mens-ice-hockey]
