# 2013–14 Vermont Catamounts men's basketball team

> The 2013–14 Vermont Catamounts men's basketball team went 22–11 (15–1 America East) to win the America East regular season title under third-year coach John Becker, lost in the conference tournament semifinals to Albany, and fell to Georgia in the first round of the National Invitation Tournament.

## Key Facts

- The 2013–14 Vermont Catamounts men's basketball team represented the [[organizations/University-of-Vermont]] during the 2013–14 NCAA Division I men's basketball season. [src:2013-14-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]
- The team was led by third year head coach [[people/John-Becker]]. [src:2013-14-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]
- The Catamounts played their home games at [[places/Patrick-Gym]] and were members of the [[organizations/America-East-Conference]]. [src:2013-14-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]
- Vermont finished the season 22–11 overall and 15–1 in America East play to win the America East regular season championship. [src:2013-14-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]
- The Catamounts advanced to the semifinals of the [[events/America-East-Basketball-Tournament]], where they lost to [[organizations/Albany-Great-Danes]]. [src:2013-14-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]
- As a regular season conference champion that failed to win its conference tournament, Vermont received an automatic bid to the [[events/National-Invitation-Tournament]]. [src:2013-14-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]
- Vermont lost in the first round of the National Invitation Tournament to [[organizations/Georgia-Bulldogs]]. [src:2013-14-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]
