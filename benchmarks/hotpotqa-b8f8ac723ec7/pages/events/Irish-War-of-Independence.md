# Irish War of Independence

> Conflict during which the Irish Republic's Department of Propaganda produced the Irish Bulletin.

## Key Facts

- The [[media/Irish-Bulletin]] was produced by the Department of Propaganda during the Irish War of Independence. [src:Irish-Bulletin]
- During the war, the Irish Republic's government issued its official gazette, the Irish Bulletin, in weekly editions from 11 November 1919 to 11 July 1921. [src:Irish-Bulletin]
