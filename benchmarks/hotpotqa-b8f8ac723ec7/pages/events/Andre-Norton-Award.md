# Andre Norton Award

> Annual award presented by the Science Fiction and Fantasy Writers of America for the best young adult or middle grade science fiction or fantasy book published in the United States in the preceding year.

## Key Facts

- The Andre Norton Award for Young Adult Science Fiction and Fantasy is an annual award presented by the [[organizations/Science-Fiction-and-Fantasy-Writers-of-America]]. [src:Andre-Norton-Award]
- The award honors the best young adult or middle grade science fiction or fantasy book published in the United States in the preceding year. [src:Andre-Norton-Award]
- It was established by then SFWA president [[people/Catherine-Asaro]] together with the SFWA Young Adult Fiction committee. [src:Andre-Norton-Award]
- The award was announced on February 20, 2005. [src:Andre-Norton-Award]
- Any published young adult or middle grade science fiction or fantasy novel is eligible, including graphic novels, with no limit on word count. [src:Andre-Norton-Award]
- The award is presented along with the [[events/Nebula-Awards]] and follows the same rules for nominations and voting. [src:Andre-Norton-Award]
- Because the awards are separate, works may be simultaneously nominated for both the Andre Norton Award and a Nebula Award. [src:Andre-Norton-Award]
