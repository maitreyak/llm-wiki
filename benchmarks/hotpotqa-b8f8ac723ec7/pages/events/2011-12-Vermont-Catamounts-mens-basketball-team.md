# 2011–12 Vermont Catamounts men's basketball team

> The 2011–12 Vermont Catamounts men's basketball team represented the University of Vermont in the 2011–12 NCAA Division I season, finishing 24–12 and winning the America East tournament to reach the NCAA Tournament, where it beat Lamar in the First Four before losing to North Carolina.

## Key Facts

- The 2011–12 Vermont Catamounts men's basketball team represented the [[organizations/University-of-Vermont]] during the 2011–12 NCAA Division I men's basketball season. [src:2011-12-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]
- The team was led by first year head coach [[people/John-Becker]]. [src:2011-12-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]
- The Catamounts played their home games at [[places/Patrick-Gym]] and were members of the [[organizations/America-East-Conference]]. [src:2011-12-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]
- Vermont finished the season 24–12 overall and 13–3 in America East play, good for second place in the conference. [src:2011-12-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]
- The Catamounts were champions of the [[events/America-East-Basketball-Tournament]] and earned the conference's automatic bid into the [[events/2012-NCAA-Tournament]]. [src:2011-12-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]
- Vermont defeated [[organizations/Lamar-Cardinals]] in the "First Four" round of the 2012 NCAA Tournament before falling to [[organizations/North-Carolina-Tar-Heels]] in the second round. [src:2011-12-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]
