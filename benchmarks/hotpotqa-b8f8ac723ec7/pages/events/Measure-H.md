# Measure H

> 2004 Mendocino County ballot ordinance that banned GMO cultivation, production and distribution.

## Key Facts

- Measure H was an ordinance banning the cultivation, production or distribution of genetically modified organisms in [[places/Mendocino-County]], California. [src:Mendocino-County-GMO-Ban]
- Measure H was passed by referendum on March 2, 2004. [src:Mendocino-County-GMO-Ban]
- Measure H was met with opposition by several interest groups representing the biotechnology industry, including the [[organizations/Western-Plant-Health-Association]] and [[organizations/CropLife-America]]. [src:Mendocino-County-GMO-Ban]
