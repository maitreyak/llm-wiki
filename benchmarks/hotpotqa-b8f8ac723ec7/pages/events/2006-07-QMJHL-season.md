# 2006–07 QMJHL season

> The 38th season of the Quebec Major Junior Hockey League, played from September 14, 2006 to March 18, 2007, featuring eighteen teams playing 70 games each. The Lewiston Maineiacs dominated, winning both the regular-season Jean Rougeau Trophy and the President's Cup playoff title.

## Key Facts

- The 2006–07 QMJHL season was the 38th season in the history of the [[organizations/Quebec-Major-Junior-Hockey-League]]. [src:2006-07-QMJHL-season]
- The regular season ran from September 14, 2006 to March 18, 2007. [src:2006-07-QMJHL-season]
- Eighteen teams each played 70 games in the 2006–07 schedule. [src:2006-07-QMJHL-season]
- The [[organizations/Lewiston-Maineiacs]] finished first overall in the regular season, winning their first [[concepts/Jean-Rougeau-Trophy]]. [src:2006-07-QMJHL-season]
- Lewiston won 16 playoff games and lost only one en route to their first [[concepts/Presidents-Cup]]. [src:2006-07-QMJHL-season]
- In the 2006–07 playoff finals, Lewiston defeated the [[organizations/Val-dOr-Foreurs]]. [src:2006-07-QMJHL-season]
