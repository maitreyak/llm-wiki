# 2011–12 QMJHL season

> The 43rd season of the Quebec Major Junior Hockey League, with seventeen teams playing 68 games each from September 2011 to March 2012. It marked the debut of the Blainville-Boisbriand Armada and ended with the Saint John Sea Dogs winning a second straight President's Cup.

## Key Facts

- The 2011–12 QMJHL season was the 43rd season of the [[organizations/Quebec-Major-Junior-Hockey-League]]. [src:2011-12-QMJHL-season]
- The regular season consisted of seventeen teams each playing 68 games, beginning in September 2011 and ending in March 2012. [src:2011-12-QMJHL-season]
- The season was the first in the league for the [[organizations/Blainville-Boisbriand-Armada]]. [src:2011-12-QMJHL-season]
- The league entered the season having lost charter team the [[organizations/Lewiston-Maineiacs]], which folded after the previous season. [src:2011-12-QMJHL-season]
- During this period the QMJHL announced an expansion team for [[places/Sherbrooke]] for the 2012–13 season. [src:2011-12-QMJHL-season]
- In the 2011–12 playoffs, the [[organizations/Saint-John-Sea-Dogs]] became the seventh team in league history to capture consecutive [[concepts/Presidents-Cup]] championships. [src:2011-12-QMJHL-season]
