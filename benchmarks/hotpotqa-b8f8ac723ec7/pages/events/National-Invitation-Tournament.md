# National Invitation Tournament

> The National Invitation Tournament is a postseason college basketball tournament; Vermont was invited in 2011 and again in 2014 via an automatic bid as America East regular season champion, losing in the first round both times.

## Key Facts

- The 2010–11 Vermont Catamounts mens basketball team was invited to the National Invitation Tournament. [src:2010-11-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]
- Vermont lost its National Invitation Tournament first-round game to [[organizations/Cleveland-State-Vikings]] by a score of 63–60. [src:2010-11-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]
- The National Invitation Tournament grants automatic bids to regular season conference champions who fail to win their conference tournament, which is how the [[events/2013-14-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]] qualified. [src:2013-14-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]
- Vermont lost in the first round of the National Invitation Tournament to [[organizations/Georgia-Bulldogs]] in 2014. [src:2013-14-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]
