# FA Charity Shield

> Annual English football match played between the winners of the previous season's Premier League and FA Cup competitions.

## Key Facts

- The FA Charity Shield is an annual football match played between the winners of the previous season's [[organizations/Premier-League]] and [[events/FA-Cup]] competitions. [src:1996-FA-Charity-Shield]
- The [[events/1996-FA-Charity-Shield]] was the 74th edition of the FA Charity Shield. [src:1996-FA-Charity-Shield]
- The competition was known as the Littlewoods FA Charity Shield in 1996 for sponsorship reasons. [src:1996-FA-Charity-Shield]
