# Acts of Union 1707

> The 1707 Acts of Union, after which formerly English government posts such as the Lord High Treasurer became British.

## Key Facts

- Since the Acts of Union of 1707, the post of [[concepts/Lord-High-Treasurer]] has been a British government position rather than an English one. [src:Lord-High-Treasurer]
