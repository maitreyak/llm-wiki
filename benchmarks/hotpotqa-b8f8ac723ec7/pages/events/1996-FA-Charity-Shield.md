# 1996 FA Charity Shield

> The 74th FA Charity Shield, played on 11 August 1996 at Wembley Stadium, in which Manchester United beat Newcastle United 4–0.

## Key Facts

- The 1996 FA Charity Shield was the 74th [[events/FA-Charity-Shield]]. [src:1996-FA-Charity-Shield]
- The match was also known as the Littlewoods FA Charity Shield for sponsorship reasons. [src:1996-FA-Charity-Shield]
- The match was played on 11 August 1996 at [[places/Wembley-Stadium]]. [src:1996-FA-Charity-Shield]
- The match was contested by [[organizations/Manchester-United-F-C]], who had won the [[concepts/The-Double]] of [[organizations/Premier-League]] and [[events/FA-Cup]] in 1995–96, and [[organizations/Newcastle-United-F-C]], who had finished as Premier League runners-up. [src:1996-FA-Charity-Shield]
- Manchester United won the match 4–0. [src:1996-FA-Charity-Shield]
- The goals were scored by [[people/Eric-Cantona]], [[people/Nicky-Butt]], [[people/David-Beckham]] and [[people/Roy-Keane]]. [src:1996-FA-Charity-Shield]
