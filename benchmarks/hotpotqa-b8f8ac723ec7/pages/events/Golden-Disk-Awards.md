# Golden Disk Awards

> South Korean music awards ceremony at which Shinee won Newcomer, Disk Bonsang and Popularity awards.

## Key Facts

- At the 23rd Golden Disk Awards, [[works/The-Shinee-World]] won Newcomer Album of the Year. [src:List-of-awards-and-nominations-received-by-Shinee]
- At the 25th Golden Disk Awards, [[works/Lucifer]] won the Disk Bonsang Award as well as the Popularity Award. [src:List-of-awards-and-nominations-received-by-Shinee]
- At the 27th Golden Disc Awards, [[organizations/Shinee]] was awarded a Disk Bonsang Award for the EP [[works/Sherlock]]. [src:List-of-awards-and-nominations-received-by-Shinee]
