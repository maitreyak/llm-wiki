# 1995–96 Manchester United F.C. season

> Manchester United's 1995–96 campaign, their fourth Premier League season and 21st consecutive season in the English top division, in which they became the first English team to win the Double twice while fielding a squad rebuilt around young players.

## Key Facts

- The 1995–96 season was [[organizations/Manchester-United-F-C]]'s fourth season in the [[organizations/Premier-League]]. [src:1995-96-Manchester-United-F-C-season]
- The 1995–96 season was Manchester United's 21st consecutive season in the top division of English football. [src:1995-96-Manchester-United-F-C-season]
- United finished the 1995–96 season by becoming the first English team to win the [[concepts/The-Double]] (league title and [[events/FA-Cup]]) twice. [src:1995-96-Manchester-United-F-C-season]
- Before the start of the 1995–96 season, [[people/Alex-Ferguson]] sold experienced players [[people/Paul-Ince]], [[people/Mark-Hughes]] and [[people/Andrei-Kanchelskis]] and did not make any major signings. [src:1995-96-Manchester-United-F-C-season]
- Ferguson instead drafted in young players such as [[people/Nicky-Butt]], [[people/David-Beckham]], [[people/Paul-Scholes]] and the Neville brothers, [[people/Gary-Neville]] and [[people/Phil-Neville]]. [src:1995-96-Manchester-United-F-C-season]
