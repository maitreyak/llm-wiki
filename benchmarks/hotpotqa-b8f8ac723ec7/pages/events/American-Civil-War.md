# American Civil War

> Conflict whose first battlefield was the Missouri–Kansas border after Kansas was opened to Euro-American settlement in 1854.

## Key Facts

- In 1854, when [[places/Kansas]] was opened to Euro-American settlement, the [[places/Missouri]]–Kansas border became the first battlefield in the conflict in the American Civil War. [src:History-of-the-Kansas-City-metropolitan-area]
