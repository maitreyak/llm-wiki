# 2016-17 Vermont Catamounts men's basketball team

> The 2016–17 Vermont Catamounts men's basketball team went 29–6 overall and 16–0 in America East play, won the conference regular season and tournament titles, and lost to Purdue in the first round of the NCAA Tournament as a No. 13 seed.

## Key Facts

- The team was led by sixth-year head coach [[people/John-Becker]]. [src:2016-17-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]
- The Catamounts played their home games at [[places/Patrick-Gym]] in Burlington, Vermont. [src:2016-17-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]
- The team was a member of the [[organizations/America-East-Conference]]. [src:2016-17-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]
- Vermont finished the season 29–6 overall and 16–0 in America East play to win the America East regular season championship. [src:2016-17-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]
- In the [[events/America-East-Basketball-Tournament]], Vermont defeated [[organizations/Maine-Black-Bears]], [[organizations/New-Hampshire-Wildcats]] and [[organizations/Albany-Great-Danes]] to win the tournament championship. [src:2016-17-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]
- Winning the conference tournament gave Vermont the America East automatic bid to the [[events/2017-NCAA-Tournament]]. [src:2016-17-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]
- As a No. 13 seed in the Midwest region, Vermont lost to No. 4-seeded [[organizations/Purdue-Boilermakers]] in the first round. [src:2016-17-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]
