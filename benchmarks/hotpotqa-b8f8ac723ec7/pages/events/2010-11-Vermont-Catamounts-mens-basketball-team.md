# 2010–11 Vermont Catamounts men's basketball team

> The 2010–11 Vermont Catamounts men's basketball team represented the University of Vermont during the 2010–11 NCAA Division I men's basketball season, winning a third consecutive America East regular season title and reaching the National Invitation Tournament.

## Key Facts

- The 2010–11 Vermont Catamounts men's basketball team represented the [[organizations/University-of-Vermont]] in the 2010–11 NCAA Division I men's basketball season. [src:2010-11-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]
- The Catamounts won their third consecutive [[organizations/America-East-Conference]] regular season championship. [src:2010-11-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]
- The team lost in the semifinals of the America East Conference tournament to [[organizations/Stony-Brook-Seawolves]]. [src:2010-11-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]
- Vermont was invited to the [[events/National-Invitation-Tournament]] but lost in the first round to [[organizations/Cleveland-State-Vikings]], 63–60. [src:2010-11-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]
