# Nebula Awards

> Science fiction and fantasy awards presented alongside the Andre Norton Award under the same nomination and voting rules.

## Key Facts

- The [[events/Andre-Norton-Award]] is presented along with the Nebula Awards and follows the same rules for nominations and voting. [src:Andre-Norton-Award]
- The Nebula Awards and the Andre Norton Award are separate awards, so a work may be nominated for both simultaneously. [src:Andre-Norton-Award]
