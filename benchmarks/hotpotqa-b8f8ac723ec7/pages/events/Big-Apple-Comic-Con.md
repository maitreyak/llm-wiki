# Big Apple Comic Con

> Annual New York City comic convention established by promoter Michael Carbonaro in 1996.

## Key Facts

- The Big Apple Comic Con was established in 1996 by promoter [[people/Michael-Carbonaro]] as an annual [[places/New-York-City]] event. [src:Great-Eastern-Conventions]
- The establishment of the Big Apple Comic Con marked the end of the gap in New York City shows filled by [[organizations/Great-Eastern-Conventions]]. [src:Great-Eastern-Conventions]
