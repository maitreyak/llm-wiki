# Corliss Archer

> Teenage character portrayed by Shirley Temple in the 1945 comedy film Kiss and Tell.

## Key Facts

- Corliss Archer is the character played by [[people/Shirley-Temple]] in the 1945 comedy film [[works/Kiss-and-Tell]]. [src:Kiss-and-Tell-1945-film]
- Shirley Temple was 17 years old when she played Corliss Archer. [src:Kiss-and-Tell-1945-film]
- Kiss and Tell, in which Corliss Archer appears, was followed by the 1949 sequel [[works/A-Kiss-for-Corliss]]. [src:A-Kiss-for-Corliss]
