# Esma Sultan

> "Esma Sultan" is a name shared by three daughters of three different Ottoman Sultans.

## Key Facts

- Esma Sultan is the name of three daughters of three Ottoman Sultans. [src:Esma-Sultan]
- The name Esma Sultan is associated with Ottoman dynastic princesses, each being a sultan's daughter. [src:Esma-Sultan]
- One bearer of the name is Esma Sultan daughter of Abdülaziz (1873–1899), daughter of Sultan Abdülaziz. [src:Esma-Sultan-daughter-of-Abdulaziz]
- Another bearer of the name is [[people/Esma-Sultan-daughter-of-Ahmed-III]] (1726–1788), daughter of Sultan [[people/Ahmed-III]] and Zeynep Kadın. [src:Esma-Sultan-daughter-of-Ahmed-III]
- A further bearer of the name is [[people/Esma-Sultan-daughter-of-Abdul-Hamid-I]] (1778–1848), daughter of Sultan [[people/Abdul-Hamid-I]]. [src:Esma-Sultan-daughter-of-Abdul-Hamid-I]
- The [[places/Esma-Sultan-Mansion]] in Ortaköy, Istanbul, is named after its original owner Esma Sultan. [src:Esma-Sultan-Mansion]
- An Ottoman princess named Esma Sultan married [[people/Kucuk-Huseyin-Pasha]], who thereby became a [[concepts/Damat]] of the Ottoman dynasty. [src:Kucuk-Huseyin-Pasha]
