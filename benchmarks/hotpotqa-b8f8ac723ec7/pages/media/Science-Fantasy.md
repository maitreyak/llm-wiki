# Science Fantasy

> British fantasy and science fiction magazine published from 1950, later retitled Impulse and SF Impulse.

## Key Facts

- Science Fantasy was a British fantasy and science fiction magazine that also appeared under the titles Impulse and SF Impulse. [src:Science-Fantasy-magazine]
- It was launched in 1950 by [[organizations/Nova-Publications]] as a companion to Nova's [[media/New-Worlds]]. [src:Science-Fantasy-magazine]
- The titles were acquired by [[organizations/Roberts-Vinter]], who hired [[people/Kyril-Bonfiglioli]] to edit Science Fantasy. [src:Science-Fantasy-magazine]
- Bonfiglioli changed the title to Impulse in early 1966, which caused confusion with distributors and led to falling sales, though the magazine remained profitable. [src:Science-Fantasy-magazine]
- The title was changed again to SF Impulse for the last few issues. [src:Science-Fantasy-magazine]
- The magazine ceased publication in 1967, when Roberts & Vinter came under financial pressure after their printer went bankrupt. [src:Science-Fantasy-magazine]
