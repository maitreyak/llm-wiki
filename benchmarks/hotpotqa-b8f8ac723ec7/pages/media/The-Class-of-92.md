# The Class of '92

> 2013 British documentary film about the rise of six young Manchester United footballers from 1992.

## Key Facts

- The Class of '92 is a 2013 British documentary film. [src:The-Class-of-92]
- The Class of '92 was released on 1 December 2013. [src:The-Class-of-92]
- The Class of '92 centres on the rise of six young [[organizations/Manchester-United-F-C]] footballers: [[people/David-Beckham]], [[people/Nicky-Butt]], [[people/Ryan-Giggs]], [[people/Gary-Neville]], [[people/Phil-Neville]] and [[people/Paul-Scholes]]. [src:The-Class-of-92]
- The Class of '92 details the six players' careers for [[organizations/Manchester-United-F-C]] starting in 1992. [src:The-Class-of-92]
