# Giuseppe Verdi (1953 film)

> 1953 Italian biographical film about the composer Giuseppe Verdi, directed by Raffaello Matarazzo and starring Pierre Cressoy.

## Key Facts

- Giuseppe Verdi is a 1953 Italian biographical film. [src:Verdi-the-King-of-Melody]
- The film was directed by [[people/Raffaello-Matarazzo]]. [src:Verdi-the-King-of-Melody]
- It stars [[people/Pierre-Cressoy]]. [src:Verdi-the-King-of-Melody]
- It is based on adult life events of the composer [[people/Giuseppe-Verdi]]. [src:Verdi-the-King-of-Melody]
- The film was released theatrically in the USA as The Life and Music of Giuseppe Verdi. [src:Verdi-the-King-of-Melody]
- It was released on video as Verdi, the King of Melody. [src:Verdi-the-King-of-Melody]
- The film was a commercial success, grossing over 957 million lire at the Italian box office. [src:Verdi-the-King-of-Melody]
