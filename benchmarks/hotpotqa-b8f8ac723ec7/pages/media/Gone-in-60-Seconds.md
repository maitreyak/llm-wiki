# Gone in 60 Seconds

> 2000 American action heist film directed by Dominic Sena, starring Nicolas Cage and Angelina Jolie, and a loose remake of the 1974 H.B. Halicki film.

## Key Facts

- Gone in 60 Seconds is a 2000 American action heist film. [src:Gone-in-60-Seconds-2000-film]
- Gone in 60 Seconds stars [[people/Nicolas-Cage]], [[people/Angelina-Jolie]], [[people/Giovanni-Ribisi]], [[people/Christopher-Eccleston]], [[people/Robert-Duvall]], [[people/Vinnie-Jones]], and [[people/Will-Patton]]. [src:Gone-in-60-Seconds-2000-film]
- The film was directed by [[people/Dominic-Sena]] and written by [[people/Scott-Rosenberg]]. [src:Gone-in-60-Seconds-2000-film]
- The film was produced by [[people/Jerry-Bruckheimer]]. [src:Gone-in-60-Seconds-2000-film]
- Gone in 60 Seconds is a loose remake of the 1974 [[people/H-B-Halicki]] film of the same name. [src:Gone-in-60-Seconds-2000-film]
- Sena and Cage, who worked together on Gone in 60 Seconds, later reunited on [[media/Season-of-the-Witch]]. [src:Season-of-the-Witch-2011-film]
