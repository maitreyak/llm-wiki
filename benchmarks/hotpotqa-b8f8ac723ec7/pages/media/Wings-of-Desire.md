# Wings of Desire

> 1987 film by Wim Wenders, set in Berlin, whose German title is Der Himmel über Berlin; loosely remade as City of Angels.

## Key Facts

- Wings of Desire (German title: Der Himmel über Berlin) is a 1987 film directed by [[people/Wim-Wenders]]. [src:City-of-Angels-film]
- The film was set in Berlin. [src:City-of-Angels-film]
- It tells the story of an angel who falls in love with a mortal woman and wishes to become human to be with her. [src:City-of-Angels-film]
- [[media/City-of-Angels]] (1998) is a loose remake of Wings of Desire. [src:City-of-Angels-film]
