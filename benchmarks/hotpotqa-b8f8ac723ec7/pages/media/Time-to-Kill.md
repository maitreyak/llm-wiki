# Time to Kill

> 1989 Italian drama film directed by Giuliano Montaldo, starring Nicolas Cage, set in 1936 colonial Ethiopia.

## Key Facts

- Time to Kill (Italian: Tempo di uccidere) is a 1989 Italian drama film. [src:Time-to-Kill-1989-film]
- Time to Kill stars [[people/Nicolas-Cage]] and Italian actors [[people/Ricky-Tognazzi]] and [[people/Giancarlo-Giannini]]. [src:Time-to-Kill-1989-film]
- Time to Kill is directed by [[people/Giuliano-Montaldo]]. [src:Time-to-Kill-1989-film]
- The film is set in 1936, when [[places/Ethiopia]] was an Italian colony. [src:Time-to-Kill-1989-film]
- Time to Kill was filmed in [[places/Zimbabwe]]. [src:Time-to-Kill-1989-film]
- The film is based on the novel of the same name written by [[people/Ennio-Flaiano]]. [src:Time-to-Kill-1989-film]
