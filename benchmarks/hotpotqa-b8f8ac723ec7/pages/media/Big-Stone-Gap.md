# Big Stone Gap

> 2014 American drama romantic comedy film written and directed by Adriana Trigiani, based on her 2000 novel of the same name.

## Key Facts

- Big Stone Gap is a 2014 American drama romantic comedy film. [src:Big-Stone-Gap-film]
- Big Stone Gap was written and directed by [[people/Adriana-Trigiani]]. [src:Big-Stone-Gap-film]
- Big Stone Gap was produced by [[people/Donna-Gigliotti]] for [[organizations/Altar-Identity-Studios]]. [src:Big-Stone-Gap-film]
- The film is based on Adriana Trigiani's 2000 best-selling novel of the same name. [src:Big-Stone-Gap-film]
- The story of Big Stone Gap is set in the actual Virginia town of [[places/Big-Stone-Gap]] circa the 1970s. [src:Big-Stone-Gap-film]
- Big Stone Gap had its world premiere at the [[events/Virginia-Film-Festival]] on November 6, 2014. [src:Big-Stone-Gap-film]
