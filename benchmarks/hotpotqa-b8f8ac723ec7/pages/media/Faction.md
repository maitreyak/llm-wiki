# Faction

> Uncensored mixed hard rock, punk, hip hop, and heavy metal channel on Sirius XM, hosted under the name Faction With Jason Ellis.

## Key Facts

- Faction With Jason Ellis is an uncensored hard rock, punk, hip hop, and heavy metal mixed music channel on [[organizations/Sirius-XM]] Satellite Radio. [src:Faction-Punk]
- Until mid-July 2017, Faction appeared on Sirius XM channel 41. [src:Faction-Punk]
- In mid-July 2017, Faction was temporarily replaced by [[organizations/Guns-N-Roses]] radio. [src:Faction-Punk]
- After August 16, 2017, channel 41 was rebranded to [[media/Turbo]], and Faction moved to channel 314, Turbo's previous channel. [src:Faction-Punk]
- Faction is available on select Sirius XM radios, Sirius XM streaming, and the Sirius XM smartphone app. [src:Faction-Punk]
