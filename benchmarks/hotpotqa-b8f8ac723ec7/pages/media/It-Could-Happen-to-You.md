# It Could Happen to You

> 1994 American romantic comedy-drama film starring Nicolas Cage and Bridget Fonda about a police officer who splits his lottery winnings with a waitress.

## Key Facts

- It Could Happen to You is a 1994 American romantic comedy-drama film. [src:It-Could-Happen-to-You-1994-film]
- The film stars [[people/Nicolas-Cage]] and [[people/Bridget-Fonda]]. [src:It-Could-Happen-to-You-1994-film]
- The story follows a [[places/New-York-City]] police officer, played by Cage, who wins the lottery and splits his winnings with a waitress, played by Fonda. [src:It-Could-Happen-to-You-1994-film]
- The film's basic premise was inspired by a real-life incident. [src:It-Could-Happen-to-You-1994-film]
