# David Beckham's Soccer USA

> British football highlights and discussion show presented by Tim Lovejoy, produced and broadcast by Five, following David Beckham's move to Los Angeles Galaxy.

## Key Facts

- David Beckham's Soccer USA was a football highlights and general discussion show presented by [[people/Tim-Lovejoy]]. [src:David-Beckhams-Soccer-USA]
- The show was produced and broadcast in the United Kingdom by [[organizations/Five]]. [src:David-Beckhams-Soccer-USA]
- The show began following [[people/David-Beckham]]'s move to [[organizations/Los-Angeles-Galaxy]]. [src:David-Beckhams-Soccer-USA]
- [[people/David-Beckham]] often contributed to the show in the form of interviews. [src:David-Beckhams-Soccer-USA]
- Each week there was a special guest in the studio, usually a British sports personality, with whom [[people/Tim-Lovejoy]] chatted about their career and their views on [[organizations/Major-League-Soccer]]. [src:David-Beckhams-Soccer-USA]
- A slightly different version of the show, hosted by [[people/Natalie-Pinkham]] and completely devoid of any content derived from British studio footage, was broadcast in the USA on [[organizations/Fox-Soccer-Channel]]. [src:David-Beckhams-Soccer-USA]
