# City of Angels

> 1998 American romantic fantasy film directed by Brad Silberling, starring Nicolas Cage and Meg Ryan, a loose remake of Wings of Desire.

## Key Facts

- City of Angels is a 1998 American romantic fantasy film. [src:City-of-Angels-film]
- The film was directed by [[people/Brad-Silberling]]. [src:City-of-Angels-film]
- It stars [[people/Nicolas-Cage]] and [[people/Meg-Ryan]]. [src:City-of-Angels-film]
- The film is set in [[places/Los-Angeles]], California. [src:City-of-Angels-film]
- City of Angels is a loose remake of [[media/Wings-of-Desire]], the 1987 film by [[people/Wim-Wenders]] that was set in Berlin. [src:City-of-Angels-film]
- As with the original, the film tells the story of an angel (Cage) who falls in love with a mortal woman (Ryan) and wishes to become human in order to be with her. [src:City-of-Angels-film]
- With the guidance of a man played by [[people/Dennis-Franz]], who has already made the transition from immortality, the angel falls and discovers the human experience. [src:City-of-Angels-film]
