# Season of the Witch

> 2011 American historical fantasy adventure film directed by Dominic Sena, starring Nicolas Cage and Ron Perlman as Teutonic Knights escorting an accused witch to a monastery.

## Key Facts

- Season of the Witch is a 2011 American historical fantasy adventure film. [src:Season-of-the-Witch-2011-film]
- The film stars [[people/Nicolas-Cage]] and [[people/Ron-Perlman]]. [src:Season-of-the-Witch-2011-film]
- The film was directed by [[people/Dominic-Sena]], with extensive uncredited reshoots by [[people/Brett-Ratner]]. [src:Season-of-the-Witch-2011-film]
- Cage and Perlman play Teutonic Knights who return from the Crusades to find their fatherland ruined by the Black Death. [src:Season-of-the-Witch-2011-film]
- The elders command the two knights to transport the girl to a monastery so the monks can lift her curse from the land. [src:Season-of-the-Witch-2011-film]
- The film draws inspiration from the 1957 film [[media/The-Seventh-Seal]]. [src:Season-of-the-Witch-2011-film]
- Season of the Witch reunited Sena and Cage, who had previously worked together on [[media/Gone-in-60-Seconds]]. [src:Season-of-the-Witch-2011-film]
