# Snoecks

> Belgian annual magazine of over 550 pages, published each October, covering new international developments in arts, photography and literature.

## Key Facts

- Snoecks is a Belgian magazine. [src:Snoecks]
- Snoecks is a huge, 550-plus-page magazine that appears once a year in October. [src:Snoecks]
- Snoecks focuses on the most interesting new international developments in the arts, photography and literature. [src:Snoecks]
- Recent editions of Snoecks had features on artists such as Anton Corbijn, Larry Sultan, Matthew Barney, [[people/Terry-Richardson]], Ron Mueck, Alberto Garcia-Alix, Peter Lindbergh, Albert Watson, Desiree Dolron, Bettina Rheims, Diana Scheunemann, Timothy Greenfield-Sanders and Andres Serrano. [src:Snoecks]
