# Hamish & Andy's Gap Year

> Logie Award-winning Australian comedy series following comedians Hamish Blake and Andy Lee as they travel to international locations, with seasons based in America, Europe and Asia.

## Key Facts

- Hamish & Andy's Gap Year is a Logie Award winning comedy series [src:Hamish-and-Andys-Gap-Year]
- The series follows [[people/Hamish-Blake]] and [[people/Andy-Lee]], a pair of Australian comedians, on their trips to various international locations [src:Hamish-and-Andys-Gap-Year]
- The first season saw the pair visiting America for ten episodes, broadcasting their show weekly from [[places/New-York-City]] [src:Hamish-and-Andys-Gap-Year]
- The second season, in 2012, was titled "Hamish & Andy's Euro Gap Year" and consisted of seven episodes broadcast from [[places/The-Lord-Stanley]], a disused pub in East London, England [src:Hamish-and-Andys-Gap-Year]
- The third season, known as "Hamish and Andy's Gap Year Asia", aired in 2013 and was broadcast from a bar in [[places/Bangkok]], Thailand, called [[places/The-Raintree]] [src:Hamish-and-Andys-Gap-Year]
