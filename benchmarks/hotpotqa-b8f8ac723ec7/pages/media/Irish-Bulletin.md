# Irish Bulletin

> Official gazette of the government of the Irish Republic, published weekly from 1919 to 1921 by the Department of Propaganda.

## Key Facts

- The Irish Bulletin was the official gazette of the government of the Irish Republic. [src:Irish-Bulletin]
- It was produced by the Department of Propaganda during the [[events/Irish-War-of-Independence]]. [src:Irish-Bulletin]
- Its offices were originally located at No. 6 Harcourt Street, Dublin. [src:Irish-Bulletin]
- The paper's first editor was [[people/Desmond-FitzGerald]], until his arrest. [src:Irish-Bulletin]
- After FitzGerald's arrest he was replaced as editor by [[people/Erskine-Childers]]. [src:Irish-Bulletin]
- "The Bulletin" appeared in weekly editions from 11 November 1919 to 11 July 1921. [src:Irish-Bulletin]
