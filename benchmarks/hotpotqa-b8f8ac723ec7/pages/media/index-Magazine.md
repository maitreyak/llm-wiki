# index Magazine

> New York City based magazine (1996–2005) known for in-depth interviews with prominent figures in art and culture.

## Key Facts

- index Magazine was a prominent [[places/New-York-City]] based publication featuring in-depth interviews with prominent figures in art and culture. [src:Index-Magazine]
- index Magazine was created by [[people/Peter-Halley]] and [[people/Bob-Nickas]] in 1996. [src:Index-Magazine]
- index Magazine ran until late 2005. [src:Index-Magazine]
- index Magazine covered the burgeoning Indie culture of the 1990s. [src:Index-Magazine]
- index regularly employed rising photographers such as [[people/Juergen-Teller]], [[people/Terry-Richardson]], [[people/Wolfgang-Tillmans]], and [[people/Ryan-McGinley]]. [src:Index-Magazine]
- index featured interviews with figures including [[people/Bjork]], [[people/Brian-Eno]], [[people/Marc-Jacobs]], and [[people/Scarlett-Johansson]]. [src:Index-Magazine]
- Besides famous personalities, index also featured interviews with not-so-famous New York personalities such as Queen Itchie or Ducky Doolittle. [src:Index-Magazine]
