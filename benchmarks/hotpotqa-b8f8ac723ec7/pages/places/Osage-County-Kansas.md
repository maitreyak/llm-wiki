# Osage County, Kansas

> A county in Kansas whose northern portion contains Osage State Fishing Lake.

## Key Facts

- Osage County is a county in [[places/Kansas]]. [src:Osage-State-Fishing-Lake]
- [[places/Osage-State-Fishing-Lake]] is located in northern Osage County. [src:Osage-State-Fishing-Lake]
- US Route 75 runs through the county, about a mile west of Osage State Fishing Lake. [src:Osage-State-Fishing-Lake]
