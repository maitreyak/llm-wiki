# Shawnee County Kansas

> County in Kansas whose northern portion contains Shawnee State Fishing Lake and which includes the city of Topeka.

## Key Facts

- [[places/Shawnee-State-Fishing-Lake]] is located in northern Shawnee County, [[places/Kansas]]. [src:Shawnee-State-Fishing-Lake]
- [[places/Topeka-Kansas]] lies about eight miles southeast of Shawnee State Fishing Lake within the same area of the county. [src:Shawnee-State-Fishing-Lake]
