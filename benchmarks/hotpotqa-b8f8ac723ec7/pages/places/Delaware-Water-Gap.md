# Delaware Water Gap

> Landform on the Lackawanna route modeled in soaring form on the New York Society of Model Engineers' Hoboken layout.

## Key Facts

- The layout depicting the Delaware Water Gap was based on the [[organizations/Lackawanna-Railroad]] between Hoboken and [[places/Scranton-Pennsylvania]]. [src:New-York-Society-of-Model-Engineers]
