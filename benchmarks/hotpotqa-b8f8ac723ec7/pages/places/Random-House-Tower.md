# Random House Tower

> A 52-story mixed-use tower in New York City housing the headquarters of Random House and the Park Imperial luxury apartment complex.

## Key Facts

- The Random House Tower, also known as the Park Imperial Apartments, is a 52-story mixed-use tower in [[places/New-York-City]], United States. [src:Random-House-Tower]
- The tower is used as the headquarters of book publisher [[organizations/Random-House]] and as a luxury apartment complex. [src:Random-House-Tower]
- The book publisher entrance to the tower is on Broadway and goes up to 27 floors. [src:Random-House-Tower]
- The apartment complex entrance to the tower is on West 56th Street. [src:Random-House-Tower]
