# R509 road

> Regional road in Ireland running through the southeastern side of Limerick City, following part of the Childers Road.

## Key Facts

- The R509 road is a regional road in Ireland. [src:R509-road-Ireland]
- The R509 road follows part of the [[places/Childers-Road]]. [src:R509-road-Ireland]
- The R509 runs through the southeastern side of [[places/Limerick-City]]. [src:R509-road-Ireland]
- The R509 forms what is somewhat akin to an inner ring road, albeit mostly two-lane only. [src:R509-road-Ireland]
