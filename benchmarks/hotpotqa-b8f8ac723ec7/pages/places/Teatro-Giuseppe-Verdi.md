# Teatro Giuseppe Verdi

> Small 300-seat opera house in Busseto, Italy, opened on 15 August 1868 and housed in a wing of the Rocca dei Marchesi Pallavicino.

## Key Facts

- Teatro Giuseppe Verdi, in English the Giuseppe Verdi Theatre, is a small opera house in Busseto, Italy. [src:Teatro-Giuseppe-Verdi]
- The theatre is located in a wing of the [[places/Rocca-dei-Marchesi-Pallavicino]] on the Piazza Giuseppe Verdi in [[places/Busseto]]. [src:Teatro-Giuseppe-Verdi]
- The theatre opened on 15 August 1868. [src:Teatro-Giuseppe-Verdi]
- The theatre seats 300. [src:Teatro-Giuseppe-Verdi]
- It is named after the opera composer [[people/Giuseppe-Verdi]], with whose life the town of Busseto is closely associated. [src:Teatro-Giuseppe-Verdi]
