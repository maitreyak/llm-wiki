# South Phoenix

> A region of Phoenix, Arizona, south of the Salt River, comprising South Mountain, Laveen, and Ahwatukee villages.

## Key Facts

- South Phoenix is a region of [[places/Phoenix]], Arizona. [src:South-Phoenix]
- The area includes the Phoenix urban villages of [[places/South-Mountain-Village]], [[places/Laveen-Village]], and [[places/Ahwatukee-Village]]. [src:South-Phoenix]
- Residents sometimes refer to the area simply as "the Southside". [src:South-Phoenix]
- Major arterial east-west streets in South Phoenix include Broadway Road, Southern Avenue, Baseline Road, Dobbins Road, Elliott Road, Warner Road, Chandler Boulevard, and Pecos Road, most of which connect the area with the suburbs of [[places/Tempe]] and [[places/Chandler]]. [src:South-Phoenix]
