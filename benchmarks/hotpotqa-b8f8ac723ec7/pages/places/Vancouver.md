# Vancouver

> A city in British Columbia, Canada, host of the 2007 Memorial Cup at the Pacific Coliseum and home of the Vancouver Giants.

## Key Facts

- Vancouver, British Columbia hosted the [[events/2007-Memorial-Cup]] in May 2007. [src:2007-Memorial-Cup]
- The host venue in Vancouver was the [[places/Pacific-Coliseum]]. [src:2007-Memorial-Cup]
- Vancouver is home to the [[organizations/Vancouver-Giants]], the 2007 Memorial Cup host team and champion. [src:2007-Memorial-Cup]
