# Breezy Point, Minnesota

> A community in Minnesota, United States, home to the Wilford H. Fawcett House and Breezy Point Resort.

## Key Facts

- Breezy Point is a place in Minnesota, United States. [src:Wilford-H-Fawcett-House]
- The [[places/Wilford-H-Fawcett-House]], listed on the National Register of Historic Places, is located in Breezy Point. [src:Wilford-H-Fawcett-House]
- The present-day [[places/Breezy-Point-Resort]] is located there on land bought by [[people/Wilford-H-Fawcett]] in 1920. [src:Wilford-H-Fawcett-House]
