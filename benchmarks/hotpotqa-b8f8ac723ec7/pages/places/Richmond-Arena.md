# Richmond Arena

> Richmond-area venue that hosted University of Richmond Spiders home basketball games from 1954 to 1971.

## Key Facts

- It was one of several off-campus venues used by the Spiders before the [[places/Robins-Center]] opened. [src:Robins-Center]
