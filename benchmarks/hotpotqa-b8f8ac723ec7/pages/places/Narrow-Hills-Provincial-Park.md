# Narrow Hills Provincial Park

> A provincial park in Saskatchewan, Canada, containing recreational facilities and numerous lakes.

## Key Facts

- Narrow Hills Provincial Park is a provincial park in [[places/Saskatchewan]], Canada. [src:Narrow-Hills-Provincial-Park]
- The park contains a series of recreational facilities within its boundaries. [src:Narrow-Hills-Provincial-Park]
- A variety of lakes lie within the park's boundaries, including the Gem Lakes, Lower Fishing Lake, and Upper Fishing Lake. [src:Narrow-Hills-Provincial-Park]
