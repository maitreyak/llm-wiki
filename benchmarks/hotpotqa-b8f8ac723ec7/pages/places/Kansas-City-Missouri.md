# Kansas City, Missouri

> City in Missouri, founded in 1838, anchor of the 15-county Kansas City metropolitan area, whose central business district is Downtown Kansas City, and a cradle of jazz where Kansas City jazz developed.

## Key Facts

- Kansas City, Missouri anchors the 15-county [[places/Kansas-City-metropolitan-area]], which straddles the Missouri–Kansas border. [src:Kansas-City-metropolitan-area]
- [[places/Downtown-Kansas-City]] is the central business district of Kansas City, Missouri. [src:Downtown-Kansas-City]
- Kansas City, Missouri is part of the [[places/Kansas-City-metropolitan-area]], for which Downtown Kansas City also serves as the central business district. [src:Downtown-Kansas-City]
- The City of Kansas City formulated the 2010 Greater Downtown Area Plan, which defines the Greater Downtown Area. [src:Downtown-Kansas-City]
- Kansas City, Missouri was founded in 1838. [src:History-of-the-Kansas-City-metropolitan-area]
- Kansas City, Missouri defeated its rival [[places/Westport]] to become the predominant city west of [[places/St-Louis-Missouri]]. [src:History-of-the-Kansas-City-metropolitan-area]
- Kansas City, Missouri is the anchor city of the Kansas City Metropolitan Area and is located in [[places/Missouri]]. [src:Kansas-City-Scout]
- The [[places/Kansas-City-Scout-Statue]] stands in [[places/Penn-Valley-Park]] in the city, overlooking Downtown Kansas City. [src:Kansas-City-Scout]
- [[concepts/Kansas-City-jazz]] developed in Kansas City, Missouri along with [[places/Kansas-City-Kansas]] and the surrounding metropolitan area during the 1930s. [src:Kansas-City-jazz]
- Kansas City is known as one of the most popular "cradles of jazz". [src:Kansas-City-jazz]
- [[people/Charlie-Parker]], who ushered in the [[concepts/Bebop]] style in the 1940s, was a Kansas City native. [src:Kansas-City-jazz]
- Kansas City, Missouri is part of the Kansas City metropolitan area, for which Downtown Kansas City also serves as the central business district. [src:Downtown-Kansas-City]
- [[places/North-Kansas-City-Missouri]], despite the similarity in name, is an independent municipality separate from Kansas City, Missouri. [src:North-Kansas-City-Missouri]
- Kansas City has annexed far to the north of North Kansas City's northern city limits, almost completely surrounding that city. [src:North-Kansas-City-Missouri]
- North Kansas City was originally a northern suburb across the [[places/Missouri-River]] from Kansas City, Missouri. [src:North-Kansas-City-Missouri]
- The [[organizations/University-of-Missouri-Kansas-City]], a public research university, is located in Kansas City, Missouri. [src:University-of-Missouri-Kansas-City]
- UMKC's main [[places/Volker-Campus]] is located in Kansas City's Rockhill neighborhood, east of the Country Club Plaza. [src:University-of-Missouri-Kansas-City]
