# Dwyer Arena

> Ice hockey arena on the Niagara University campus in Lewiston, New York, home of the Niagara Purple Eagles men's ice hockey team.

## Key Facts

- The Robert and Concetta Dwyer Arena houses two ice surfaces, both 200 x 85 ft., and a pro shop. [src:Dwyer-Arena]
- Dwyer Arena is located on [[organizations/Niagara-University]]'s campus in [[places/Lewiston-New-York]], United States. [src:Dwyer-Arena]
- The arena's main rink can seat up to 1,400 people. [src:Dwyer-Arena]
- The main rink is the home of the [[organizations/Niagara-Purple-Eagles-mens-ice-hockey]] team. [src:Dwyer-Arena]
- The arena was formerly home to Niagara's women's ice hockey team, which played in [[organizations/College-Hockey-America]]. [src:Dwyer-Arena]
