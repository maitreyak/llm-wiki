# Fishing Lake

> A lake in Saskatchewan, Canada, located near Foam Lake and Wadena, prone to flooding due to lacking an effective outlet channel.

## Key Facts

- Fishing Lake is a lake in the Canadian province of [[places/Saskatchewan]]. [src:Fishing-Lake]
- The lake is located between highway 5 and highway 16. [src:Fishing-Lake]
- Fishing Lake lies 22 km north of the town of [[places/Foam-Lake]], Saskatchewan. [src:Fishing-Lake]
- Fishing Lake lies 24 km east of the town of [[places/Wadena]], Saskatchewan. [src:Fishing-Lake]
- The lake does not have an effective outlet channel, and so is prone to flooding. [src:Fishing-Lake]
- Record floods in 2007 resulted in a plan by the [[organizations/Government-of-Saskatchewan]] to lower the level of the lake by digging a drainage channel. [src:Fishing-Lake]
- The [[organizations/Fishing-Lake-First-Nation]] opposed the drainage channel plan, and instead flood control berms were constructed. [src:Fishing-Lake]
