# Laleli Mosque

> 18th-century Ottoman imperial mosque in Laleli, Fatih, Istanbul, Turkey.

## Key Facts

- The Laleli Mosque is an 18th-century Ottoman imperial mosque. [src:Laleli-Mosque]
- Its Turkish name is "Laleli Camii", also rendered as the Tulip Mosque. [src:Laleli-Mosque]
- The mosque is located in [[places/Laleli]], in the district of [[places/Fatih]], [[places/Istanbul]], Turkey. [src:Laleli-Mosque]
