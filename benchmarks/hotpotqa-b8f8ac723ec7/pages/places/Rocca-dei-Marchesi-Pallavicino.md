# Rocca dei Marchesi Pallavicino

> Fortress in Busseto, Italy, formerly the Pallavicino family palace and now the city hall, containing the Teatro Giuseppe Verdi in one wing.

## Key Facts

- The Rocca dei Marchesi Pallavicino is located on the Piazza Giuseppe Verdi in [[places/Busseto]], Italy. [src:Teatro-Giuseppe-Verdi]
- From the 13th century the "rocca" or "fortress" was the Pallavicino family's palace. [src:Teatro-Giuseppe-Verdi]
- The municipality acquired the building in 1856, after which it became the city hall. [src:Teatro-Giuseppe-Verdi]
- A wing of the rocca houses the [[places/Teatro-Giuseppe-Verdi]], a small opera house. [src:Teatro-Giuseppe-Verdi]
