# Missouri River

> River traveled upstream by French fur trappers from St. Louis, meeting the Kansas River at the site of the Kansas City metropolitan area.

## Key Facts

- Frenchmen from [[places/St-Louis-Missouri]] moved up the Missouri River to trap for furs and trade with the Native Americans. [src:History-of-the-Kansas-City-metropolitan-area]
- The [[places/Kansas-City-metropolitan-area]] lies at the confluence of the [[places/Kansas-River]] and the Missouri River. [src:History-of-the-Kansas-City-metropolitan-area]
- The Missouri River forms the northern boundary of [[places/Downtown-Kansas-City]] as defined by the [[organizations/Downtown-Council-of-Kansas-City]]. [src:Downtown-Kansas-City]
- [[places/North-Kansas-City-Missouri]] lies across the Missouri River from [[places/Kansas-City-Missouri]]. [src:North-Kansas-City-Missouri]
