# Woodson

> Woodson is a census-designated place in Pulaski County, Arkansas, United States, named for plantation owner Ed Wood Sr.

## Key Facts

- Woodson is a census-designated place (CDP) in [[places/Pulaski-County]], Arkansas, in the United States. [src:Woodson-Arkansas]
- Woodson's population was 403 at the 2010 census. [src:Woodson-Arkansas]
- Woodson is part of the [[places/Little-Rock-North-Little-Rock-Conway-Metropolitan-Statistical-Area]]. [src:Woodson-Arkansas]
- Woodson, along with Woodson Lake and Wood Hollow, is the namesake for [[people/Ed-Wood-Sr]], a prominent plantation owner, trader, and businessman at the turn of the 20th century. [src:Woodson-Arkansas]
- Woodson is adjacent to the [[places/Wood-Plantation]], the largest of the plantations owned by Ed Wood Sr. [src:Woodson-Arkansas]
