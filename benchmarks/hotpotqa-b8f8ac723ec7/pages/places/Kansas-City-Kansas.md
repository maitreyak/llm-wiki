# Kansas City, Kansas

> City in Kansas, one of the metro area's largest municipalities with over 100,000 residents, where the Kansas City jazz style developed in the 1930s and where the University of Kansas medical school and hospital are located.

## Key Facts

- Kansas City, Kansas is one of the largest cities in the [[places/Kansas-City-metropolitan-area]] besides Kansas City, Missouri, with a population over 100,000. [src:Kansas-City-metropolitan-area]
- Kansas City, Kansas is one of the cities where [[concepts/Kansas-City-jazz]] developed during the 1930s. [src:Kansas-City-jazz]
- Kansas City, Kansas lies within the [[places/Kansas-City-metropolitan-area]]. [src:Kansas-City-jazz]
- Kansas City, Kansas is located in the state of [[places/Kansas]]. [src:Kansas-City-jazz]
- The medical school and hospital of the [[organizations/University-of-Kansas]] are located in Kansas City. [src:University-of-Kansas]
