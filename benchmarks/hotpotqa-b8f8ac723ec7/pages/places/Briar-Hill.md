# Briar Hill

> An inner suburban neighbourhood in northwest Calgary, Alberta, built on an escarpment with views of downtown and the Rocky Mountains.

## Key Facts

- Hounsfield Heights/Briar Hill is an inner suburban neighbourhood in northwest [[places/Calgary]], Alberta, Canada. [src:Hounsfield-Heights-Briar-Hill-Calgary]
- The neighbourhood is located north of the [[places/Hillhurst]] and [[places/West-Hillhurst]] communities. [src:Hounsfield-Heights-Briar-Hill-Calgary]
- Its northern boundary is 16th Avenue N (Trans-Canada Highway) and its eastern boundary is 14th Street W. [src:Hounsfield-Heights-Briar-Hill-Calgary]
- Its southern boundary is the lane north of 7th Avenue N to 19th Street W and 8th Avenue N. [src:Hounsfield-Heights-Briar-Hill-Calgary]
- Its western boundary is formed by Crowchild Trail, 12th Avenue N, Juniper Road, and 22nd Street W. [src:Hounsfield-Heights-Briar-Hill-Calgary]
- [[places/Lions-Park-station]] on the C-Train is located within the community. [src:Hounsfield-Heights-Briar-Hill-Calgary]
- The community is built on an escarpment and is popular for its views of downtown to the south and the Rocky Mountains to the west. [src:Hounsfield-Heights-Briar-Hill-Calgary]
