# Breezy Point Resort

> A Minnesota resort developed by Wilford H. Fawcett beginning in 1920 on a tract of land where he built cottages and a large log lodge.

## Key Facts

- In 1920, [[people/Wilford-H-Fawcett]] bought the tract of land where the present-day Breezy Point Resort is located. [src:Wilford-H-Fawcett-House]
- Fawcett began building cottages and a large log lodge at the resort site. [src:Wilford-H-Fawcett-House]
- The large log lodge has since burnt down. [src:Wilford-H-Fawcett-House]
- The [[places/Wilford-H-Fawcett-House]] was built as a private residence within the resort property. [src:Wilford-H-Fawcett-House]
- The local logging industry had declined before the resort was built. [src:Wilford-H-Fawcett-House]
