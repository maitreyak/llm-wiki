# Clinton, Minnesota

> Clinton is a small city in Big Stone County, Minnesota, United States, named for New York Governor DeWitt Clinton.

- Clinton is a city in [[places/Big-Stone-County-Minnesota]], [[places/Minnesota]], United States. [src:Clinton-Minnesota]
