# Los Angeles

> City in California where filmmaker Scott Derrickson lives, where Guns N' Roses formed in 1985, where film editor Charles Craft died in 1968, the setting of the 1998 film City of Angels, and site of a David Beckham Academy branch.

## Key Facts

- [[people/Scott-Derrickson]], an American director, screenwriter and producer, lives in Los Angeles, California. [src:Scott-Derrickson]
- [[organizations/Guns-N-Roses]] was formed in Los Angeles, California in 1985. [src:Guns-N-Roses-discography]
- The 1998 film [[media/City-of-Angels]] is set in Los Angeles, California. [src:City-of-Angels-film]
- The [[organizations/David-Beckham-Academy]] operated a branch in Los Angeles, California, United States, in 2009. [src:David-Beckham-Academy]
- The California branch of the [[organizations/David-Beckham-Academy]] closed soon after the London site was given up in October 2009. [src:David-Beckham-Academy]
- DJ [[people/Mick-Batyske]] has performed in venues in Los Angeles, among other international cities. [src:Mick-DJ]
