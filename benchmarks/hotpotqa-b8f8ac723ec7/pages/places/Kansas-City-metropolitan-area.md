# Kansas City metropolitan area

> Bi-state, 15-county metropolitan area anchored by Kansas City, Missouri, with about 2,340,000 residents, whose history begins in the 19th century with French fur trappers and traders from St. Louis, and where Kansas City jazz developed in the 1930s.

## Key Facts

- The Kansas City metropolitan area is a 15-county metropolitan area anchored by [[places/Kansas-City-Missouri]]. [src:Kansas-City-metropolitan-area]
- The Kansas City metropolitan area has a population of about 2,340,000. [src:Kansas-City-metropolitan-area]
- The Kansas City metropolitan area ranks as the second largest metropolitan area with its core in [[places/Missouri]], after [[places/Greater-St-Louis]]. [src:Kansas-City-metropolitan-area]
- Alongside Kansas City, the area includes other cities and suburbs, the largest being [[places/Overland-Park-Kansas]], [[places/Kansas-City-Kansas]], [[places/Olathe-Kansas]], and [[places/Independence-Missouri]], each with over 100,000 in population. [src:Kansas-City-metropolitan-area]
- The [[organizations/Mid-America-Regional-Council]] (MARC) serves as the Council of Governments and the Metropolitan Planning Organization for the Kansas City metropolitan area. [src:Kansas-City-metropolitan-area]
- The history of the Kansas City metropolitan area begins in the 19th century as Frenchmen from [[places/St-Louis-Missouri]] moved up the [[places/Missouri-River]] to trap for furs and trade with the Native Americans. [src:History-of-the-Kansas-City-metropolitan-area]
- The Kansas City metropolitan area straddles the border between [[places/Missouri]] and [[places/Kansas]] at the confluence of the [[places/Kansas-River]] and [[places/Missouri-River]]. [src:History-of-the-Kansas-City-metropolitan-area]
- The area's location at the confluence of the Kansas and Missouri Rivers made it a strategic point for commerce and security. [src:History-of-the-Kansas-City-metropolitan-area]
- The Kansas City metropolitan area played a major role in the westward expansion of the United States. [src:History-of-the-Kansas-City-metropolitan-area]
- The [[places/Santa-Fe-Trail]] and [[places/Oregon-Trail]] ran through the area. [src:History-of-the-Kansas-City-metropolitan-area]
- [[places/Downtown-Kansas-City]] serves as the central business district of the Kansas City metropolitan area. [src:Downtown-Kansas-City]
- The [[concepts/Kansas-City-Scout]] electronic traffic management system was created in 2000 to serve the Kansas City Metropolitan Area. [src:Kansas-City-Scout]
- [[concepts/Kansas-City-jazz]] developed in the Kansas City Metropolitan Area, including [[places/Kansas-City-Kansas]] and [[places/Kansas-City-Missouri]], during the 1930s. [src:Kansas-City-jazz]
- [[places/North-Kansas-City-Missouri]], an independent municipality in [[places/Clay-County-Missouri]], is part of the Kansas City metropolitan area. [src:North-Kansas-City-Missouri]
