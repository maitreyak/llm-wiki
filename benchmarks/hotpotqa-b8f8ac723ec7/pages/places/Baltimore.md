# Baltimore

> City in Maryland, birthplace of Robert Hormats.

## Key Facts

- [[people/Robert-Hormats]] was born in Baltimore, Maryland, on April 13, 1943. [src:Robert-Hormats]
- Downtown Baltimore was the location of [[places/Hammerjacks]] Concert Hall and Nightclub through the 1980s and into the 1990s. [src:Hammerjacks]
- [[places/M-T-Bank-Stadium]] is located in Baltimore, and its parking lot replaced the demolished Hammerjacks site. [src:Hammerjacks]
- Baltimore, Maryland is the birthplace of [[people/Conrad-Brooks]], born there on January 3, 1931. [src:Conrad-Brooks]
