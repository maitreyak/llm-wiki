# District of Parry Sound

> A district in central Ontario, Canada, containing the Almaguin Highlands region and Commanda Lake.

## Key Facts

- The District of Parry Sound is in central Ontario, Canada, and includes the Almaguin Highlands region. [src:Commanda-Lake]
