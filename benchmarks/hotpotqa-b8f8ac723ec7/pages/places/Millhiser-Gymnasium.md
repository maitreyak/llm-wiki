# Millhiser Gymnasium

> Former on-campus gymnasium of the University of Richmond that the Spiders outgrew in the mid-1940s.

## Key Facts

- Spider basketball did not return to an on-campus facility after Millhiser Gymnasium until the [[places/Robins-Center]] opened in 1972. [src:Robins-Center]
