# Lake Shawnee

> A lake on the east side of Topeka, Kansas, sometimes confused with Shawnee State Fishing Lake.

## Key Facts

- Lake Shawnee is located on the east side of [[places/Topeka-Kansas]]. [src:Shawnee-State-Fishing-Lake]
