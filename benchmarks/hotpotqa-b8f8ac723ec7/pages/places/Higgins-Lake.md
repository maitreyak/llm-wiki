# Higgins Lake

> A 9,900-acre recreational and fishing lake in Roscommon County, Michigan, the 10th largest lake in the state, noted for deep, clear waters and ringed by small lakeshore communities.

## Key Facts

- Higgins Lake is a large recreational and fishing lake in [[places/Roscommon-County]], in the U.S. state of Michigan. [src:Higgins-Lake]
- Higgins Lake is known for its deep, clear waters. [src:Higgins-Lake]
- Higgins Lake has a maximum width of 4 mi, a length of 7 mi, and a maximum depth of 135 ft. [src:Higgins-Lake]
- Its mean depth is 44 ft and it contains almost 20 billion cubic feet (570 million m³) of water. [src:Higgins-Lake]
- The lake's retention time is about 12.5 years. [src:Higgins-Lake]
- Higgins Lake drains into [[places/Marl-Lake]] by the [[places/Cut-River]], which runs into [[places/Houghton-Lake]] and eventually to [[places/Lake-Michigan]]. [src:Higgins-Lake]
- The lake is situated on the boundary between [[places/Lyon-Township]] on the west and [[places/Gerrish-Township]] on the east. [src:Higgins-Lake-Michigan]
- US 127 passes on the west side of the lake, while I-75 and M-18 both pass a few miles to the east. [src:Higgins-Lake-Michigan]
- The unincorporated community of [[places/Higgins-Lake-Michigan]] lies along the lake's southern and western shores. [src:Higgins-Lake-Michigan]
- [[places/Houghton-Lake]] is just a few miles south of Higgins Lake. [src:Higgins-Lake-Michigan]
