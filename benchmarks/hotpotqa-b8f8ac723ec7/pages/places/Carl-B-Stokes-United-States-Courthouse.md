# Carl B. Stokes United States Courthouse

> A 23-story, 430-foot federal courthouse skyscraper in downtown Cleveland, Ohio, also known as the Stokes Tower.

## Key Facts

- The Carl B. Stokes Federal Court House Building is a skyscraper located in downtown [[places/Cleveland]], Ohio. [src:Carl-B-Stokes-United-States-Courthouse]
- The building has 23 stories and stands 430 ft tall. [src:Carl-B-Stokes-United-States-Courthouse]
- It is located at the corner of Huron Road and Superior Avenue. [src:Carl-B-Stokes-United-States-Courthouse]
- It is currently the fourth tallest United States courthouse in the country. [src:Carl-B-Stokes-United-States-Courthouse]
