# Central Park

> New York City park containing a lake for which the New York Society of Model Engineers sought a permit for model motor boat races.

## Key Facts

- The [[organizations/New-York-Society-of-Model-Engineers]] incorporated in 1926 so it could obtain a permit to use a lake in Central Park for model motor boat races. [src:New-York-Society-of-Model-Engineers]
