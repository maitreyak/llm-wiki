# Gerrish Township

> Michigan township forming the eastern side of the Higgins Lake boundary.

## Key Facts

- [[places/Higgins-Lake]] is situated on the boundary between [[places/Lyon-Township]] on the west and Gerrish Township on the east. [src:Higgins-Lake-Michigan]
- I-75 and M-18 pass a few miles to the east of the lake, while US 127 passes on its west side. [src:Higgins-Lake-Michigan]
