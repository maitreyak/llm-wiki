# Big Stone Gap

> Actual town in Virginia where the story of the film Big Stone Gap is set, circa the 1970s, and one of the recording locations for Dave Eggar's Kingston Morning.

## Key Facts

- Big Stone Gap is an actual town in Virginia. [src:Big-Stone-Gap-film]
- The story of the film [[media/Big-Stone-Gap]] is set in Big Stone Gap circa the 1970s. [src:Big-Stone-Gap-film]
- [[works/Kingston-Morning]], [[people/Dave-Eggar]]'s 4th solo release, was partly recorded in Big Stone Gap, Virginia. [src:Kingston-Morning]
