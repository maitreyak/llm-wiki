# Kansas

> U.S. state opened to Euro-American settlement in 1854 whose border with Missouri became the first battlefield in the conflict in the American Civil War, which contains several large cities of the Kansas City metro area and the University of Kansas.

## Key Facts

- Kansas is a U.S. state. [src:Brown-County-Kansas]
- [[places/Brown-County-Kansas]] is located in the northeast portion of Kansas. [src:Brown-County-Kansas]
- The [[places/Kickapoo-Indian-Reservation-of-Kansas]] is located in Kansas. [src:Brown-County-Kansas]
- The Kansas–Missouri state line forms the western boundary of [[places/Downtown-Kansas-City]] under both the Downtown Council definition and the 2010 Greater Downtown Area Plan. [src:Downtown-Kansas-City]
- Kansas was opened to Euro-American settlement in 1854. [src:History-of-the-Kansas-City-metropolitan-area]
- The [[places/Kansas-City-metropolitan-area]] straddles the border between [[places/Missouri]] and Kansas. [src:History-of-the-Kansas-City-metropolitan-area]
- Part of the Kansas City Metropolitan Area lies in Kansas, and the [[organizations/Kansas-Department-of-Transportation]] partnered with Missouri to create [[concepts/Kansas-City-Scout]]. [src:Kansas-City-Scout]
- [[places/Kansas-City-Kansas]], one of the cities where [[concepts/Kansas-City-jazz]] developed, is in Kansas. [src:Kansas-City-jazz]
- [[places/Overland-Park-Kansas]], [[places/Kansas-City-Kansas]], and [[places/Olathe-Kansas]] are Kansas cities in the Kansas City metropolitan area each with over 100,000 residents. [src:Kansas-City-metropolitan-area]
- The State of Kansas owns [[places/Osage-State-Fishing-Lake]], a man-made lake in [[places/Osage-County-Kansas]]. [src:Osage-State-Fishing-Lake]
- [[places/Neosho-State-Fishing-Lake]], also known as Lake McKinley, is located in Kansas near [[places/Parsons-Kansas]]. [src:Neosho-State-Fishing-Lake]
- The [[organizations/Kansas-Department-of-Wildlife-and-Parks]] operates Neosho State Fishing Lake in Kansas. [src:Neosho-State-Fishing-Lake]
- The [[organizations/University-of-Kansas]] is a public research university in the U.S. state of Kansas. [src:University-of-Kansas]
