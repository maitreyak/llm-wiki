# Scranton, Pennsylvania

> Pennsylvania city that was the western endpoint of the Lackawanna Railroad route modeled by the New York Society of Model Engineers.

## Key Facts

- The [[organizations/New-York-Society-of-Model-Engineers]]' Hoboken layout was based on the [[organizations/Lackawanna-Railroad]] from [[places/Hoboken-New-Jersey]] to Scranton, Pennsylvania. [src:New-York-Society-of-Model-Engineers]
