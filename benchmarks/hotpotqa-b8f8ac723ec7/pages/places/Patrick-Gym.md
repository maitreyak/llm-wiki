# Patrick Gym

> Patrick Gym, in Burlington, Vermont, is the home venue of the Vermont Catamounts men's basketball team, hosting home games in the 2011–12, 2012–13, 2013–14, 2015–16 and 2016–17 seasons.

## Key Facts

- Patrick Gym hosted the home games of the [[events/2011-12-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]]. [src:2011-12-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]
- Patrick Gym hosted the home games of the [[events/2012-13-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]]. [src:2012-13-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]
- Patrick Gym hosted the home games of the [[events/2013-14-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]]. [src:2013-14-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]
- Patrick Gym hosted the home games of the [[events/2015-16-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]]. [src:2015-16-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]
- Patrick Gym hosted the home games of the [[events/2016-17-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]]. [src:2016-17-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]
- Patrick Gym is located in Burlington, Vermont. [src:2016-17-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]
- Patrick Gym is the current home venue of the [[organizations/Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball]] team. [src:Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball]
