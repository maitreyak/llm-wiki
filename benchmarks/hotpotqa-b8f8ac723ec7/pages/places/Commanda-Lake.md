# Commanda Lake

> A lake in central Ontario, Canada, on the border of Patterson and Pringle townships in the Almaguin Highlands region of the District of Parry Sound.

## Key Facts

- The lake lies on the border between Patterson Township and Pringle Township, in the Almaguin Highlands region of the [[places/District-of-Parry-Sound]]. [src:Commanda-Lake]
- It is fed by Gee Creek, Commanda Creek and Beaudry Creek, and is drained by the [[places/Restoule-River]]. [src:Commanda-Lake]
- It is a mud bottom lake whose water is described as "tea coloured." [src:Commanda-Lake]
- The community of [[places/Restoule]] is on the north shore, while cottages line many of the shorelines. [src:Commanda-Lake]
- The lake sits at an altitude of 222 m (729 ft) above sea level. [src:Commanda-Lake]
- Fish species in the lake include northern pike, walleye, smallmouth bass and largemouth bass. [src:Commanda-Lake]
- Ice gets thick in the winter, making it an all-year-round fishing lake. [src:Commanda-Lake]
