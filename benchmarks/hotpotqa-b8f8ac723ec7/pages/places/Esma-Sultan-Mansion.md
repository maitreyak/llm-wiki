# Esma Sultan Mansion

> Historical waterside mansion (yalı) on the Bosphorus in Ortaköy, Istanbul, named after its original owner Esma Sultan and now used as a cultural center.

## Key Facts

- The Esma Sultan Mansion is known in Turkish as "Esma Sultan Yalısı". [src:Esma-Sultan-Mansion]
- It is a historical [[concepts/Yal]], meaning a waterside mansion. [src:Esma-Sultan-Mansion]
- The mansion is located at the [[places/Bosphorus]] in the [[places/Ortakoy]] neighborhood of [[places/Istanbul]], Turkey. [src:Esma-Sultan-Mansion]
- It is named after its original owner, [[topics/Esma-Sultan]]. [src:Esma-Sultan-Mansion]
- After being redeveloped, the mansion is used today as a cultural center. [src:Esma-Sultan-Mansion]
