# Lyon Township

> Michigan township forming the western side of the Higgins Lake boundary.

## Key Facts

- [[places/Higgins-Lake]] is situated on the boundary between Lyon Township on the west and [[places/Gerrish-Township]] on the east. [src:Higgins-Lake-Michigan]
- The community of [[places/Higgins-Lake-Michigan]] and lakeshore locales such as Lyon Manor lie around the lake shared by Lyon Township and Gerrish Township. [src:Higgins-Lake-Michigan]
