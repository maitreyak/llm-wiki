# Centennial Ice Arena

> A 550-seat ice arena that served as the most recent home of the Billings Bulls.

## Key Facts

- Centennial Ice Arena has 550 seats. [src:Billings-Bulls]
- The [[organizations/Billings-Bulls]] most recently played their home games at Centennial Ice Arena. [src:Billings-Bulls]
- Because of the arena's small size, Bulls games there were frequently sold out. [src:Billings-Bulls]
