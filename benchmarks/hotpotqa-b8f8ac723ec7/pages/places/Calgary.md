# Calgary

> City in Alberta, Canada, whose northwest quadrant contains the Hounsfield Heights/Briar Hill neighbourhood.

## Key Facts

- Calgary is a city in Alberta, Canada. [src:Hounsfield-Heights-Briar-Hill-Calgary]
- Hounsfield Heights Briar Hill is an inner suburban neighbourhood in northwest Calgary. [src:Hounsfield-Heights-Briar-Hill-Calgary]
- The city is served by the C-Train, which includes [[places/Lions-Park-station]]. [src:Hounsfield-Heights-Briar-Hill-Calgary]
- Calgary communities include [[places/Hillhurst]] and [[places/West-Hillhurst]]. [src:Hounsfield-Heights-Briar-Hill-Calgary]
