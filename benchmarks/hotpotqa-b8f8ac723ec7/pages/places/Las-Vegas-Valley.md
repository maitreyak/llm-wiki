# Las Vegas Valley

> Nevada metropolitan area that became Criss Angel's base of operations and home of his illusion shows.

## Key Facts

- [[people/Criss-Angel]] moved his base of operations to the Las Vegas Valley after beginning his career in [[places/New-York-City]]. [src:Criss-Angel]
- [[works/Criss-Angel-Believe]] was staged at the [[places/Luxor]] casino in Las Vegas. [src:Criss-Angel]
- [[works/Criss-Angel-Believe]] generated $150 million in tourist revenue to Las Vegas in 2010. [src:Criss-Angel]
- DJ [[people/Mick-Batyske]] has performed in venues in Las Vegas, among other international cities. [src:Mick-DJ]
