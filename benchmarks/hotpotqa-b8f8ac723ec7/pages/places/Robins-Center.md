# Robins Center

> A 7,201-seat multi-purpose arena in Richmond, Virginia, opened in 1972 and home to University of Richmond Spiders basketball.

## Key Facts

- The Robins Center is a 7,201-seat multi-purpose arena located in [[places/Richmond-Virginia]]. [src:Robins-Center]
- The arena opened in 1972 and is home to [[organizations/Richmond-Spiders]] basketball of the [[organizations/University-of-Richmond]]. [src:Robins-Center]
- The Robins Center hosted the ECAC South (now known as the [[organizations/Colonial-Athletic-Association]]) men's basketball tournament in 1983. [src:Robins-Center]
- The Robins Center serves as the location of the University of Richmond's commencement exercises. [src:Robins-Center]
- The arena hosted the [[events/1992-Presidential-Debate-at-the-Robins-Center]] involving [[people/Bill-Clinton]], [[people/George-H-W-Bush]] and [[people/Ross-Perot]]. [src:Robins-Center]
