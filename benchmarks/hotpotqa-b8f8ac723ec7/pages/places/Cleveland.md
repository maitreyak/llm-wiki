# Cleveland

> City in Ohio whose downtown contains the Carl B. Stokes Federal Court House Building.

## Key Facts

- Downtown Cleveland, Ohio, is the location of the [[places/Carl-B-Stokes-United-States-Courthouse]], a 23-story skyscraper. [src:Carl-B-Stokes-United-States-Courthouse]
- Huron Road and Superior Avenue intersect in Cleveland at the site of the Stokes Tower. [src:Carl-B-Stokes-United-States-Courthouse]
