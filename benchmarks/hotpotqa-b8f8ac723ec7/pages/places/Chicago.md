# Chicago

> American city counted among the cradles of jazz.

## Key Facts

- Chicago is listed among the popular "cradles of jazz" along with Kansas City, [[places/New-Orleans]], [[places/St-Louis-Missouri]], [[places/Pittsburgh]], [[places/Philadelphia]], and [[places/New-York-City]]. [src:Kansas-City-jazz]
- [[organizations/Local-H]] is a rock band based in Chicago. [src:Hallelujah-Im-a-Bum-album]
- Sounds of the El Train in Chicago are used frequently to transition songs on [[works/Hallelujah-Im-a-Bum]]. [src:Hallelujah-Im-a-Bum-album]
- The rock band [[organizations/Suffrajett]] is currently based in Chicago, Illinois. [src:Suffrajett]
