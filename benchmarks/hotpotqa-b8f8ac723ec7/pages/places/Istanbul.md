# Istanbul

> City in Turkey on the Bosphorus, containing the Ortaköy neighborhood, the Esma Sultan Mansion, and the Fatih district with the Laleli Mosque.

- Istanbul is a city in Turkey situated at the [[places/Bosphorus]]. [src:Esma-Sultan-Mansion]
- [[places/Ortakoy]] is a neighborhood of Istanbul. [src:Esma-Sultan-Mansion]
- The [[places/Esma-Sultan-Mansion]], now used as a cultural center, is located in Istanbul. [src:Esma-Sultan-Mansion]
- [[places/Fatih]] is a district of Istanbul. [src:Laleli-Mosque]
