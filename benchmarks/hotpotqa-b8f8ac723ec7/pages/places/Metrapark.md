# Metrapark

> Billings-area arena with a maximum hockey capacity of 9,000 that formerly hosted Billings Bulls home games.

## Key Facts

- Metrapark had a maximum capacity of 9,000 for hockey games. [src:Billings-Bulls]
- The [[organizations/Billings-Bulls]] previously played their home games at Metrapark. [src:Billings-Bulls]
- A negotiating dispute with Metrapark officials and local county commissioners resulted in the Bulls losing their lease. [src:Billings-Bulls]
