# Higgins Lake, Michigan

> Small unincorporated community along the southern and western shores of Higgins Lake in Roscommon County, Michigan, served by the Higgins Lake post office (ZIP 48627).

## Key Facts

- Higgins Lake is a small unincorporated community along the southern and western shores of [[places/Higgins-Lake]], in [[places/Roscommon-County]], Michigan. [src:Higgins-Lake-Michigan]
- Higgins Lake is the name of the post office for the area, with ZIP code 48627. [src:Higgins-Lake-Michigan]
- Because all of these locales use Higgins Lake as their postal address, they are often all considered part of the Higgins Lake community. [src:Higgins-Lake-Michigan]
- The community lies near the boundary between [[places/Lyon-Township]] on the west of the lake and [[places/Gerrish-Township]] on the east. [src:Higgins-Lake-Michigan]
- US 127 passes on the west side of the lake, while I-75 and M-18 both pass a few miles to the east. [src:Higgins-Lake-Michigan]
- [[places/Houghton-Lake]] is just a few miles to the south. [src:Higgins-Lake-Michigan]
