# Busseto

> Italian town, site of the St. Bartolomeo cathedral where Ferdinando Provesi was Maestro di cappella, close to Verdi's birthplace.

## Key Facts

- Busseto is the town where [[people/Ferdinando-Provesi]] served as "Maestro di cappella" at the St. Bartolomeo cathedral. [src:Ferdinando-Provesi]
- The town had a municipal music school and a local Philharmonic Society, both directed by Provesi. [src:Ferdinando-Provesi]
