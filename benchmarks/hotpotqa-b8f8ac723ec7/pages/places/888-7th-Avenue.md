# 888 7th Avenue

> A 628-foot, 46-floor modern-style office skyscraper in Midtown Manhattan, completed in 1969 and designed by Emery Roth & Sons. Formerly the Arlen Building, it houses the corporate headquarters of Vornado Realty Trust, the offices of Pershing Square Capital Management, and the Red Eye Grill at street level.
