# Astor Building

> Building whose basement housed the New York Society of Model Engineers' largest layout until the space was requested for the World War II war effort.

## Key Facts

- The largest layout of the [[organizations/New-York-Society-of-Model-Engineers]] was located in the basement of the Astor Building. [src:New-York-Society-of-Model-Engineers]
