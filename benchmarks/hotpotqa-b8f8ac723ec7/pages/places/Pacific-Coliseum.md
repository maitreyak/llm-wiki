# Pacific Coliseum

> The arena in Vancouver, British Columbia that hosted the 2007 Memorial Cup, where a record 121,561 fans attended nine games.

## Key Facts

- The Pacific Coliseum in [[places/Vancouver]], British Columbia hosted the [[events/2007-Memorial-Cup]] in May 2007. [src:2007-Memorial-Cup]
- A record 121,561 fans attended the nine games of the 2007 Memorial Cup at the Pacific Coliseum. [src:2007-Memorial-Cup]
