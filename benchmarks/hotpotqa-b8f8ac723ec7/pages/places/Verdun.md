# Verdun

> A Quebec locality that hosted the Montreal Junior Hockey Club of the QMJHL from 2008 to 2011 before the franchise moved to Boisbriand.

## Key Facts

- The franchise later known as the [[organizations/Blainville-Boisbriand-Armada]] played in Verdun as the Montreal Junior Hockey Club from 2008 to 2011. [src:2011-12-QMJHL-season]
- The team relocated from Verdun to [[places/Boisbriand]] before the [[events/2011-12-QMJHL-season]]. [src:2011-12-QMJHL-season]
