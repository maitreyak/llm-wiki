# Brown County, Kansas

> A county in northeast Kansas with county seat Hiawatha, home to several American Indian reservations and Brown State Fishing Lake.

## Key Facts

- Brown County has the county code BR. [src:Brown-County-Kansas]
- Brown County is located in the northeast portion of the U.S. state of [[places/Kansas]]. [src:Brown-County-Kansas]
- As of the 2010 census, the population of Brown County was 9,984. [src:Brown-County-Kansas]
- The county seat and most populous city of Brown County is [[places/Hiawatha-Kansas]]. [src:Brown-County-Kansas]
- Brown County is the location of the [[places/Kickapoo-Indian-Reservation-of-Kansas]]. [src:Brown-County-Kansas]
- The majority of the [[places/Sac-and-Fox-Reservation]] lies in Brown County. [src:Brown-County-Kansas]
- The majority of the [[places/Iowa-Reservation-of-Kansas-and-Nebraska]] lies in Brown County. [src:Brown-County-Kansas]
- [[places/Brown-State-Fishing-Lake]], a protected area formerly known as Brown County State Park, is located in Brown County. [src:Brown-State-Fishing-Lake]
