# Hammerjacks

> Large concert hall and nightclub in downtown Baltimore, active through the 1980s and into the 1990s, demolished in 1997.

## Key Facts

- Hammerjacks Concert Hall and Nightclub was a large concert hall in downtown [[places/Baltimore]] through the 1980s and into the 1990s. [src:Hammerjacks]
- Hammerjacks was owned by [[people/Louis-J-Principio-III]]. [src:Hammerjacks]
- The club attracted many big-name national acts and also showcased many rising stars in the music world. [src:Hammerjacks]
- Bands playing Hammerjacks ranged from punk, glam and heavy metal acts such as [[organizations/Guns-N-Roses]], [[organizations/Kix]], [[organizations/Ratt]], [[organizations/Skid-Row]] and [[organizations/Extreme]] to pop acts such as [[organizations/Badfinger]] and alternative rock groups such as the [[organizations/Goo-Goo-Dolls]]. [src:Hammerjacks]
- The club was often frequented by hard core patrons and musicians donning big hair, leather, lace, spandex, and heavy makeup, and was considered a "hard rock shrine." [src:Hammerjacks]
- Hammerjacks was torn down on June 12, 1997 to make way for a parking lot for [[places/M-T-Bank-Stadium]]. [src:Hammerjacks]
- Hammerjacks was billed as "The largest nightclub on the east coast." [src:Hammerjacks]
