# Parma Airport

> Airport serving Parma, Italy, opened in 1991 and named after Giuseppe Verdi.

## Key Facts

- Its IATA code is PMF and its ICAO code is LIMP. [src:Parma-Airport]
- The airport is located 1.3 NM northwest of [[places/Parma]], a city in the Emilia-Romagna region of Italy. [src:Parma-Airport]
- The airport was opened on 5 May 1991. [src:Parma-Airport]
- It is also known as Giuseppe Verdi Airport or Parma "Giuseppe Verdi" Airport, named after [[people/Giuseppe-Verdi]]. [src:Parma-Airport]
