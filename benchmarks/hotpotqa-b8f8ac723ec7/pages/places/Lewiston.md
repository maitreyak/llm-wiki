# Lewiston

> A city in Maine, home to the Androscoggin Bank Colisée arena and the QMJHL's Lewiston Maineiacs.

## Key Facts

- Lewiston is a city in Maine. [src:Androscoggin-Bank-Colisee]
- Lewiston is the location of the [[places/Androscoggin-Bank-Colisee]], a 4,000-capacity multi-purpose arena that opened in 1958. [src:Androscoggin-Bank-Colisee]
- A World Heavyweight Title fight took place in Lewiston in 1965. [src:Androscoggin-Bank-Colisee]
- Lewiston was the base of the [[organizations/Lewiston-Maineiacs]], a junior ice hockey team of the [[organizations/Quebec-Major-Junior-Hockey-League]]. [src:Lewiston-Maineiacs]
