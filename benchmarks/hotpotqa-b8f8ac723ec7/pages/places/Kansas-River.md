# Kansas River

> River whose confluence with the Missouri River marks the site of the Kansas City metropolitan area.

## Key Facts

- The [[places/Kansas-City-metropolitan-area]] is located at the confluence of the Kansas River and the [[places/Missouri-River]]. [src:History-of-the-Kansas-City-metropolitan-area]
- The confluence of the Kansas and Missouri Rivers made the area a strategic point for commerce and security. [src:History-of-the-Kansas-City-metropolitan-area]
