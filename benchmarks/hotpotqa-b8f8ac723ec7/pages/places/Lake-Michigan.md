# Lake Michigan

> Great Lake that ultimately receives water draining from Higgins Lake via the Cut River and Houghton Lake.

## Key Facts

- Water from [[places/Higgins-Lake]] eventually reaches Lake Michigan after passing through [[places/Marl-Lake]] and [[places/Houghton-Lake]]. [src:Higgins-Lake]
