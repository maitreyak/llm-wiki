# Case Gym

> A 1,800-seat multi-purpose arena at Boston University, opened in 1972 atop the Harold Case Physical Education Center and nicknamed "The Roof".

## Key Facts

- Case Gym is a 1,800-seat multi-purpose arena at [[organizations/Boston-University]] in [[places/Boston]], Massachusetts. [src:Case-Gym]
- Case Gym opened in 1972 as part of the Harold Case Physical Education Center. [src:Case-Gym]
- The Harold Case Physical Education Center is named after Boston University's fifth president, [[people/Harold-C-Case]]. [src:Case-Gym]
- Case Gym is referred to as "The Roof" because it is located on the top level of the building, above [[places/Walter-Brown-Arena]]. [src:Case-Gym]
- Case Gym is home to the [[organizations/Boston-University-Terriers]] men's and women's basketball teams and the men's wrestling team. [src:Case-Gym]
- Boston University basketball games are also played in [[places/Agganis-Arena]] and on occasion Walter Brown Arena. [src:Case-Gym]
- When Boston University left the [[organizations/America-East-Conference]] for the [[organizations/Patriot-League]] in July 2013, it announced that all home men's basketball conference games would be played at Agganis Arena, with non-conference games played at Case. [src:Case-Gym]
