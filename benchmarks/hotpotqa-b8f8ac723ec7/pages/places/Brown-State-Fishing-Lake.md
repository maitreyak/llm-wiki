# Brown State Fishing Lake

> A 62-acre protected area and fishing lake in Brown County, Kansas, formerly known as Brown County State Park.

## Key Facts

- Brown State Fishing Lake is a protected area in [[places/Brown-County-Kansas]] in the United States. [src:Brown-State-Fishing-Lake]
- Brown State Fishing Lake is sometimes also known as Brown State Fishing Lake And Wildlife Area. [src:Brown-State-Fishing-Lake]
- The lake is 62 acres (0.25 km²) in area. [src:Brown-State-Fishing-Lake]
- The lake is up to 13 feet (4 m) deep. [src:Brown-State-Fishing-Lake]
- The area was formerly known as Brown County State Park. [src:Brown-State-Fishing-Lake]
- Brown State Fishing Lake is 8 miles (13 km) east of [[places/Hiawatha-Kansas]]. [src:Brown-State-Fishing-Lake]
