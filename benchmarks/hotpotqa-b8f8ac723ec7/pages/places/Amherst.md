# Amherst

> A town in Hampshire County, Massachusetts, in the Connecticut River valley, home to three of the Five Colleges.

## Key Facts

- Amherst is a town in [[places/Hampshire-County]], Massachusetts, United States, located in the Connecticut River valley. [src:Amherst-Massachusetts]
- As of the 2010 census, Amherst's population was 37,819, making it the largest municipality in [[places/Hampshire-County]]. [src:Amherst-Massachusetts]
- Although Amherst is the largest municipality in the county, the county seat is [[places/Northampton]]. [src:Amherst-Massachusetts]
- Amherst is home to [[organizations/Amherst-College]], [[organizations/Hampshire-College]], and the [[organizations/University-of-Massachusetts-Amherst]], three of the [[organizations/Five-Colleges]]. [src:Amherst-Massachusetts]
- The name of the town is pronounced without the "h" ("AM-erst"). [src:Amherst-Massachusetts]
- The pronunciation gave rise to the local saying "only the 'h' is silent", referring both to the pronunciation and to the town's politically active populace. [src:Amherst-Massachusetts]
