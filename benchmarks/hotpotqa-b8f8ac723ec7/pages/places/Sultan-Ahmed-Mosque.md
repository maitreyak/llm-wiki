# Sultan Ahmed Mosque

> Historic Ottoman mosque in Istanbul, Turkey, popularly known as the Blue Mosque.

## Key Facts

- The Sultan Ahmed Mosque, also spelled Sultan Ahmet Mosque (Turkish: "Sultan Ahmet Camii"), is a historic mosque located in [[places/Istanbul]], Turkey. [src:Sultan-Ahmed-Mosque]
- It is popularly known as the Blue Mosque. [src:Sultan-Ahmed-Mosque]
- The mosque was constructed between 1609 and 1616 during the rule of [[people/Ahmed-I]]. [src:Sultan-Ahmed-Mosque]
- Its külliye contains Ahmed's tomb, a madrasah and a hospice. [src:Sultan-Ahmed-Mosque]
- Hand-painted blue tiles adorn the mosque's interior walls. [src:Sultan-Ahmed-Mosque]
- The mosque sits next to the [[places/Hagia-Sophia]], another popular tourist site. [src:Sultan-Ahmed-Mosque]
