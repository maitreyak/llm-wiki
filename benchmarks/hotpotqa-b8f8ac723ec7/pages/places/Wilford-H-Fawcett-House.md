# Wilford H. Fawcett House

> A historic house in Breezy Point, Minnesota, built as publisher Wilford H. Fawcett's private residence within his resort property and listed on the National Register of Historic Places.

## Key Facts

- The Wilford H. Fawcett House is a house in [[places/Breezy-Point-Minnesota]], United States. [src:Wilford-H-Fawcett-House]
- The Wilford H. Fawcett House is listed on the National Register of Historic Places. [src:Wilford-H-Fawcett-House]
- [[people/Wilford-H-Fawcett]] built the Fawcett House as a private residence within the resort property of [[places/Breezy-Point-Resort]]. [src:Wilford-H-Fawcett-House]
- Fawcett hired the Minneapolis architects [[organizations/Magney-and-Tusler]] and commissioned the design in the Swiss Chateau style. [src:Wilford-H-Fawcett-House]
- The house's rustic log style was a bit ironic, since the local logging industry had declined before the resort was built. [src:Wilford-H-Fawcett-House]
