# Osage State Fishing Lake

> A 140-acre man-made freshwater fishing lake built in 1955 in northern Osage County, Kansas, set within a 366-acre state-owned parkland.

## Key Facts

- Osage State Fishing Lake is located in northern [[places/Osage-County-Kansas]], [[places/Kansas]]. [src:Osage-State-Fishing-Lake]
- The lake is a mile east of US Route 75. [src:Osage-State-Fishing-Lake]
- [[places/Scranton-Kansas]] is to the lake's northwest. [src:Osage-State-Fishing-Lake]
- [[places/Overbrook-Kansas]] is to the lake's northeast. [src:Osage-State-Fishing-Lake]
- Osage Lake is a freshwater man-made lake, constructed in 1955. [src:Osage-State-Fishing-Lake]
- The lake has a surface area of 140 acres. [src:Osage-State-Fishing-Lake]
- The parkland containing the lake has 366 acres of land, mostly tall grass prairie with numerous wooded areas. [src:Osage-State-Fishing-Lake]
- The lake is owned by the State of [[places/Kansas]]. [src:Osage-State-Fishing-Lake]
