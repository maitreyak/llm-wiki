# Neosho State Fishing Lake

> A 92-acre state fishing lake near Parsons, Kansas, completed in 1927 and operated by the Kansas Department of Wildlife and Parks.

## Key Facts

- Neosho State Fishing Lake is also known as Lake McKinley. [src:Neosho-State-Fishing-Lake]
- The lake is located in the state of [[places/Kansas]]. [src:Neosho-State-Fishing-Lake]
- The lake is located 5 miles north on highway 59 and 4 miles east on 40th Rd from the city of [[places/Parsons-Kansas]], USA. [src:Neosho-State-Fishing-Lake]
- Construction of the lake was completed in 1927. [src:Neosho-State-Fishing-Lake]
- The lake covers 92 acres of water. [src:Neosho-State-Fishing-Lake]
- There are 124 public use acres surrounding the lake. [src:Neosho-State-Fishing-Lake]
- Fishing, camping, and picnicking are popular activities at the lake. [src:Neosho-State-Fishing-Lake]
- The park has a shelter house, fishing piers, picnic tables, barbecue grills, vault toilets and a water hydrant. [src:Neosho-State-Fishing-Lake]
- Below the Lake McKinley dam is a catch and release youth fishing pond for anglers age 15 and under when accompanied by an adult. [src:Neosho-State-Fishing-Lake]
- The lake is operated by the [[organizations/Kansas-Department-of-Wildlife-and-Parks]]. [src:Neosho-State-Fishing-Lake]
