# Sir Matt Busby Sports Complex

> Public leisure centre in Bellshill, North Lanarkshire, Scotland, named in honour of football manager Sir Matt Busby.

## Key Facts

- The Sir Matt Busby Sports Complex is a public leisure centre. [src:Sir-Matt-Busby-Sports-Complex]
- The complex is located in [[places/Bellshill]], North Lanarkshire, Scotland. [src:Sir-Matt-Busby-Sports-Complex]
- The complex is named in honour of [[people/Matt-Busby]], the football manager born in Bellshill in 1909. [src:Sir-Matt-Busby-Sports-Complex]
