# Androscoggin Bank Colisée

> A 4,000-capacity multi-purpose arena in Lewiston, Maine, opened in 1958, home of the QMJHL's Lewiston Maineiacs and site of the 1965 World Heavyweight Title fight famously photographed.

## Key Facts

- The Androscoggin Bank Colisée is a multi-purpose arena in [[places/Lewiston]], Maine. [src:Androscoggin-Bank-Colisee]
- The arena was formerly known as the Central Maine Civic Center and the Lewiston Colisee. [src:Androscoggin-Bank-Colisee]
- The venue has a capacity of 4,000, with 3,677 seated. [src:Androscoggin-Bank-Colisee]
- The arena opened in 1958. [src:Androscoggin-Bank-Colisee]
- In 1965 the arena hosted a World Heavyweight Title fight. [src:Androscoggin-Bank-Colisee]
- One of the most famous sports photographs of the century, showing [[people/Muhammed-Ali]] standing over [[people/Sonny-Liston]], was taken at the arena during the 1965 title fight. [src:Androscoggin-Bank-Colisee]
- The [[organizations/Lewiston-Maineiacs]] played their home games at the Androscoggin Bank Colisée. [src:Lewiston-Maineiacs]
