# Mendocino County

> California county that became the first U.S. jurisdiction to ban genetically modified organisms.

## Key Facts

- Mendocino County, California, was the first jurisdiction in the United States to ban the cultivation, production or distribution of genetically modified organisms ([[concepts/Genetically-Modified-Organism]]). [src:Mendocino-County-GMO-Ban]
- The GMO ban ordinance in Mendocino County was entitled [[events/Measure-H]] and was passed by referendum on March 2, 2004. [src:Mendocino-County-GMO-Ban]
- Since the enactment of the ordinance, Mendocino County has been added to an international list of "GMO free zones." [src:Mendocino-County-GMO-Ban]
