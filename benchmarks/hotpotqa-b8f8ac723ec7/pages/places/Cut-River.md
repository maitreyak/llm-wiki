# Cut River

## Key Facts

- The Cut River drains [[places/Higgins-Lake]] into [[places/Marl-Lake]]. [src:Higgins-Lake]
- Water carried by the Cut River runs into [[places/Houghton-Lake]] and eventually to [[places/Lake-Michigan]]. [src:Higgins-Lake]
