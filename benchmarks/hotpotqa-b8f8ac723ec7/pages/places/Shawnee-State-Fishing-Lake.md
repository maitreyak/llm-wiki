# Shawnee State Fishing Lake

> A 135-acre man-made freshwater fishing lake in northern Shawnee County, Kansas, about eight miles northwest of Topeka, constructed in the late 1960s.

## Key Facts

- Shawnee State Fishing Lake is approximately eight miles northwest of [[places/Topeka-Kansas]], [[places/Kansas]]. [src:Shawnee-State-Fishing-Lake]
- The lake is located in northern [[places/Shawnee-County-Kansas]]. [src:Shawnee-State-Fishing-Lake]
- Shawnee State Fishing Lake is sometimes confused with [[places/Lake-Shawnee]], which is on the east side of Topeka. [src:Shawnee-State-Fishing-Lake]
- The lake has a surface area of 135 acres and a maximum depth of 25 feet. [src:Shawnee-State-Fishing-Lake]
- It is a manmade freshwater lake that was constructed in the late 1960s. [src:Shawnee-State-Fishing-Lake]
- The lake is widest at the dam, which is located at its south end. [src:Shawnee-State-Fishing-Lake]
- The lake can be reached by a number of roads, but the only asphalted road reaching it is on the southeast corner. [src:Shawnee-State-Fishing-Lake]
- 94th Street lies to the north of the lake and Humphrey Road is on its west side. [src:Shawnee-State-Fishing-Lake]
