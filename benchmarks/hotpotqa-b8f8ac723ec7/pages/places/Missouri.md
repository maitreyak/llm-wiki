# Missouri

> U.S. state containing St. Louis and Kansas City, home to the Greater St. Louis and Kansas City metro areas, whose border with Kansas became the first battlefield in the conflict in the American Civil War.

## Key Facts

- The [[places/Kansas-City-metropolitan-area]] straddles the border between Missouri and [[places/Kansas]]. [src:History-of-the-Kansas-City-metropolitan-area]
- The Kansas City metropolitan area is the second largest metropolitan area with its core in Missouri, after [[places/Greater-St-Louis]]. [src:Kansas-City-metropolitan-area]
- [[places/Independence-Missouri]], a city with over 100,000 residents in the Kansas City metropolitan area, is in Missouri. [src:Kansas-City-metropolitan-area]
- [[places/St-Louis-Missouri]] is a city in Missouri from which Frenchmen moved up the [[places/Missouri-River]] to trap for furs and trade with Native Americans. [src:History-of-the-Kansas-City-metropolitan-area]
- [[places/Kansas-City-Missouri]] was founded in Missouri in 1838. [src:History-of-the-Kansas-City-metropolitan-area]
- In 1854, when Kansas was opened to Euro-American settlement, the Missouri–Kansas border became the first battlefield in the conflict in the [[events/American-Civil-War]]. [src:History-of-the-Kansas-City-metropolitan-area]
