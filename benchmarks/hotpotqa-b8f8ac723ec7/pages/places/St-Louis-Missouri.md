# St. Louis, Missouri

> Missouri city from which Frenchmen moved up the Missouri River in the 19th century to trap furs and trade with Native Americans, and one of the cradles of jazz.

## Key Facts

- Frenchmen from St. Louis, Missouri moved up the [[places/Missouri-River]] to trap for furs and trade with the Native Americans, beginning the history of the [[places/Kansas-City-metropolitan-area]] in the 19th century. [src:History-of-the-Kansas-City-metropolitan-area]
- [[places/Kansas-City-Missouri]] became the predominant city west of St. Louis after defeating its rival [[places/Westport]]. [src:History-of-the-Kansas-City-metropolitan-area]
- St. Louis is listed among the popular "cradles of jazz" along with Kansas City, [[places/New-Orleans]], [[places/Chicago]], [[places/Pittsburgh]], [[places/Philadelphia]], and [[places/New-York-City]]. [src:Kansas-City-jazz]
