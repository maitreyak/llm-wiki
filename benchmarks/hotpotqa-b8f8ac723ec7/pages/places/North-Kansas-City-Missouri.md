# North Kansas City, Missouri

> Independent city in Clay County, Missouri, part of the Kansas City metropolitan area, with a 2010 census population of 4,208.

## Key Facts

- North Kansas City is a city in [[places/Clay-County-Missouri]], [[places/Missouri]], United States. [src:North-Kansas-City-Missouri]
- Despite the similarity in name to its larger counterpart [[places/Kansas-City-Missouri]], North Kansas City is an independent municipality. [src:North-Kansas-City-Missouri]
- North Kansas City is part of the [[places/Kansas-City-metropolitan-area]]. [src:North-Kansas-City-Missouri]
- The population of North Kansas City was 4,208 at the 2010 census. [src:North-Kansas-City-Missouri]
- North Kansas City was originally a northern suburb across the [[places/Missouri-River]] from [[places/Kansas-City-Missouri]]. [src:North-Kansas-City-Missouri]
- North Kansas City is now almost completely surrounded by [[places/Kansas-City-Missouri]], which has annexed far to the north of North Kansas City's northern city limits. [src:North-Kansas-City-Missouri]
- North Kansas City adjoins the small municipality of [[places/Avondale-Missouri]]. [src:North-Kansas-City-Missouri]
