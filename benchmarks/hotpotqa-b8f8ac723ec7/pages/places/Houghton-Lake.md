# Houghton Lake

> Michigan lake downstream of Higgins Lake in the Cut River drainage toward Lake Michigan, lying a few miles south of Higgins Lake.

## Key Facts

- The [[places/Cut-River]], which drains [[places/Higgins-Lake]], runs into Houghton Lake. [src:Higgins-Lake]
- Water from Houghton Lake eventually reaches [[places/Lake-Michigan]]. [src:Higgins-Lake]
- Houghton Lake is just a few miles south of [[places/Higgins-Lake]] and the [[places/Higgins-Lake-Michigan]] community. [src:Higgins-Lake-Michigan]
