# Hoboken, New Jersey

> New Jersey city, site of the Lackawanna Railroad's passenger terminal where the New York Society of Model Engineers built its world-record model railroad.

## Key Facts

- Hoboken, New Jersey was the site of the [[organizations/Lackawanna-Railroad]]'s passenger terminal, [[places/Hoboken-Terminal]]. [src:New-York-Society-of-Model-Engineers]
- The [[organizations/New-York-Society-of-Model-Engineers]] moved to Hoboken after World War II and built there what became the largest model railroad in the world at that time. [src:New-York-Society-of-Model-Engineers]
- The layout was based on the Lackawanna Railroad line from Hoboken to [[places/Scranton-Pennsylvania]]. [src:New-York-Society-of-Model-Engineers]
