# University of Vermont

> The University of Vermont, located in Burlington, Vermont, is the school represented by the Vermont Catamounts men's basketball team (America East Conference, Patrick Gym, six NCAA Tournament appearances), the Vermont Catamounts men's ice hockey team (Hockey East, Gutterson Fieldhouse) and the Vermont Catamounts men's soccer team (America East Conference).

## Key Facts

- The University of Vermont was represented by the 2010–11 Vermont Catamounts mens basketball team in the 2010–11 NCAA Division I men's basketball season. [src:2010-11-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]
- Vermont's men's basketball team competed in the [[organizations/America-East-Conference]], winning three consecutive regular season championships through 2010–11. [src:2010-11-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]
- The University of Vermont was also represented by the [[events/2011-12-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]], which finished 24–12 under first-year coach [[people/John-Becker]]. [src:2011-12-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]
- Vermont men's basketball plays its home games at [[places/Patrick-Gym]]. [src:2011-12-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]
- Vermont won the [[events/America-East-Basketball-Tournament]] in 2012 and advanced to the [[events/2012-NCAA-Tournament]]. [src:2011-12-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]
- Vermont reached the 2013 America East Tournament championship game, losing to [[organizations/Albany-Great-Danes]], and then played in the [[events/2013-College-Basketball-Invitational]]. [src:2012-13-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]
- The University of Vermont was represented by the [[events/2013-14-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]], which finished 22–11 and 15–1 in America East play to win the regular season championship. [src:2013-14-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]
- Vermont lost the 2014 America East Tournament semifinal to Albany and then received an automatic bid to the [[events/National-Invitation-Tournament]], losing in the first round to [[organizations/Georgia-Bulldogs]]. [src:2013-14-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]
- The University of Vermont was represented by the [[events/2015-16-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]], which finished 23–14 and 11–5 in America East play to tie for third place. [src:2015-16-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]
- The University of Vermont was represented by the [[events/2016-17-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]], which finished 29–6 and 16–0 in America East play to win the regular season championship. [src:2016-17-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]
- Vermont won the 2017 America East Tournament by beating [[organizations/Maine-Black-Bears]], [[organizations/New-Hampshire-Wildcats]] and Albany, then lost to [[organizations/Purdue-Boilermakers]] in the first round of the [[events/2017-NCAA-Tournament]]. [src:2016-17-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]
- The University of Vermont is located in Burlington, Vermont, and is represented by the [[organizations/Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball]] team. [src:Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball]
- Vermont has reached the NCAA Division I Men's Basketball Tournament six times, in 2003, 2004, 2005, 2010, 2012 and 2017. [src:Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball]
- UVM famously upset [[organizations/Syracuse-University]] in the first round of the 2005 NCAA Tournament. [src:Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball]
- The University of Vermont is represented in NCAA Division I college ice hockey by the [[organizations/Vermont-Catamounts-mens-ice-hockey]] team. [src:Vermont-Catamounts-mens-ice-hockey]
- Vermont's men's ice hockey team joined [[organizations/Hockey-East]] in 2005 after competing in [[organizations/ECAC-Hockey]] from 1974 to 2005. [src:Vermont-Catamounts-mens-ice-hockey]
- Vermont men's ice hockey plays home games at [[places/Gutterson-Fieldhouse]] in Burlington, Vermont. [src:Vermont-Catamounts-mens-ice-hockey]
- Vermont has appeared in the [[events/NCAA-Mens-Hockey-Championship]] five times since moving to Division I in 1974–75, including Frozen Four trips in 1996 and 2009. [src:Vermont-Catamounts-mens-ice-hockey]
- The University of Vermont is represented in all NCAA Division I men's college soccer competitions by the [[organizations/Vermont-Catamounts-mens-soccer]] team. [src:Vermont-Catamounts-mens-soccer]
- Vermont's men's soccer team competes in the [[organizations/America-East-Conference]]. [src:Vermont-Catamounts-mens-soccer]
