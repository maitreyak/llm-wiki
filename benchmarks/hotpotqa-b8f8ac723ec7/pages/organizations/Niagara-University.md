# Niagara University

> University in Lewiston, New York, whose campus includes the Robert and Concetta Dwyer Arena.

## Key Facts

- Niagara University's campus in [[places/Lewiston-New-York]] is the site of [[places/Dwyer-Arena]]. [src:Dwyer-Arena]
- Niagara University's athletic teams are known as the Purple Eagles, including the [[organizations/Niagara-Purple-Eagles-mens-ice-hockey]] team. [src:Dwyer-Arena]
- Niagara University formerly fielded a women's ice hockey team that played in [[organizations/College-Hockey-America]] and used [[places/Dwyer-Arena]]. [src:Dwyer-Arena]
