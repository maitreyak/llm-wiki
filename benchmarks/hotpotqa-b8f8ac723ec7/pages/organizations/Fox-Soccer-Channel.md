# Fox Soccer Channel

> US television channel that broadcast an alternate version of David Beckham's Soccer USA.

## Key Facts

- Fox Soccer Channel broadcast in the USA a version of [[media/David-Beckhams-Soccer-USA]] hosted by [[people/Natalie-Pinkham]]. [src:David-Beckhams-Soccer-USA]
- The Fox Soccer Channel version of the show was completely devoid of any content derived from British studio footage. [src:David-Beckhams-Soccer-USA]
