# Maine Black Bears

> The Maine Black Bears are an America East Conference men's basketball team that lost to Vermont in the 2016 and 2017 America East Tournaments.

## Key Facts

- Maine is a member of the [[organizations/America-East-Conference]]. [src:2016-17-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]
- Maine lost to Vermont in the 2016 [[events/America-East-Basketball-Tournament]]. [src:2015-16-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]
- Maine lost to the [[events/2016-17-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]] in the 2017 America East Tournament. [src:2016-17-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]
- Maine lost to the [[events/2015-16-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]] in the [[events/America-East-Basketball-Tournament]]. [src:2015-16-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]
