# Quebec Major Junior Hockey League

> A major junior ice hockey league and CHL member whose 38th season was played in 2006–07 with eighteen teams on a 70-game schedule and whose 43rd season came in 2011–12 with seventeen teams on a 68-game schedule. Its regular-season title is marked by the Jean Rougeau Trophy and its playoff title by the President's Cup, and its champion advances to the Memorial Cup.

## Key Facts

- The 2006–07 season was the 38th season in the history of the Quebec Major Junior Hockey League. [src:2006-07-QMJHL-season]
- In [[events/2006-07-QMJHL-season]], the league consisted of eighteen teams that each played 70 regular-season games. [src:2006-07-QMJHL-season]
- The league awards the [[concepts/Jean-Rougeau-Trophy]] to its first-place regular-season team and the [[concepts/Presidents-Cup]] to its playoff champion. [src:2006-07-QMJHL-season]
- The QMJHL is a member league of the [[organizations/Canadian-Hockey-League]] and sends its champion to the [[concepts/Memorial-Cup]]. [src:2007-Memorial-Cup]
- The [[organizations/Lewiston-Maineiacs]] represented the QMJHL as its champion at the [[events/2007-Memorial-Cup]]. [src:2007-Memorial-Cup]
- The [[events/2011-12-QMJHL-season]] was the 43rd season of the league, featuring seventeen teams playing 68 games each. [src:2011-12-QMJHL-season]
- The league lost one of its charter teams when the [[organizations/Lewiston-Maineiacs]] folded after the 2010–11 season. [src:2011-12-QMJHL-season]
- The QMJHL announced an expansion team for [[places/Sherbrooke]] to begin play in the 2012–13 season. [src:2011-12-QMJHL-season]
- By 2011–12, seven teams in league history had captured consecutive [[concepts/Presidents-Cup]] championships, the [[organizations/Saint-John-Sea-Dogs]] being the seventh. [src:2011-12-QMJHL-season]
- The [[organizations/Lewiston-Maineiacs]] of [[places/Lewiston]], Maine were the second QMJHL team in the United States and the only one to play a full season. [src:Lewiston-Maineiacs]
