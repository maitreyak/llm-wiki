# Lackawanna Railroad

> Railroad that invited the New York Society of Model Engineers to occupy its Hoboken passenger terminal after World War II and whose Hoboken–Scranton line was the basis of the Society's layout.

## Key Facts

- The Lackawanna Railroad invited the [[organizations/New-York-Society-of-Model-Engineers]] to move into its passenger terminal in [[places/Hoboken-New-Jersey]] after World War II. [src:New-York-Society-of-Model-Engineers]
- The Society's Hoboken layout was based on the Lackawanna Railroad from Hoboken to [[places/Scranton-Pennsylvania]]. [src:New-York-Society-of-Model-Engineers]
