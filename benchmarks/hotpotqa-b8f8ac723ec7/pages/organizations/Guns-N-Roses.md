# Guns N' Roses

> American hard rock band formed in Los Angeles in 1985, known for its debut album Appetite for Destruction; also appeared in a music video with Arnold Schwarzenegger and had a temporary Sirius XM radio channel.

## Key Facts

- Guns N' Roses is an American hard rock band formed in [[places/Los-Angeles]], California in 1985 by members of Hollywood Rose and L.A. Guns. [src:Guns-N-Roses-discography]
- The band has released six studio albums, two live albums, two compilation albums, two extended plays, seven video albums, eighteen singles, twenty four music videos and one video single. [src:Guns-N-Roses-discography]
- Guns N' Roses signed a deal with [[organizations/Geffen-Records]] in 1986. [src:Guns-N-Roses-discography]
- The band's debut studio album [[works/Appetite-for-Destruction]] was released in 1987. [src:Guns-N-Roses-discography]
- [[people/Arnold-Schwarzenegger]] has appeared in a music video for Guns N' Roses. [src:Arnold-Schwarzenegger-filmography]
- In mid-July 2017, Guns N Roses radio temporarily replaced [[media/Faction]] on [[organizations/Sirius-XM]] channel 41. [src:Faction-Punk]
- Guns N' Roses is cited among the punk, glam and heavy metal acts associated with [[places/Hammerjacks]] in Baltimore. [src:Hammerjacks]
- Guns N' Roses released the song [[works/Oh-My-God]] in 1999 on the soundtrack to the film [[works/End-of-Days]]. [src:Oh-My-God-Guns-N-Roses-song]
- "Oh My God" was the band's first recorded release in almost five years and was never issued as a stand-alone retail single. [src:Oh-My-God-Guns-N-Roses-song]
- [[people/Steven-Adler]] was the band's drummer and co-songwriter, achieving worldwide success with them in the late 1980s. [src:Steven-Adler]
- Steven Adler was fired from Guns N' Roses over his heroin addiction in 1990. [src:Steven-Adler]
- Steven Adler was inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame in 2012 as a member of Guns N' Roses. [src:Steven-Adler]
