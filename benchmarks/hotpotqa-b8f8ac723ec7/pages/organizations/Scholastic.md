# Scholastic

> Publisher of the Animorphs young adult book series and of The Divide trilogy.

## Key Facts

- Scholastic published the [[works/Animorphs]] series. [src:Animorphs]
- Scholastic published the later volumes of [[works/The-Divide-trilogy]] and reprinted its first novel. [src:The-Divide-trilogy]
- [[organizations/Chicken-House]], the small press that originally published "The Divide" (2002), is now a division of Scholastic. [src:The-Divide-trilogy]
