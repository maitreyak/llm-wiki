# Employee Benefits Security Administration

> EBSA is the U.S. Department of Labor agency headed by the Assistant Secretary for Employee Benefits Security.

## Key Facts

- The Employee Benefits Security Administration (EBSA) is the agency of the [[organizations/United-States-Department-of-Labor]] led by the Assistant Secretary for Employee Benefits Security. [src:Bradford-P-Campbell]
- [[people/Bradford-P-Campbell]] was the official in charge of EBSA as Assistant Secretary for Employee Benefits Security until January 20, 2009. [src:Bradford-P-Campbell]
- EBSA has a Deputy Assistant Secretary for Policy position, held by Bradford P. Campbell starting March 5, 2004. [src:Bradford-P-Campbell]
