# WINNER

> South Korean boy group formed in 2013 by YG Entertainment, debuted in 2014, currently a four-member group after Taehyun's 2016 departure.

## Key Facts

- WINNER is a South Korean group. [src:2014-S-S]
- WINNER's debut album is 2014 S S, released on August 12, 2014. [src:2014-S-S]
- WINNER's record label is [[organizations/YG-Entertainment]]. [src:2014-S-S]
- WINNER's members were credited for writing the lyrics and composing the majority of the songs on 2014 S/S. [src:2014-S-S]
- WINNER is a South Korean boy group, written in Hangul as 위너 and often stylized as WINNER. [src:Winner-band]
- WINNER was formed in 2013 by [[organizations/YG-Entertainment]] and debuted in 2014. [src:Winner-band]
- WINNER currently consists of four members: [[people/Jinwoo]], [[people/Seunghoon]], [[people/Mino]] and [[people/Seungyoon]]. [src:Winner-band]
- WINNER was originally a five-piece group that included [[people/Taehyun]], who departed from the group in November 2016. [src:Winner-band]
