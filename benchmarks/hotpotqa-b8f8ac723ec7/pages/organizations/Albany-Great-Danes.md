# Albany Great Danes

> The Albany Great Danes are an America East Conference men's basketball team that beat Vermont in the 2013 conference final and 2014 semifinal and lost to Vermont in the 2017 America East Tournament championship game.

## Key Facts

- Albany is a member of the [[organizations/America-East-Conference]]. [src:2016-17-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]
- Albany lost to the [[events/2016-17-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]] in the 2017 [[events/America-East-Basketball-Tournament]] championship game. [src:2016-17-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]
- Albany defeated the [[events/2012-13-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]] in the championship game of the [[events/America-East-Basketball-Tournament]]. [src:2012-13-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]
