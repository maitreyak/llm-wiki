# UMKC Health Sciences District

> Health sciences district formed in 2017 on Hospital Hill by UMKC and partner healthcare and government agencies.

## Key Facts

- The UMKC Health Sciences District was formed in 2017 on Hospital Hill by the [[organizations/University-of-Missouri-Kansas-City]] in collaboration with Truman Medical Center, Children's Mercy Hospital, the Missouri Health Department, the Jackson County Medical Examiners Office, and the Missouri Department of Mental Health Behavioral Medicine.
