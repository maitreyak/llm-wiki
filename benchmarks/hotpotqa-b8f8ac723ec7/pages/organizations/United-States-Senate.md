# United States Senate

> The upper chamber of the U.S. Congress; it confirms presidential nominees, counted Richard Nixon among its members from California, and its Illinois seat was contested in 1978.

## Key Facts

- The United States Senate unanimously confirmed [[people/Bradford-P-Campbell]] as Assistant Secretary for Employee Benefits Security on August 3, 2007. [src:Bradford-P-Campbell]
- [[people/Richard-Nixon]] served as a Senator from [[places/California]] before becoming Vice President. [src:Richard-Nixon]
- [[people/Charles-H-Percy]] was an incumbent Republican United States Senator who sought a third Senate term in 1978. [src:United-States-Senate-election-in-Illinois-1978]
- The [[events/United-States-Senate-election-in-Illinois-1978]] on November 7, 1978 determined an Illinois seat in the Senate, won by Percy over [[people/Alex-Seith]]. [src:United-States-Senate-election-in-Illinois-1978]
