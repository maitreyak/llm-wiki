# Ontario Hockey League

> A CHL member major junior league whose 2006–07 champion, the Plymouth Whalers, competed in the 2007 Memorial Cup.

## Key Facts

- The Ontario Hockey League is a member league of the [[organizations/Canadian-Hockey-League]] that sends its champion to the [[concepts/Memorial-Cup]]. [src:2007-Memorial-Cup]
- The [[organizations/Plymouth-Whalers]] were the OHL champion at the [[events/2007-Memorial-Cup]]. [src:2007-Memorial-Cup]
