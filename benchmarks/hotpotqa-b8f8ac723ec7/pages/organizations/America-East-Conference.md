# America East Conference

> The America East Conference is an NCAA Division I collegiate athletic conference with members located mainly in the Northeastern United States; formerly the Eastern College Athletic Conference-North (1979–1988) and the North Atlantic Conference (1988–1996), it is the conference in which Vermont, Albany, Stony Brook, Maine and New Hampshire competed in men's basketball, and its tournament champion receives an automatic NCAA Tournament bid.

## Key Facts

- The America East Conference is a collegiate athletic conference affiliated with NCAA Division I. [src:America-East-Conference]
- The members of the America East Conference are located mainly in the Northeastern United States. [src:America-East-Conference]
- The conference was known as the Eastern College Athletic Conference-North from 1979 to 1988. [src:America-East-Conference]
- The conference was known as the North Atlantic Conference from 1988 to 1996, after which it took the America East name. [src:America-East-Conference]
- Vermont won its third consecutive America East Conference regular season championship in the 2010–11 season. [src:2010-11-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]
- The America East Conference tournament semifinals in 2011 saw [[organizations/Stony-Brook-Seawolves]] defeat Vermont. [src:2010-11-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]
- The [[events/2011-12-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]] went 13–3 in America East play to finish in second place. [src:2011-12-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]
- The conference awards an automatic bid to the NCAA Tournament to the winner of the [[events/America-East-Basketball-Tournament]], which Vermont claimed for the [[events/2012-NCAA-Tournament]]. [src:2011-12-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]
- The [[events/2012-13-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]] went 11–5 in America East play to tie for second place. [src:2012-13-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]
- [[organizations/Albany-Great-Danes]] defeated Vermont in the 2013 America East Tournament championship game. [src:2012-13-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]
- The [[events/2013-14-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]] went 15–1 in America East play to win the regular season championship. [src:2013-14-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]
- Albany defeated Vermont in the semifinals of the 2014 America East Conference Tournament. [src:2013-14-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]
- The [[events/2015-16-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]] went 11–5 in America East play to tie for third place. [src:2015-16-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]
- In the 2016 America East Tournament, Vermont beat [[organizations/Maine-Black-Bears]] and [[organizations/New-Hampshire-Wildcats]] before losing the championship game to Stony Brook. [src:2015-16-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]
- The [[events/2016-17-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]] went 16–0 in America East play to win the regular season championship. [src:2016-17-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]
- In the 2017 America East Tournament, Vermont defeated Maine, New Hampshire and Albany to win the championship and the conference's automatic bid to the [[events/2017-NCAA-Tournament]]. [src:2016-17-Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball-team]
- [[organizations/Boston-University]] left the America East Conference for the [[organizations/Patriot-League]] in July 2013. [src:Case-Gym]
- The [[organizations/Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball]] team currently competes in the America East Conference. [src:Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball]
- The [[organizations/Vermont-Catamounts-mens-soccer]] team competes in the America East Conference. [src:Vermont-Catamounts-mens-soccer]
