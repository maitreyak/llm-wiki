# United States Department of Labor

> The U.S. Department of Labor is the federal department that includes the Employee Benefits Security Administration.

## Key Facts

- The United States Department of Labor (DOL) includes the [[organizations/Employee-Benefits-Security-Administration]], which is headed by the Assistant Secretary for Employee Benefits Security. [src:Bradford-P-Campbell]
- [[people/Bradford-P-Campbell]] served as the DOL's Assistant Secretary for Employee Benefits Security from his confirmation in August 2007 until January 20, 2009. [src:Bradford-P-Campbell]
