# University of Richmond

> University in Richmond, Virginia, whose athletics program the Spiders plays at the Robins Center.

## Key Facts

- The University of Richmond's basketball team plays at [[places/Robins-Center]], which opened in 1972. [src:Robins-Center]
- The university's commencement exercises are held at the [[places/Robins-Center]]. [src:Robins-Center]
- Before the Robins Center opened, the school's basketball team played home games at numerous locations around the Richmond area. [src:Robins-Center]
