# Office of Population Affairs

> U.S. federal office within the Department of Health and Human Services that advises on reproductive health and population issues and oversees three component program offices.

## Key Facts

- The Office of Population Affairs (OPA) is a part of the [[organizations/United-States-Department-of-Health-and-Human-Services]], within the [[organizations/Office-of-Public-Health-and-Science]]. [src:Office-of-Population-Affairs]
- OPA serves as the focal point to advise the Secretary and the Assistant Secretary for Health on a wide range of reproductive health topics, including adolescent pregnancy, family planning, and sterilization, as well as other population issues. [src:Office-of-Population-Affairs]
- OPA was created by an Act of Congress in 1970 (Public Law 91-572, 84 Stat. 1504, Dec. 24, 1970). [src:Office-of-Population-Affairs]
- OPA operates under the direction of the Deputy Assistant Secretary of Population Affairs (DASPA). [src:Office-of-Population-Affairs]
- OPA has three component offices responsible for the oversight of program functions: the [[organizations/Office-of-Family-Planning]], the [[organizations/Office-of-Adolescent-Pregnancy-Programs]], and the [[organizations/Office-of-Research-and-Evaluation]]. [src:Office-of-Population-Affairs]
