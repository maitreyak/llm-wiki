# Science Fiction and Fantasy Writers of America

> Professional organization, abbreviated SFWA, that presents the Andre Norton Award and the Nebula Awards.

## Key Facts

- The Science Fiction and Fantasy Writers of America (SFWA) presents the [[events/Andre-Norton-Award]] annually. [src:Andre-Norton-Award]
- SFWA's Young Adult Fiction committee helped establish the Andre Norton Award, which was announced on February 20, 2005. [src:Andre-Norton-Award]
- [[people/Catherine-Asaro]] was SFWA president at the time the Andre Norton Award was established. [src:Andre-Norton-Award]
- SFWA also presents the [[events/Nebula-Awards]], alongside which the Andre Norton Award is given. [src:Andre-Norton-Award]
