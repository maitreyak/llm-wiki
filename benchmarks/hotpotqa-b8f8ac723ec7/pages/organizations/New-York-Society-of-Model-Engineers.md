# New York Society of Model Engineers

> Model engineering society incorporated in New York in 1926, later builder of what was then the world's largest model railroad in Hoboken and now based in Carlstadt, New Jersey.

## Key Facts

- The New York Society of Model Engineers (NYSME) was originally incorporated in 1926 in [[places/New-York-City]]. [src:New-York-Society-of-Model-Engineers]
- Published records show the Society existed as early as 1905. [src:New-York-Society-of-Model-Engineers]
- The Society was formalized and incorporated under the laws of the State of New York in 1926 so it could obtain a permit to use a lake in [[places/Central-Park]] for model motor boat races. [src:New-York-Society-of-Model-Engineers]
- In 1926 the Society began construction of its first model railroad, "[[works/The-Union-Connecting]]". [src:New-York-Society-of-Model-Engineers]
- After the war the [[organizations/Lackawanna-Railroad]] invited the Society to move into its passenger terminal in [[places/Hoboken-New-Jersey]]. [src:New-York-Society-of-Model-Engineers]
- During the early 1950s the organization moved to its current location in [[places/Carlstadt-New-Jersey]]. [src:New-York-Society-of-Model-Engineers]
