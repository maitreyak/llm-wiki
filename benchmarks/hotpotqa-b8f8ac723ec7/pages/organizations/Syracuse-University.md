# Syracuse University

> Syracuse University is the school whose men's basketball team was famously upset by Vermont in the first round of the 2005 NCAA Tournament.

## Key Facts

- Syracuse University was upset by the [[organizations/Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball]] team in the first round of the 2005 NCAA Division I Men's Basketball Tournament. [src:Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball]
