# Madtown

> South Korean boy group formed in 2014 by J. Tune Camp, later contracted to GNI Entertainment.

## Key Facts

- Madtown (Hangul: 매드타운), often stylized as MADTOWN, is a South Korean boy group formed in 2014 by [[organizations/J-Tune-Camp]]. [src:Madtown]
- The group consists of Moos, Daewon, Lee Geon, Jota, Heo Jun, Buffy and H.O. [src:Madtown]
- Madtown's debut album, "Mad Town", was released on October 6, 2014. [src:Madtown]
- Two of the members, Moos and Buffy, originally debuted as the hip hop duo [[organizations/Pro-C]] in 2013. [src:Madtown]
- Madtown's official fan-base name is Mad-people. [src:Madtown]
- Starting December 22, 2016, MADTOWN's contract was sold to [[organizations/GNI-Entertainment]] after J. Tune Camp closed. [src:Madtown]
