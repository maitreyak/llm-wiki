# Boston University

> University in Boston, Massachusetts, whose athletic facilities include Case Gym, Walter Brown Arena and Agganis Arena.

## Key Facts

- [[places/Case-Gym]], a 1,800-seat multi-purpose arena, is located at Boston University in [[places/Boston]], Massachusetts. [src:Case-Gym]
- Boston University's fifth president was [[people/Harold-C-Case]], for whom the Harold Case Physical Education Center is named. [src:Case-Gym]
- Boston University left the [[organizations/America-East-Conference]] for the [[organizations/Patriot-League]] in July 2013. [src:Case-Gym]
- After joining the Patriot League, Boston University announced that all home men's basketball conference games would be played at [[places/Agganis-Arena]], with non-conference games at Case Gym. [src:Case-Gym]
- Boston University's athletic teams are known as the [[organizations/Boston-University-Terriers]]. [src:Case-Gym]
