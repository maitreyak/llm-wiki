# Shinee

> South Korean boy group formed by S.M. Entertainment in 2008 that has received several awards and nominations for its music work.

## Key Facts

- Shinee is a South Korean boy group that has received several awards and nominations for their music work. [src:List-of-awards-and-nominations-received-by-Shinee]
- Shinee was formed by [[organizations/S-M-Entertainment]] in 2008. [src:List-of-awards-and-nominations-received-by-Shinee]
- Shinee earned their first win on Korean music shows since debut when [[works/Love-Like-Oxygen]] took first place on [[media/M-Countdown]] on September 18, 2008. [src:List-of-awards-and-nominations-received-by-Shinee]
