# Stabbing Westward

> American industrial rock band whose debut album Ungod was released in 1994.

## Key Facts

- The band's debut album [[works/Ungod]] was released in 1994 on [[organizations/Columbia-Records]]. [src:Ungod]
- The band toured with acts such as [[organizations/Depeche-Mode]] following Ungod. [src:Ungod]
- A second album, [[works/Wither-Blister-Burn-Peel]], was warranted by adequate sales of Ungod and touring. [src:Ungod]
