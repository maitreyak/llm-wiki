# ECAC Hockey

> ECAC Hockey is the college ice hockey conference in which Vermont competed from 1974 to 2005.

## Key Facts

- ECAC Hockey was the conference of the [[organizations/Vermont-Catamounts-mens-ice-hockey]] team from 1974 to 2005. [src:Vermont-Catamounts-mens-ice-hockey]
- Vermont left ECAC Hockey in 2005 to join [[organizations/Hockey-East]]. [src:Vermont-Catamounts-mens-ice-hockey]
