# Square

> Japanese video game developer that began producing companion books in 1998 and merged with Enix on April 1, 2003 to form Square Enix.

## Key Facts

- Square was a video game developer that in 1998 began producing books focused on artwork, developer interviews, and game world background rather than gameplay details. [src:List-of-Square-Enix-companion-books]
- [[organizations/DigiCube]] was a subsidiary of Square. [src:List-of-Square-Enix-companion-books]
- Square merged with video game publisher [[organizations/Enix]] on April 1, 2003 to form [[organizations/Square-Enix]]. [src:List-of-Square-Enix-companion-books]
