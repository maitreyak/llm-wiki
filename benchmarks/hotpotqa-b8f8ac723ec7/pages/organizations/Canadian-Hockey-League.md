# Canadian Hockey League

> The umbrella organization for Canadian major junior ice hockey whose champion is determined annually by the Memorial Cup, comprising the WHL, OHL and QMJHL.

## Key Facts

- The Memorial Cup competition determines the major junior ice hockey champion of the Canadian Hockey League. [src:2007-Memorial-Cup]
