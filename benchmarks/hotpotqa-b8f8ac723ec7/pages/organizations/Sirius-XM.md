# Sirius XM

> Satellite radio provider whose channels include Faction and Turbo.

## Key Facts

- Sirius XM Satellite Radio carries [[media/Faction]], an uncensored mixed hard rock, punk, hip hop, and heavy metal channel. [src:Faction-Punk]
- Sirius XM operates [[media/Turbo]], its channel for hard rock from the 1990s and 2000s. [src:Faction-Punk]
- Sirius XM content is accessible via select Sirius XM radios, Sirius XM streaming, and the Sirius XM smartphone app. [src:Faction-Punk]
- In mid-July 2017, Sirius XM channel 41 temporarily carried [[organizations/Guns-N-Roses]] radio in place of Faction. [src:Faction-Punk]
