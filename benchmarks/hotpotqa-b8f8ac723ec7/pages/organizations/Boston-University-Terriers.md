# Boston University Terriers

> The athletic teams of Boston University, including men's and women's basketball and men's wrestling that play at Case Gym.

## Key Facts

- The Boston University Terriers men's and women's basketball teams and the men's wrestling team are based at [[places/Case-Gym]]. [src:Case-Gym]
- Terriers basketball games are also played in [[places/Agganis-Arena]] and on occasion [[places/Walter-Brown-Arena]]. [src:Case-Gym]
- The Terriers are the athletic teams of [[organizations/Boston-University]]. [src:Case-Gym]
- Following the move from the [[organizations/America-East-Conference]] to the [[organizations/Patriot-League]] in July 2013, all Terriers home men's basketball conference games were to be played at Agganis Arena. [src:Case-Gym]
