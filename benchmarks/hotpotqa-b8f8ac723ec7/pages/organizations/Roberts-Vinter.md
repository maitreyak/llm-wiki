# Roberts & Vinter

> Publisher that acquired Science Fantasy and New Worlds from Nova Publications in 1964 and ceased the magazine in 1967.

## Key Facts

- Roberts & Vinter acquired the magazine titles of [[organizations/Nova-Publications]] after Nova went out of business in early 1964. [src:Science-Fantasy-magazine]
- The firm hired [[people/Kyril-Bonfiglioli]] to edit [[media/Science-Fantasy]]. [src:Science-Fantasy-magazine]
- Roberts & Vinter came under financial pressure after their printer went bankrupt, leading to the end of Science Fantasy's publication in 1967. [src:Science-Fantasy-magazine]
