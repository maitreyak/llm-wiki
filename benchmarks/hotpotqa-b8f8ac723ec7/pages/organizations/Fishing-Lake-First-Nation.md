# Fishing Lake First Nation

> A First Nation that opposed the Government of Saskatchewan's plan to lower Fishing Lake with a drainage channel.

## Key Facts

- The Fishing Lake First Nation opposed the [[organizations/Government-of-Saskatchewan]] plan to lower the level of [[places/Fishing-Lake]] by digging a drainage channel. [src:Fishing-Lake]
- Instead of the drainage channel, flood control berms were constructed at [[places/Fishing-Lake]]. [src:Fishing-Lake]
