# Villard

> Publishing imprint of Random House, founded in 1983 and now a sub-imprint of Ballantine Books.

## Key Facts

- Villard, also known as Villard Books, is a publishing imprint of [[organizations/Random-House]]. [src:Villard-imprint]
- Villard was founded in 1983. [src:Villard-imprint]
- Villard began as an independent imprint of [[organizations/Random-House]] and is currently a sub-imprint of [[organizations/Ballantine-Books]]. [src:Villard-imprint]
- Villard was named after a [[people/Stanford-White]] brownstone mansion on [[places/Madison-Avenue]] that was the home of [[organizations/Random-House]] for twenty years. [src:Villard-imprint]
