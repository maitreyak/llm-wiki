# Vermont Catamounts men's basketball

> The Vermont Catamounts men's basketball team represents the University of Vermont, competes in the America East Conference at Patrick Gym, and has made six NCAA Tournament appearances including a famous 2005 upset of Syracuse.

## Key Facts

- The Vermont Catamounts basketball team represents the [[organizations/University-of-Vermont]] in Burlington, Vermont. [src:Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball]
- The team currently competes in the [[organizations/America-East-Conference]]. [src:Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball]
- The team plays its home games at [[places/Patrick-Gym]]. [src:Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball]
- The Catamounts have reached the NCAA Division I Men's Basketball Tournament six times, in 2003, 2004, 2005, 2010, 2012 and 2017. [src:Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball]
- UVM famously upset [[organizations/Syracuse-University]] in the first round of the 2005 NCAA Tournament. [src:Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball]
- The Catamounts are coached by [[people/John-Becker]]. [src:Vermont-Catamounts-mens-basketball]
