# Saint John Sea Dogs

> A QMJHL team that in the 2011–12 playoffs became the seventh franchise in league history to win consecutive President's Cup championships.

## Key Facts

- The Saint John Sea Dogs won the [[concepts/Presidents-Cup]] in the [[events/2011-12-QMJHL-season]] playoffs. [src:2011-12-QMJHL-season]
- With that title the Sea Dogs became the seventh team in [[organizations/Quebec-Major-Junior-Hockey-League]] history to capture consecutive President's Cup championships. [src:2011-12-QMJHL-season]
