# United States Department of State

> U.S. federal department for foreign affairs, organized into bureaus led by Assistant Secretaries who rank below the Under Secretaries.

## Key Facts

- The United States Department of State uses the title Assistant Secretary of State for many executive positions ranking below the Under Secretaries. [src:United-States-Assistant-Secretary-of-State]
- The Department of State is organized into individual bureaus, which are usually managed by Assistant Secretaries. [src:United-States-Assistant-Secretary-of-State]
- Six Assistant Secretaries report to the Department's Under Secretary for Political Affairs and manage diplomatic missions within designated geographic regions. [src:United-States-Assistant-Secretary-of-State]
- Managers of Department bureaus or agencies holding other titles, such as "Director," may be described as being of "Assistant Secretary equivalent rank." [src:United-States-Assistant-Secretary-of-State]
