# Manchester United F.C.

> English football club managed by Matt Busby from 1945 to 1969 and by Alex Ferguson from 1986 to 2013, which won the Double in 1995–96 — becoming the first English team to do so twice — and beat Newcastle United 4–0 in the 1996 FA Charity Shield; earlier managed by Ernest Mangnall from 1903 to 1912.

## Key Facts

- Manchester United was managed by [[people/Alex-Ferguson]] from 1986 to 2013. [src:Alex-Ferguson]
- Manchester United played their fourth [[organizations/Premier-League]] season in [[events/1995-96-Manchester-United-F-C-season]]. [src:1995-96-Manchester-United-F-C-season]
- 1995–96 was Manchester United's 21st consecutive season in the top division of English football. [src:1995-96-Manchester-United-F-C-season]
- Manchester United became the first English team to win the [[concepts/The-Double]] twice, achieving it in 1995–96. [src:1995-96-Manchester-United-F-C-season]
- In 1995–96 the club was managed by [[people/Alex-Ferguson]]. [src:1995-96-Manchester-United-F-C-season]
- Manchester United qualified for the [[events/1996-FA-Charity-Shield]] as winners of the 1995–96 Premier League and [[events/FA-Cup]] Double. [src:1996-FA-Charity-Shield]
- Manchester United beat [[organizations/Newcastle-United-F-C]] 4–0 in the 1996 FA Charity Shield at [[places/Wembley-Stadium]] on 11 August 1996. [src:1996-FA-Charity-Shield]
- The club's goals in the 1996 FA Charity Shield came from [[people/Eric-Cantona]], [[people/Nicky-Butt]], [[people/David-Beckham]] and [[people/Roy-Keane]]. [src:1996-FA-Charity-Shield]
- Manchester United was managed by [[people/Ernest-Mangnall]] between 1903 and 1912. [src:Ernest-Mangnall]
- Ernest Mangnall is the only man to date to have managed both Manchester United and [[organizations/Manchester-City-F-C]]. [src:Ernest-Mangnall]
- Manchester United was managed by [[people/Matt-Busby]] between 1945 and 1969, and again for the second half of the 1970–71 season. [src:Matt-Busby]
- Matt Busby's managerial records and longevity at Manchester United are surpassed only by [[people/Alex-Ferguson]]. [src:Matt-Busby]
