# David Beckham Academy

> Football school founded by David Beckham in 2005, with sites in London and Los Angeles that both closed around late 2009.

## Key Facts

- The David Beckham Academy was a football school founded by England international [[people/David-Beckham]] in 2005. [src:David-Beckham-Academy]
- In 2009 the David Beckham Academy operated in two locations: London, United Kingdom, and [[places/Los-Angeles]], California, United States. [src:David-Beckham-Academy]
- The academy pulled out of the London site at the end of the lease in October 2009. [src:David-Beckham-Academy]
- The California branch of the David Beckham Academy closed soon after the London site was vacated. [src:David-Beckham-Academy]
