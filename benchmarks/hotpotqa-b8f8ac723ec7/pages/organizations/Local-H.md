# Local H

> American rock band formed in Zion, Illinois by Scott Lucas and high-school friends; Chicago-based band whose seventh studio album was Hallelujah! I'm A Bum.

## Key Facts

- Local H is a rock band based in [[places/Chicago]]. [src:Hallelujah-Im-a-Bum-album]
- [[works/Hallelujah-Im-a-Bum]] is Local H's seventh studio album. [src:Hallelujah-Im-a-Bum-album]
- Interviews with Local H revealed that Hallelujah! I'm A Bum is a concept album based on politics in the United States. [src:Hallelujah-Im-a-Bum-album]
- The band completed the album in spring 2012 but chose to release it in September to be closer to the [[events/2012-United-States-presidential-election]]. [src:Hallelujah-Im-a-Bum-album]
- Local H is an American rock band originally formed in [[places/Zion-Illinois]] in 1987. [src:Local-H]
- The band's original lineup consisted of guitarist and vocalist [[people/Scott-Lucas]], bassist [[people/Matt-Garcia]], drummer [[people/Joe-Daniels]], and lead guitarist [[people/John-Sparkman]]. [src:Local-H]
- The members all met in high school in 1987 and founded Local H three years later. [src:Local-H]
- Local H was among the national acts that the band [[organizations/Ophur]] performed with. [src:Ophur]
- [[organizations/Suffrajett]] has toured with Local H. [src:Suffrajett]
