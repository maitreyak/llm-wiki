# Government of Saskatchewan

> The provincial government of Saskatchewan, which planned to lower Fishing Lake after record 2007 floods.

## Key Facts

- Following record floods in 2007, the Government of Saskatchewan planned to lower the level of [[places/Fishing-Lake]] by digging a drainage channel. [src:Fishing-Lake]
- The plan was opposed by the [[organizations/Fishing-Lake-First-Nation]], and flood control berms were constructed instead. [src:Fishing-Lake]
