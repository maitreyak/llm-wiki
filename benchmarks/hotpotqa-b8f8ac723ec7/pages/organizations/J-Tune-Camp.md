# J. Tune Camp

> South Korean entertainment agency that formed the boy group Madtown in 2014 and later closed.

## Key Facts

- J. Tune Camp formed the South Korean boy group [[organizations/Madtown]] in 2014. [src:Madtown]
- J. Tune Camp closed, and MADTOWN's contract was sold to [[organizations/GNI-Entertainment]] starting December 22, 2016. [src:Madtown]
