# University of Missouri–Kansas City

> Public research university in Kansas City, Missouri, one of four campuses of the University of Missouri system, serving the greater Kansas City metropolitan area.

## Key Facts

- UMKC is located in [[places/Kansas-City-Missouri]]. [src:University-of-Missouri-Kansas-City]
- UMKC is one of four system campuses that collectively constitute the [[organizations/University-of-Missouri]]. [src:University-of-Missouri-Kansas-City]
- In 2017 UMKC, in collaboration with Truman Medical Center, Children's Mercy Hospital, the Missouri Health Department, the Jackson County Medical Examiners Office, and the Missouri Department of Mental Health Behavioral Medicine, formed the [[organizations/UMKC-Health-Sciences-District]] on Hospital Hill. [src:University-of-Missouri-Kansas-City]
- Also in 2017, UMKC announced plans to expand its metropolitan identity with the construction of a downtown Campus for the Arts, located near the [[places/Kauffman-Center-for-the-Performing-Arts]]. [src:University-of-Missouri-Kansas-City]
- UMKC's enrollment as of 2015 exceeded more than 16,600 students. [src:University-of-Missouri-Kansas-City]
