# Blainville-Boisbriand Armada

> A QMJHL team that began play under its current name in 2011–12 after relocating to Boisbriand from Verdun, where it had operated as the Montreal Junior Hockey Club from 2008 to 2011.

## Key Facts

- The [[events/2011-12-QMJHL-season]] was the Blainville-Boisbriand Armada's first season in the [[organizations/Quebec-Major-Junior-Hockey-League]]. [src:2011-12-QMJHL-season]
- In Verdun the franchise played as the Montreal Junior Hockey Club from 2008 to 2011. [src:2011-12-QMJHL-season]
