# For Against

> For Against is a United States post-punk/dream pop band from Lincoln, Nebraska, formed in 1984 and producing material steadily since.

## Key Facts

- For Against is a United States post-punk/dream pop band. [src:For-Against]
- For Against is from [[places/Lincoln-Nebraska]]. [src:For-Against]
- For Against was formed in 1984. [src:For-Against]
- The band has experienced numerous lineup shuffles and some periods of dormancy. [src:For-Against]
- Despite lineup changes and dormancy, For Against has produced material steadily from its 1984 inception to the present. [src:For-Against]
