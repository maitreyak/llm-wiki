# Nova Publications

> British publisher of the science fiction magazines New Worlds and Science Fantasy until it went out of business in early 1964.

## Key Facts

- Nova Publications launched [[media/Science-Fantasy]] in 1950 as a companion to its magazine [[media/New-Worlds]]. [src:Science-Fantasy-magazine]
- Nova Publications went out of business in early 1964, and its magazine titles were acquired by [[organizations/Roberts-Vinter]]. [src:Science-Fantasy-magazine]
