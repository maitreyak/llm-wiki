# United States National Security Council

> U.S. body where Robert Hormats served as senior staff member for International Economic Affairs from 1969 to 1977.

## Key Facts

- [[people/Robert-Hormats]] served as a senior staff member for International Economic Affairs on the United States National Security Council from 1969 to 1977. [src:Robert-Hormats]
- At the NSC, Hormats was senior economic adviser to [[people/Henry-Kissinger]], General [[people/Brent-Scowcroft]] and [[people/Zbigniew-Brzezinski]]. [src:Robert-Hormats]
