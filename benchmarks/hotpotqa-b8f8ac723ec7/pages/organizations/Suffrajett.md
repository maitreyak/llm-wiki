# Suffrajett

> United States rock band from New York City, currently based in Chicago, Illinois, featuring Simi Sernaker, Jason Chasko, Kevin Roberts, and Danny Severson.

## Key Facts

- Suffrajett is a United States rock band from [[places/New-York-City]], currently based in [[places/Chicago]], Illinois. [src:Suffrajett]
- Suffrajett is composed of singer/violinist [[people/Simi-Sernaker]], guitarist [[people/Jason-Chasko]], bassist [[people/Kevin-Roberts]], and drummer [[people/Danny-Severson]]. [src:Suffrajett]
- Suffrajett has toured with [[organizations/Local-H]], [[organizations/The-Last-Vegas]], and [[people/Bob-Schneider]]. [src:Suffrajett]
- Suffrajett has opened for the reunited [[organizations/MC5]]. [src:Suffrajett]
- Suffrajett released their self-titled debut album on February 2, 2003. [src:Suffrajett]
- Suffrajett's latest album [[works/Black-Glitter]] was released on January 1, 2007. [src:Suffrajett]
- Suffrajett recently toured with [[organizations/Juliette-and-the-Licks]] and [[organizations/Scissors-for-Lefty]]. [src:Suffrajett]
