# Seventeen

> South Korean boy group formed by Pledis Entertainment in 2015, also stylized as SEVENTEEN or SVT.

- Seventeen's name is written in Hangul as 세븐틴 and is also stylized as SEVENTEEN or SVT. [src:Seventeen-discography]
