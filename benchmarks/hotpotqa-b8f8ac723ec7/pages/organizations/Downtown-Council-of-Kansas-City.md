# Downtown Council of Kansas City

> Organization that defines the boundaries of Downtown Kansas City in the most commonly accepted way.

## Key Facts

- The Downtown Council of Kansas City defines [[places/Downtown-Kansas-City]] as extending from the Missouri River in the north to 31st Street in the south, and from the [[places/Kansas]]–Missouri state line east to Bruce R. Watkins Drive. [src:Downtown-Kansas-City]
- The definition of downtown used by the Downtown Council of Kansas City is the most commonly accepted one. [src:Downtown-Kansas-City]
