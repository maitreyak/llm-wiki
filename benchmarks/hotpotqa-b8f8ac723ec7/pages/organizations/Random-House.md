# Random House

> Book publisher headquartered in the Random House Tower in New York City, and one of the largest publishing companies in the world.

## Key Facts

- Random House is a book publisher whose headquarters is in the [[places/Random-House-Tower]] in [[places/New-York-City]]. [src:Random-House-Tower]
- The Random House entrance to the tower is on Broadway and the publisher occupies up to 27 floors. [src:Random-House-Tower]
- [[organizations/Villard]], also known as Villard Books, is a publishing imprint of Random House, founded in 1983. [src:Villard-imprint]
- [[organizations/Ballantine-Books]] is an imprint of Random House. [src:Villard-imprint]
- Random House was housed for twenty years in a [[people/Stanford-White]] brownstone mansion on [[places/Madison-Avenue]], after which the Villard imprint was named. [src:Villard-imprint]
