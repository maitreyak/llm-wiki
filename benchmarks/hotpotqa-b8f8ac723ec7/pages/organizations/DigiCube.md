# DigiCube

> A subsidiary of Square that wrote and published the Perfect Works companion books and published the Ultimania series until its dissolution in 2003.

## Key Facts

- DigiCube was a subsidiary of [[organizations/Square]]. [src:List-of-Square-Enix-companion-books]
- DigiCube wrote and published the [[works/Perfect-Works]] series of companion books. [src:List-of-Square-Enix-companion-books]
- DigiCube published the [[works/Ultimania]] books until the company was dissolved in 2003. [src:List-of-Square-Enix-companion-books]
