# Tyndale House Publishing

> Publisher of both the adult Left Behind series and the young adult Left Behind: The Kids series.

## Key Facts

- Tyndale House Publishing published [[works/Left-Behind-The-Kids]] as well as the adult series [[works/Left-Behind]]. [src:Left-Behind-The-Kids]
- It released the 40 books of Left Behind: The Kids over the period 1997-2004. [src:Left-Behind-The-Kids]
