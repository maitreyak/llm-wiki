# Medicine Hat Tigers

> The WHL champion in 2006–07 that reached the 2007 Memorial Cup final, losing 3–1 to the host Vancouver Giants.

## Key Facts

- The Medicine Hat Tigers were the [[organizations/Western-Hockey-League]] champion entrant at the [[events/2007-Memorial-Cup]]. [src:2007-Memorial-Cup]
- Medicine Hat lost the 2007 Memorial Cup final 3–1 to the [[organizations/Vancouver-Giants]]. [src:2007-Memorial-Cup]
- The Tigers' 2007 final against Vancouver was the second all-WHL Memorial Cup final in tournament history. [src:2007-Memorial-Cup]
