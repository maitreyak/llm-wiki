# Vancouver Giants

> A WHL team from Vancouver that hosted and won the 2007 Memorial Cup, its first, in its second consecutive national junior championship appearance.

## Key Facts

- The Vancouver Giants were the host team of the [[events/2007-Memorial-Cup]] in [[places/Vancouver]]. [src:2007-Memorial-Cup]
- The Giants won their first Memorial Cup in 2007, defeating the [[organizations/Medicine-Hat-Tigers]] 3–1 in the final. [src:2007-Memorial-Cup]
- As a [[organizations/Western-Hockey-League]] team, the Giants took part in the second all-WHL Memorial Cup final in tournament history. [src:2007-Memorial-Cup]
