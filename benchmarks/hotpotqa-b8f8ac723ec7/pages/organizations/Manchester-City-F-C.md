# Manchester City F.C.

> English football club whose managers included Joshua Parlby in the 1890s and Ernest Mangnall from 1912 to 1924.

## Key Facts

- Manchester City was managed by [[people/Ernest-Mangnall]] from 1912 to 1924. [src:Ernest-Mangnall]
- Ernest Mangnall is the only man to date to have managed both Manchester City and [[organizations/Manchester-United-F-C]]. [src:Ernest-Mangnall]
- Manchester City was managed by [[people/Joshua-Parlby]] in the 1890s. [src:Joshua-Parlby]
