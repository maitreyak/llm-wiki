# Paris Opera

> Parisian opera house where Armand Castelmary created roles in three major premieres between 1865 and 1868.

## Key Facts

- [[people/Armand-Castelmary]] created roles in three major premieres at the Paris Opera. [src:Armand-Castelmary]
- L Africaine by [[people/Giacomo-Meyerbeer]] premiered at the Paris Opera in 1865. [src:Armand-Castelmary]
- [[works/Don-Carlos]] by [[people/Giuseppe-Verdi]] premiered at the Paris Opera in 1867. [src:Armand-Castelmary]
- [[works/Hamlet]] by [[people/Ambroise-Thomas]] premiered at the Paris Opera in 1868. [src:Armand-Castelmary]
