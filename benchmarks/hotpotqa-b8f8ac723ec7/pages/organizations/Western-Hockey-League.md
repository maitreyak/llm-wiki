# Western Hockey League

> A CHL member major junior league whose 2006–07 champion was the Medicine Hat Tigers; two WHL teams met in the 2007 Memorial Cup final.

## Key Facts

- The Western Hockey League is a member league of the [[organizations/Canadian-Hockey-League]] that sends its champion to the [[concepts/Memorial-Cup]]. [src:2007-Memorial-Cup]
- The [[organizations/Medicine-Hat-Tigers]] were the WHL champion at the [[events/2007-Memorial-Cup]]. [src:2007-Memorial-Cup]
- The 2007 final between Medicine Hat and the [[organizations/Vancouver-Giants]] was the second all-WHL Memorial Cup final, the first being in 1989. [src:2007-Memorial-Cup]
