# History

> South Korean boy group formed by LOEN Entertainment in 2013, the label's first boy group, active until its disbandment in 2017.

## Key Facts

- History (Korean: 히스토리) was a South Korean boy group formed by [[organizations/LOEN-Entertainment]] in 2013. [src:History-band]
- History debuted on April 26, 2013 with the song "Dreamer", which featured the narration of their labelmate [[people/IU]]. [src:History-band]
- History were [[organizations/LOEN-Entertainment]]'s first boy group. [src:History-band]
- History officially disbanded on May 12, 2017. [src:History-band]
