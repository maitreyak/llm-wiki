# NBC

> American broadcasting network that aired the reality-competition show "Phenomenon" and, in radio's Golden Age, carried "A Date with Judy", "The Bob Hope Show" and a 1948 run of "Meet Corliss Archer".

## Key Facts

- NBC aired the reality-competition television show [[works/Phenomenon]], starring [[people/Criss-Angel]]. [src:Criss-Angel]
- NBC broadcast the popular program [[works/A-Date-with-Judy]], to which [[organizations/CBS]]'s [[works/Meet-Corliss-Archer]] was an answer. [src:Meet-Corliss-Archer]
- NBC broadcast [[works/Meet-Corliss-Archer]] in 1948 as a summer replacement for [[works/The-Bob-Hope-Show]]. [src:Meet-Corliss-Archer]
