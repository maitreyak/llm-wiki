# CBS

> American broadcasting network that originated the radio program Meet Corliss Archer and aired its 1951 television adaptation.

## Key Facts

- [[works/Meet-Corliss-Archer]] was CBS's answer to [[organizations/NBC]]'s popular program [[works/A-Date-with-Judy]]. [src:Meet-Corliss-Archer]
- After airing on [[organizations/ABC]] from October 3, 1952 to June 26, 1953, [[works/Meet-Corliss-Archer]] returned to CBS. [src:Meet-Corliss-Archer]
- CBS aired the television sitcom [[works/Meet-Corliss-Archer-TV-series]] from July 13, 1951 to August 10, 1951. [src:Meet-Corliss-Archer-TV-series]
