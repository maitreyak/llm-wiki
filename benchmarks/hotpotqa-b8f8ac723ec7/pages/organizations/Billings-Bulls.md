# Billings Bulls

> Junior ice hockey organization based in Billings, Montana, which played home games at Centennial Ice Arena after losing its lease at Metrapark.

## Key Facts

- The Billings Bulls were a junior ice hockey organization based in [[places/Billings-Montana]]. [src:Billings-Bulls]
- The Bulls most recently played home games at the 550-seat [[places/Centennial-Ice-Arena]]. [src:Billings-Bulls]
- Due to the small size of Centennial Ice Arena, the Bulls frequently sold out games. [src:Billings-Bulls]
- The Bulls previously played their home games at [[places/Metrapark]], which had a maximum capacity of 9,000 for hockey games. [src:Billings-Bulls]
- A negotiating dispute with arena officials and local county commissioners resulted in the Bulls losing their lease at Metrapark. [src:Billings-Bulls]
