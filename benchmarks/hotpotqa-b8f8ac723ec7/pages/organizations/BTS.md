# BTS

> South Korean boy group whose early discography includes the 2013 debut single album "2 Cool 4 Skool" and the extended plays "O!RUL8,2?" and "Skool Luv Affair".

## Key Facts

- BTS is a South Korean boy group. [src:BTS-discography]
- BTS debuted in South Korea in June 2013 with the single album [[works/2-Cool-4-Skool]]. [src:BTS-discography]
- BTS made a comeback in September 2013 with the extended play [[works/O-RUL8-2]]. [src:BTS-discography]
- BTS released their second extended play, [[works/Skool-Luv-Affair]], in February 2014. [src:BTS-discography]
- "Skool Luv Affair" marked the first time a BTS album charted on international charts, specifically [[concepts/Billboard-World-Albums]] and Japan's [[concepts/Oricon-Chart]]. [src:BTS-discography]
- A repackaged version of "Skool Luv Affair", titled [[works/Skool-Luv-Affair-Special-Addition]], was released in May 2014. [src:BTS-discography]
