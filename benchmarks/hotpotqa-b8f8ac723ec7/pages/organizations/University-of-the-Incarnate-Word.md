# University of the Incarnate Word

> A private Catholic university founded in 1881, with its main campus in San Antonio and Alamo Heights, Texas.

## Key Facts

- The University of the Incarnate Word (UIW) is a private Catholic university in the United States. [src:University-of-the-Incarnate-Word]
- Its main campus is located in [[places/San-Antonio]] and [[places/Alamo-Heights]], Texas. [src:University-of-the-Incarnate-Word]
- The university was founded in 1881 by the [[organizations/Sisters-of-Charity-of-the-Incarnate-Word]]. [src:University-of-the-Incarnate-Word]
- The main campus occupies 154 acres. [src:University-of-the-Incarnate-Word]
