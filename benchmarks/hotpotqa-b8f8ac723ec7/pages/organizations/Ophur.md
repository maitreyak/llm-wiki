# Ophur

> Rock band from the Chicago suburbs in DuPage County that performed in the midwestern United States for seven years alongside numerous national acts.

## Key Facts

- Ophur was a rock band from the Chicago suburbs in [[places/DuPage-County]]. [src:Ophur]
- Ophur performed in the midwestern United States over the course of seven years. [src:Ophur]
- Ophur performed with national acts including The Plain White T's, Sum 41, Violent Femmes, [[organizations/Local-H]], Lucky Boys Confusion, Veruca Salt, Two Skinee J's, Duvall, and Sleeping at Last. [src:Ophur]
- Ophur's music has been described as an eclectic amalgamation of U2, Red Hot Chili Peppers, and Pink Floyd. [src:Ophur]
