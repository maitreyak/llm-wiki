# Vermont Catamounts men's ice hockey

> The Vermont Catamounts men's ice hockey team is an NCAA Division I program representing the University of Vermont, competing in Hockey East and playing at Gutterson Fieldhouse.

## Key Facts

- The Vermont Catamounts men's ice hockey team is a National Collegiate Athletic Association (NCAA) Division I college ice hockey program. [src:Vermont-Catamounts-mens-ice-hockey]
- The team represents the [[organizations/University-of-Vermont]]. [src:Vermont-Catamounts-mens-ice-hockey]
- The Catamounts are a member of [[organizations/Hockey-East]], joining in 2005. [src:Vermont-Catamounts-mens-ice-hockey]
- Vermont competed in [[organizations/ECAC-Hockey]] from 1974 to 2005 before joining Hockey East. [src:Vermont-Catamounts-mens-ice-hockey]
- The team plays home games at [[places/Gutterson-Fieldhouse]] in Burlington, Vermont. [src:Vermont-Catamounts-mens-ice-hockey]
- Vermont has appeared in the [[events/NCAA-Mens-Hockey-Championship]] five times since moving to Division I in 1974–75. [src:Vermont-Catamounts-mens-ice-hockey]
- Vermont's NCAA appearances include trips to the Frozen Four in 1996 and 2009. [src:Vermont-Catamounts-mens-ice-hockey]
