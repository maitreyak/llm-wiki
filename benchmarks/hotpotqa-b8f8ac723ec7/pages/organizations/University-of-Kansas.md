# University of Kansas

> Public research university in Kansas, with its main campus in Lawrence on Mount Oread, branch campuses in the Kansas City metro area, and membership in the Association of American Universities.

## Key Facts

- The University of Kansas has a fight song called [[works/Kansas-Song]] ("We're From Kansas"). [src:Kansas-Song]
- The University of Kansas is often referred to as KU or Kansas. [src:University-of-Kansas]
- The University of Kansas is a public research university in the U.S. state of [[places/Kansas]]. [src:University-of-Kansas]
- The main campus sits on [[places/Mount-Oread]], the highest elevation in Lawrence. [src:University-of-Kansas]
- Two branch campuses of the University of Kansas are in the [[places/Kansas-City-metropolitan-area]]: the Edwards Campus in [[places/Overland-Park-Kansas]], and the university's medical school and hospital in [[places/Kansas-City-Kansas]]. [src:University-of-Kansas]
- The University of Kansas has educational and research sites in [[places/Parsons-Kansas]], Topeka, Garden City, Hays, and Leavenworth. [src:University-of-Kansas]
- The University of Kansas medical school has branches in Wichita and Salina. [src:University-of-Kansas]
- The University of Kansas is one of the 62 members of the [[organizations/Association-of-American-Universities]]. [src:University-of-Kansas]
