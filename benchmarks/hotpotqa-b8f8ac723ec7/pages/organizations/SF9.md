# SF9

> SF9 is a South Korean boy group formed by FNC Entertainment, debuting on October 5, 2016.

## Key Facts

- SF9 (Korean: 에스에프나인) is a South Korean boy group formed by [[organizations/FNC-Entertainment]]. [src:SF9-band]
- The name SF9 is shortened from "Sensational Feeling 9". [src:SF9-band]
- SF9 is FNC Entertainment's first dance boy group to ever debut. [src:SF9-band]
- SF9 debuted on October 5, 2016 with the release of their first single album [[works/Feeling-Sensation]]. [src:SF9-band]
