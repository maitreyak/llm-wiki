# VIXX

> South Korean boy group that Ravi debuted with as a rapper in 2012.

## Key Facts

- VIXX is a South Korean boy group in which [[people/Ravi]] began his career as a rapper in 2012. [src:List-of-songs-written-by-Ravi]
- VIXX's debut single was "[[works/Super-Hero]]", which Ravi participated in co-writing. [src:List-of-songs-written-by-Ravi]
- As of November 2016, with the release of "VIXX 2016 Conception Ker", Ravi had contributed to writing and composing over 46 songs recorded by VIXX. [src:List-of-songs-written-by-Ravi]
