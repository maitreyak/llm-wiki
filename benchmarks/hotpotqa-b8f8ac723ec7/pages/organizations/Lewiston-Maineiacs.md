# Lewiston Maineiacs

> A QMJHL junior ice hockey team based in Lewiston, Maine, playing at the Androscoggin Bank Colisée; the second QMJHL team in the United States and the only one to play a full season, it finished first overall in 2006–07, winning its first Jean Rougeau Trophy and President's Cup with a 16-1 playoff record and representing the QMJHL at the 2007 Memorial Cup before folding after 2010–11.

## Key Facts

- The Lewiston Maineiacs were a junior ice hockey team of the [[organizations/Quebec-Major-Junior-Hockey-League]] based in [[places/Lewiston]], Maine. [src:Lewiston-Maineiacs]
- The team played its home games at the [[places/Androscoggin-Bank-Colisee]]. [src:Lewiston-Maineiacs]
- The Maineiacs were the second QMJHL team in the United States and the only one to play a full season. [src:Lewiston-Maineiacs]
- The Maineiacs won the [[concepts/Presidents-Cup]] in 2007. [src:Lewiston-Maineiacs]
- The Lewiston Maineiacs finished first overall in the [[events/2006-07-QMJHL-season]] regular season. [src:2006-07-QMJHL-season]
- Lewiston won its first [[concepts/Jean-Rougeau-Trophy]] in 2006–07. [src:2006-07-QMJHL-season]
- Lewiston won 16 playoff games while losing only one on the way to its first [[concepts/Presidents-Cup]]. [src:2006-07-QMJHL-season]
- Lewiston defeated the [[organizations/Val-dOr-Foreurs]] in the 2006–07 [[organizations/Quebec-Major-Junior-Hockey-League]] finals. [src:2006-07-QMJHL-season]
- As QMJHL champion, Lewiston competed at the [[events/2007-Memorial-Cup]] in Vancouver. [src:2007-Memorial-Cup]
- Lewiston was one of the QMJHL's charter teams and folded after the season preceding the [[events/2011-12-QMJHL-season]]. [src:2011-12-QMJHL-season]
