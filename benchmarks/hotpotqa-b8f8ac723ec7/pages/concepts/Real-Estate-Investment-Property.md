# Real Estate Investment Property

> A real estate property held as an investment, whose financial characteristics are analyzed through benchmarking; real estate is an asset class used for income, capital gains, diversification and inflation hedging.

## Key Facts

- The financial characteristics of a real estate investment property are analyzed using [[concepts/Real-Estate-Benchmarking]]. [src:Real-estate-benchmarking]
- Potential real estate investment properties can be compared against a predetermined framework of measurement. [src:Real-estate-benchmarking]
- Real estate is an asset class which represents a significant investment. [src:Real-estate-technology]
- Real estate property categories are broadly residential, commercial and industrial. [src:Real-estate-technology]
- Real estate was historically used as a more tactical investment or for providing portfolio alpha. [src:Real-estate-technology]
- Real estate has been used as part of a long-term core strategy due to increased market efficiency and increasing concerns about the future long-term variability of stock and bond returns. [src:Real-estate-technology]
- Real estate is known for its ability to serve as a portfolio diversifier and an inflation hedge. [src:Real-estate-technology]
