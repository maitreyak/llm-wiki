# Military Health System

> The Department of Defense enterprise, also known by its health.mil designation, that provides health services in support of the United States' military mission.

## Key Facts

- The Military Health System (MHS) mission is to provide optimal Health Services in support of the United States' military mission. [src:Edward-D-Martin]
- The MHS DoD enterprise consists of the Office of the Assistant Secretary of Defense (Health Affairs); the medical departments of the Army, Navy, Marine Corps, Air Force, Coast Guard, and Joint Chiefs of Staff; the Combatant Command surgeons; and TRICARE providers. [src:Edward-D-Martin]
- [[people/Edward-D-Martin]] served as the Secretary's principal advisor on matters related to the military health system. [src:Edward-D-Martin]
