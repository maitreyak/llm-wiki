# Real Estate Benchmarking

> A standard of measurement used to analyze the financial characteristics of real estate investment properties, either as a comparative framework or as the specific indicators used.

## Key Facts

- Real estate benchmarking is the standard of measurement used to analyze the financial characteristics of a [[concepts/Real-Estate-Investment-Property]]. [src:Real-estate-benchmarking]
- In the general sense, real estate benchmarking refers to the comparison of potential real estate investment properties against a predetermined framework of measurement. [src:Real-estate-benchmarking]
- In a narrow sense, real estate benchmarking refers to the specific real estate indicators used to measure real estate properties. [src:Real-estate-benchmarking]
