# Lord High Chancellor

> A Great Officer of State ranking above the Lord High Treasurer and below the Lord High Steward; certain of its departmental functions passed to the Secretary of State for Constitutional Affairs in 2003.

## Key Facts

- The Lord High Chancellor ranks above the [[concepts/Lord-High-Treasurer]] among the [[concepts/Great-Officer-of-State]] offices. [src:Lord-High-Treasurer]
- The Lord High Chancellor is named alongside the [[concepts/Lord-High-Steward]] as ranking above the Lord High Treasurer. [src:Lord-High-Treasurer]
- Certain functions of the Lord Chancellor relating to the Lord Chancellor's Department were transferred to the [[concepts/Secretary-of-State-for-Constitutional-Affairs]], an office created in 2003. [src:Secretary-of-State-for-Constitutional-Affairs]
