# Secretary of State for Constitutional Affairs

> A British Government office created in 2003 that took over certain Lord Chancellor functions.

## Key Facts

- The office of Secretary of State for Constitutional Affairs was a British Government position, created in 2003. [src:Secretary-of-State-for-Constitutional-Affairs]
- Certain functions of the [[concepts/Lord-High-Chancellor]] which related to the Lord Chancellor's Department were transferred to the Secretary of State for Constitutional Affairs. [src:Secretary-of-State-for-Constitutional-Affairs]
- At a later date further functions were transferred to the Secretary of State for Constitutional Affairs from the [[concepts/First-Secretary-of-State]]. [src:Secretary-of-State-for-Constitutional-Affairs]
