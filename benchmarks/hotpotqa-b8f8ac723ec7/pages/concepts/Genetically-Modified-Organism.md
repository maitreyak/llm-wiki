# Genetically Modified Organism

> Genetically modified organisms, whose cultivation, production and distribution were banned in Mendocino County by Measure H.

## Key Facts

- [[places/Mendocino-County]] was the first U.S. jurisdiction to ban the cultivation, production or distribution of genetically modified organisms (GMOs). [src:Mendocino-County-GMO-Ban]
- Mendocino County has been added to an international list of "GMO free zones." [src:Mendocino-County-GMO-Ban]
- Pre-emptive statutes banning local municipalities from adopting GMO ordinances have become widespread, with adoption in sixteen states. [src:Mendocino-County-GMO-Ban]
