# Jack Slater

> Fictional Los Angeles police detective and the action film franchise bearing his name within Last Action Hero.

## Key Facts

- Jack Slater is a Los Angeles police detective within the fictional "Jack Slater" action film franchise depicted in [[works/Last-Action-Hero]]. [src:Last-Action-Hero]
- Jack Slater is played by [[people/Arnold-Schwarzenegger]], who also plays himself as the actor portraying Slater. [src:Last-Action-Hero]
- An assassin played by [[people/Charles-Dance]] escapes from the "Slater" world into the real world. [src:Last-Action-Hero]
- A boy played by [[people/Austin-OBrien]] is magically transported into the "Slater" universe. [src:Last-Action-Hero]
