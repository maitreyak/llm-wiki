# Gaon Weekly Chart

> South Korean weekly music chart that ranked BTS's early album releases.

## Key Facts

- The Gaon Weekly Chart is a South Korean weekly chart on which [[organizations/BTS]]'s early releases charted. [src:BTS-discography]
- [[works/2-Cool-4-Skool]] placed at number 5 on the Week 31 Gaon Weekly Chart. [src:BTS-discography]
- [[works/O-RUL8-2]] peaked at number 4 on the Week 38 Gaon Weekly Chart. [src:BTS-discography]
- [[works/Skool-Luv-Affair]] charted at number 1 on the Week 18 Gaon Weekly Chart, and [[works/Skool-Luv-Affair-Special-Addition]] at number 1 on the Week 21 chart. [src:BTS-discography]
