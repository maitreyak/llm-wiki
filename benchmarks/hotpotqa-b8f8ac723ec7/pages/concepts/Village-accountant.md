# Village accountant

> An administrative government position found in rural parts of the Indian subcontinent, known by many regional names such as patwari, talati, karnam and adhikari.

## Key Facts

- The Village Accountant is an administrative government position found in rural parts of the Indian sub-continent. [src:Village-accountant]
- In Sindh the position is called "tapedar". [src:Village-accountant]
- The position is known as the "karnam" in Andhra Pradesh. [src:Village-accountant]
- The position is known as "patnaik" in Orissa. [src:Village-accountant]
- The position is known as "adhikari" in Tamil Nadu. [src:Village-accountant]
- The position is commonly known as the "talati" in Karnataka, Gujarat and Maharashtra. [src:Village-accountant]
- The position was known as the "kulkarni" in Northern Karnataka and Maharashtra. [src:Village-accountant]
- The position was known as the "shanbogaru" in South Karnataka. [src:Village-accountant]
