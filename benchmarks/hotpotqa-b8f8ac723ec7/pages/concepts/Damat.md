# Damat

> Ottoman title meaning "bridegroom", given to men who married into the Ottoman dynasty.

## Key Facts

- "Damat" means "bridegroom" and designated a man married into the Ottoman dynasty. [src:Kucuk-Huseyin-Pasha]
- [[people/Kucuk-Huseyin-Pasha]] became a damat to the Ottoman dynasty after marrying the Ottoman princess [[topics/Esma-Sultan]]. [src:Kucuk-Huseyin-Pasha]
