# President's Cup

> The QMJHL playoff championship trophy, won for the first time by the Lewiston Maineiacs in 2006–07 (2007) after they beat the Val-d'Or Foreurs in the finals, and won in consecutive years by the Saint John Sea Dogs through 2011–12.

## Key Facts

- The [[organizations/Lewiston-Maineiacs]] won their first President's Cup in [[events/2006-07-QMJHL-season]], going 16-1 in the playoffs. [src:2006-07-QMJHL-season]
- The 2006–07 President's Cup finals were won by Lewiston over the [[organizations/Val-dOr-Foreurs]]. [src:2006-07-QMJHL-season]
- The [[organizations/Saint-John-Sea-Dogs]] won the President's Cup in the [[events/2011-12-QMJHL-season]] playoffs, becoming the seventh team in league history to win it in consecutive seasons. [src:2011-12-QMJHL-season]
- The [[organizations/Lewiston-Maineiacs]] won the President's Cup in 2007. [src:Lewiston-Maineiacs]
