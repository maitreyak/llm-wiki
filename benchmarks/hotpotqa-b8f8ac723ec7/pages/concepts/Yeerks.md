# Yeerks

> Fictional species in Animorphs that enslaved the Hork-Bajir and invaded their world.

## Key Facts

- The Yeerks enslaved the [[concepts/Hork-Bajir]], a story told by [[people/Jara-Hamee]] in [[works/The-Hork-Bajir-Chronicles]]. [src:The-Hork-Bajir-Chronicles]
- [[people/Aldrea]] and [[people/Dak-Hamee]] tried to save their world from the Yeerk invasion. [src:The-Hork-Bajir-Chronicles]
