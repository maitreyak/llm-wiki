# Management consulting

> The practice of helping organizations improve their performance through analysis of organizational problems and development of improvement plans.

## Key Facts

- Management consulting is the practice of helping organizations to improve their performance. [src:Management-consulting]
- Management consulting operates primarily through the analysis of existing organizational problems and the development of plans for improvement. [src:Management-consulting]
- Organizations may draw upon the services of [[people/Management-consultant]] for a number of reasons. [src:Management-consulting]
- Reasons for hiring management consultants include gaining external (and presumably objective) advice and access to the consultants' specialized expertise. [src:Management-consulting]
