# Comer School Development Program

> School improvement program founded in 1968 by James P. Comer to raise scholastic performance of lower-income and minority children.

## Key Facts

- The Comer School Development Program was founded in 1968 as a result of [[people/James-P-Comer]]'s efforts to improve the scholastic performance of children from lower-income and minority backgrounds. [src:James-P-Comer]
- The program has been used in more than 600 schools in eighty-two school districts. [src:James-P-Comer]
