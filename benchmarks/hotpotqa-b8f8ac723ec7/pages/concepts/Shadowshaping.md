# Shadowshaping

> Fictional magical practice in the novel Shadowshaper in which practitioners infuse art with ancestral spirits.

## Key Facts

- A shadowshaper is a person who infuses art with ancestral spirits, as depicted in [[works/Shadowshaper]]. [src:Shadowshaper]
- In the novel, a mysterious being appropriates shadowshaper magic and begins hunting the aging shadowshapers. [src:Shadowshaper]
