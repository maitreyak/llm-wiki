# Bebop

> Jazz style based on musical improvisation, ushered in during the 1940s by Charlie Parker.

## Key Facts

- Bebop is a jazz style characterized by musical improvisation, contrasted with the structured big band style. [src:Kansas-City-jazz]
- [[concepts/Kansas-City-jazz]] marked the transition from the structured big band style to the musical improvisation style of Bebop. [src:Kansas-City-jazz]
- [[people/Charlie-Parker]] was to usher in the Bebop style in the 1940s. [src:Kansas-City-jazz]
