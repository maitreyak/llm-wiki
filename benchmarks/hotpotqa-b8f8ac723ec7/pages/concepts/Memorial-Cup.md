# Memorial Cup

> The annual championship competition that determines the major junior ice hockey champion of the Canadian Hockey League, contested by the WHL, OHL and QMJHL champions plus a host team.

## Key Facts

- The Memorial Cup competition determines the major junior ice hockey champion of the [[organizations/Canadian-Hockey-League]]. [src:2007-Memorial-Cup]
- The [[events/2007-Memorial-Cup]] was the 89th annual Memorial Cup competition. [src:2007-Memorial-Cup]
- The tournament is a four-team event played in a round-robin format, involving the champions of the [[organizations/Western-Hockey-League]], [[organizations/Ontario-Hockey-League]] and [[organizations/Quebec-Major-Junior-Hockey-League]] plus a host team. [src:2007-Memorial-Cup]
- The 2007 final between two WHL teams was only the second all-WHL final in Memorial Cup history, after 1989. [src:2007-Memorial-Cup]
- The Memorial Cup attendance record of 84,686 set at the 2003 tournament in Quebec City was broken in 2007 with 121,561 fans. [src:2007-Memorial-Cup]
