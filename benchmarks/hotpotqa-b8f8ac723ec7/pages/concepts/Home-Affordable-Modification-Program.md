# Home Affordable Modification Program

> HAMP, referred to in the passage as HAMP/HMAP and staffed by 'HAMP TEAMS', is described as a modification program introduced in 2009 to reduce mortgages and legalize loan terms for note holders.

## Key Facts

- It was in 2009 that HARP, HAMP and modifications were introduced to stop the victimization of note holders. [src:Loan-modification-in-the-United-States]
