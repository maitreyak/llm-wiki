# Lord High Treasurer

> An English, and since 1707 British, government post whose holder was the third-highest-ranked Great Officer of State.

## Key Facts

- The post of Lord High Treasurer, also called Lord Treasurer, was an English government position. [src:Lord-High-Treasurer]
- The post has been a British government position since the [[events/Acts-of-Union-1707]]. [src:Lord-High-Treasurer]
- A holder of the post would be the third-highest-ranked [[concepts/Great-Officer-of-State]]. [src:Lord-High-Treasurer]
- The Lord High Treasurer ranked below the [[concepts/Lord-High-Steward]] and the [[concepts/Lord-High-Chancellor]]. [src:Lord-High-Treasurer]
