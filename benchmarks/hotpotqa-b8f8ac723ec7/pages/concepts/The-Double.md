# The Double

> In English football, winning both the league title and the FA Cup in the same season; Manchester United became the first English team to achieve it twice in 1995–96.

## Key Facts

- The Double consists of winning the league title and the [[events/FA-Cup]] in the same season. [src:1995-96-Manchester-United-F-C-season]
- [[organizations/Manchester-United-F-C]] became the first English team to win the Double twice, completing it in [[events/1995-96-Manchester-United-F-C-season]]. [src:1995-96-Manchester-United-F-C-season]
