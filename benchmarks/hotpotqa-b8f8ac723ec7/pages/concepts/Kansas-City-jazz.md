# Kansas City jazz

> Style of jazz that developed in the Kansas City area during the 1930s, marking the transition from big band to Bebop.

## Key Facts

- Kansas City jazz is a style of jazz that developed in [[places/Kansas-City-Kansas]] and [[places/Kansas-City-Missouri]] and the surrounding [[places/Kansas-City-metropolitan-area]] during the 1930s. [src:Kansas-City-jazz]
- Kansas City jazz marked the transition from the structured big band style to the musical improvisation style of [[concepts/Bebop]]. [src:Kansas-City-jazz]
- The style is described as a hard-swinging, bluesy transition style. [src:Kansas-City-jazz]
- The Kansas City jazz transition style is bracketed by [[people/Count-Basie]], who in 1929 signed with [[organizations/Bennie-Motens-Kansas-City-Orchestra]], and Kansas City native [[people/Charlie-Parker]], who was to usher in the Bebop style in the 1940s. [src:Kansas-City-jazz]
- Kansas City is known as one of the most popular "cradles of jazz", along with [[places/New-Orleans]], [[places/Chicago]], [[places/St-Louis-Missouri]], [[places/Pittsburgh]], [[places/Philadelphia]], and [[places/New-York-City]]. [src:Kansas-City-jazz]
