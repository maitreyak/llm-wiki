# United States Assistant Secretary of State

> Title used for many executive positions in the United States Department of State, ranking below the Under Secretaries and usually managing individual bureaus.

## Key Facts

- Assistant Secretary of State (A/S) is a title used for many executive positions in the [[organizations/United-States-Department-of-State]], ranking below the Under Secretaries. [src:United-States-Assistant-Secretary-of-State]
- A set of six Assistant Secretaries reporting to the Under Secretary for Political Affairs manage diplomatic missions within their designated geographic regions. [src:United-States-Assistant-Secretary-of-State]
- One additional Assistant Secretary deals with international organizations. [src:United-States-Assistant-Secretary-of-State]
- When the manager of a bureau or another agency holds a title other than Assistant Secretary, such as "Director," the position can be said to be of "Assistant Secretary equivalent rank." [src:United-States-Assistant-Secretary-of-State]
- Assistant Secretaries typically have a set of deputies referred to as Deputy Assistant Secretaries (DAS). [src:United-States-Assistant-Secretary-of-State]
