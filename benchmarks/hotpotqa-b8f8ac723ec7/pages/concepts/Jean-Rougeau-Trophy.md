# Jean Rougeau Trophy

> The QMJHL award given to the team finishing first overall in the regular season; it was won for the first time by the Lewiston Maineiacs in 2006–07.

## Key Facts

- The Jean Rougeau Trophy is awarded in the [[organizations/Quebec-Major-Junior-Hockey-League]] to the team finishing first overall in the regular season. [src:2006-07-QMJHL-season]
- The [[organizations/Lewiston-Maineiacs]] won their first Jean Rougeau Trophy in [[events/2006-07-QMJHL-season]]. [src:2006-07-QMJHL-season]
