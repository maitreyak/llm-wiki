# Real Estate Technology

> An economic industry of companies that use technology to make real estate transactions more efficient, considered a sub-category of financial technology.

## Key Facts

- Real Estate Technology, also called Property Technology, is often referred to as "RE Tech" or "Proptech". [src:Real-estate-technology]
- Real Estate Technology is an economic industry composed of companies which use technology to make real estate transactions more efficient. [src:Real-estate-technology]
- Real estate technology is a sub-category under [[concepts/Financial-Technology]], as real estate is an asset class which represents a significant investment. [src:Real-estate-technology]
