# Amawtica Amarukiana Desestructuration

> Philosophy developed by Amaruk Kayshapanta that brings the philosophical study of the Andean Worldview to the contemporary world as a spiritual legacy of transformation and balance for the awakening of a collective conscience.

## Key Facts

- "Amawtica Amarukiana Desestructuration" is the philosophy of [[people/Amaruk-Kayshapanta]]. [src:Amaruk-Kayshapanta]
- The philosophy brings to the contemporary world the philosophical study of the Andean Worldview. [src:Amaruk-Kayshapanta]
- It is described as a spiritual legacy of transformation and balance for the awakening of a collective conscience. [src:Amaruk-Kayshapanta]
