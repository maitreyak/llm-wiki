# First Secretary of State

> A position within the British government, held by the Deputy Prime Minister, from which functions were transferred to the Secretary of State for Constitutional Affairs.

## Key Facts

- The First Secretary of State is a position within the British government held by the Deputy Prime Minister. [src:Secretary-of-State-for-Constitutional-Affairs]
- Certain functions were transferred from the First Secretary of State to the [[concepts/Secretary-of-State-for-Constitutional-Affairs]] after that office's creation. [src:Secretary-of-State-for-Constitutional-Affairs]
