# Balloon Mortgage

> A lending product the passage describes as designed to make fast money off unaware note holders and to induce default, and as central to the 2008 housing crash.

## Key Facts

- The passage describes balloon mortgages as a lending product designed to make fast money off the note holder, who is typically unaware of their escrow and equity. [src:Loan-modification-in-the-United-States]
- It claims balloon mortgages and loans were given with the hope and expectation that loan holders would default, creating opportunity to obtain the homeowners' funds. [src:Loan-modification-in-the-United-States]
