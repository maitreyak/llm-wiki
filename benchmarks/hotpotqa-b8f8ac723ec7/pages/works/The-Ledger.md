# The Ledger

> Crime-thriller novel by Dorothy Uhnak that served as the basis for Get Christie Love!.

## Key Facts

- The Ledger is a crime-thriller novel by [[people/Dorothy-Uhnak]]. [src:Get-Christie-Love]
- The Ledger is the basis for the 1974 film and series [[works/Get-Christie-Love]]. [src:Get-Christie-Love]
- The novel's main character was "Christie Opara", a white [[places/New-York-City]] police detective, who was dropped completely in the adaptation. [src:Get-Christie-Love]
