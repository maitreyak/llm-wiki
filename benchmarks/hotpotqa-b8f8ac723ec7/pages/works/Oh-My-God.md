# Oh My God

> 1999 Guns N' Roses song released on the End of Days soundtrack and promoted to radio, but never issued as a retail single.

## Key Facts

- "Oh My God" is a song by [[organizations/Guns-N-Roses]] released in 1999. [src:Oh-My-God-Guns-N-Roses-song]
- The song appeared on the soundtrack to the film [[works/End-of-Days]]. [src:Oh-My-God-Guns-N-Roses-song]
- "Oh My God" was sent out to radio stations in November 1999 as a promo for the soundtrack and the band. [src:Oh-My-God-Guns-N-Roses-song]
- The song was the band's first recorded release in almost five years. [src:Oh-My-God-Guns-N-Roses-song]
- "Oh My God" was never issued as a stand-alone single for public retail. [src:Oh-My-God-Guns-N-Roses-song]
