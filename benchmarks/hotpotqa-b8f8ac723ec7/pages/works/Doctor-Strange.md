# Doctor Strange

> 2016 American superhero film based on the Marvel Comics character, the fourteenth film of the Marvel Cinematic Universe, directed by Scott Derrickson and starring Benedict Cumberbatch.

## Key Facts

- Doctor Strange is a 2016 American superhero film based on the Marvel Comics character of the same name. [src:Doctor-Strange-2016-film]
- Doctor Strange was produced by [[organizations/Marvel-Studios]] and distributed by [[organizations/Walt-Disney-Studios-Motion-Pictures]]. [src:Doctor-Strange-2016-film]
- Doctor Strange is the fourteenth film of the [[concepts/Marvel-Cinematic-Universe]]. [src:Doctor-Strange-2016-film]
- The film was directed by [[people/Scott-Derrickson]], who wrote it with [[people/Jon-Spaihts]] and [[people/C-Robert-Cargill]]. [src:Doctor-Strange-2016-film]
- [[people/Benedict-Cumberbatch]] stars as Stephen Strange. [src:Doctor-Strange-2016-film]
- The film also stars [[people/Chiwetel-Ejiofor]], [[people/Rachel-McAdams]], [[people/Benedict-Wong]], [[people/Michael-Stuhlbarg]], [[people/Benjamin-Bratt]], [[people/Scott-Adkins]], [[people/Mads-Mikkelsen]], and [[people/Tilda-Swinton]]. [src:Doctor-Strange-2016-film]
- In the film, surgeon Strange learns the mystic arts after a career-ending car accident. [src:Doctor-Strange-2016-film]
