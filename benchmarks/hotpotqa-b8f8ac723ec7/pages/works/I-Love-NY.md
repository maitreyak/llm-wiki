# I Love NY

> 2015 Indian romantic comedy film directed by Radhika Rao and Vinay Sapru, starring Sunny Deol and Kangana Ranaut.

## Key Facts

- I Love NY, also known as I Love New Year, is an Indian romantic comedy film. [src:I-Love-NY-2015-film]
- I Love NY was directed by [[people/Radhika-Rao]] and [[people/Vinay-Sapru]]. [src:I-Love-NY-2015-film]
- I Love NY stars [[people/Sunny-Deol]] and [[people/Kangana-Ranaut]] in lead roles. [src:I-Love-NY-2015-film]
- The film was produced by [[people/Bhushan-Kumar]] and [[people/Krishan-Kumar]] under the banner of [[organizations/Super-Cassettes-Industries]]. [src:I-Love-NY-2015-film]
- I Love NY was extensively shot in [[places/Mumbai]], [[places/New-York-City]] and [[places/Bangkok]]. [src:I-Love-NY-2015-film]
- The main plot of I Love NY was taken from the Russian romantic comedy [[works/The-Irony-of-Fate]] (1976). [src:I-Love-NY-2015-film]
- After numerous delays, I Love NY was released on 10 July 2015. [src:I-Love-NY-2015-film]
