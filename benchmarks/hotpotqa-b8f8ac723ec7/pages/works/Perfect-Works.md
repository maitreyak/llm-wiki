# Perfect Works

> The first series of Square companion books, written and published by Square subsidiary DigiCube, comprising three books released between 1998 and 1999.

## Key Facts

- Perfect Works was the first series of [[organizations/Square]] companion books. [src:List-of-Square-Enix-companion-books]
- Three Perfect Works books were produced between 1998 and 1999. [src:List-of-Square-Enix-companion-books]
- The Perfect Works line was stopped in favor of the [[works/Ultimania]] series. [src:List-of-Square-Enix-companion-books]
