# Shawty What You Doin'

> 2002 single by rapper Lil Ru, the follow-up to "Will Destroy".

## Key Facts

- "Shawty What You Doin'" was released in 2002 as the follow-up single by [[people/Lil-Ru]]. [src:Lil-Ru]
- "Shawty What You Doin'" reached the Billboard R&B/Hip-Hop charts. [src:Lil-Ru]
- Along with [[works/Will-Destroy]], it helped land Lil Ru a deal with [[organizations/Capitol-Records]]. [src:Lil-Ru]
