# Kingston Morning

> Dave Eggar's fourth solo release, recorded in Brooklyn, Kingston, and Big Stone Gap and issued by Domo Records.

## Key Facts

- Kingston Morning is [[people/Dave-Eggar]]'s 4th solo release. [src:Kingston-Morning]
- Kingston Morning was recorded in Brooklyn, [[places/New-York-City]]; [[places/Kingston-Jamaica]]; and [[places/Big-Stone-Gap]]. [src:Kingston-Morning]
- Kingston Morning was released by [[organizations/Domo-Records]]. [src:Kingston-Morning]
- The track [[works/Itsbynne-Reel]] from the release was nominated at the [[events/53rd-Grammy-Awards]] for "Best Instrumental Arrangement". [src:Kingston-Morning]
