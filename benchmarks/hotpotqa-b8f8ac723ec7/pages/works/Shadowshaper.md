# Shadowshaper

> 2015 American urban fantasy young adult novel by Daniel José Older about a Brooklyn teenager who discovers her family's magical artistic heritage.

## Key Facts

- Shadowshaper is a 2015 American urban fantasy young adult novel written by [[people/Daniel-Jose-Older]]. [src:Shadowshaper]
- The novel follows [[people/Sierra-Santiago]], an Afro-Boricua teenager living in [[places/Brooklyn]]. [src:Shadowshaper]
- Sierra Santiago is the granddaughter of a "shadowshaper", a person who infuses art with ancestral spirits. [src:Shadowshaper]
- In the story, forces of gentrification invade the community while a mysterious being who appropriates shadowshaper magic begins hunting the aging shadowshapers. [src:Shadowshaper]
- Sierra must learn about her artistic and spiritual heritage in order to foil the killer. [src:Shadowshaper]
