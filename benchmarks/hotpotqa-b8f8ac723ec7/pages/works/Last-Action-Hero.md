# Last Action Hero

> 1993 American fantasy action comedy film directed and produced by John McTiernan, starring Arnold Schwarzenegger as Jack Slater.

## Key Facts

- Last Action Hero is a 1993 American fantasy action comedy film. [src:Last-Action-Hero]
- Last Action Hero was directed and produced by [[people/John-McTiernan]]. [src:Last-Action-Hero]
- The film is a satire of the action genre and its associated clichés, containing several parodies of action films in the form of films within the film. [src:Last-Action-Hero]
- The film stars [[people/Arnold-Schwarzenegger]] as [[concepts/Jack-Slater]], a Los Angeles police detective within the "Jack Slater" action film franchise. [src:Last-Action-Hero]
- [[people/Austin-OBrien]] co-stars as a boy magically transported into the "Slater" universe. [src:Last-Action-Hero]
- [[people/Arnold-Schwarzenegger]] served as the film's executive producer and plays himself as the actor portraying Jack Slater. [src:Last-Action-Hero]
- [[people/Charles-Dance]] plays an assassin who escapes from the "Slater" world into the real world. [src:Last-Action-Hero]
