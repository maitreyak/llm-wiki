# Get Christie Love!

> 1974 made-for-television film and subsequent crime drama TV series starring Teresa Graves as an undercover police detective.

## Key Facts

- Get Christie Love! is a 1974 made-for-television film and subsequent crime drama TV series. [src:Get-Christie-Love]
- Get Christie Love! stars [[people/Teresa-Graves]] as an undercover female police detective determined to overthrow a drug ring. [src:Get-Christie-Love]
- Get Christie Love! is based on [[people/Dorothy-Uhnak]]'s crime-thriller novel [[works/The-Ledger]]. [src:Get-Christie-Love]
- In adapting the story, the novel's main character "Christie Opara"—a white, [[places/New-York-City]] police detective—was dropped completely and the character "Christie Love" emerged. [src:Get-Christie-Love]
