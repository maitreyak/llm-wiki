# Sinister

> Sinister is a 2012 supernatural horror film directed by Scott Derrickson and starring Ethan Hawke.

## Key Facts

- Sinister is a horror film directed by [[people/Scott-Derrickson]]. [src:Scott-Derrickson]
- Sinister is among the horror films for which Scott Derrickson is best known. [src:Scott-Derrickson]
- Sinister is a 2012 supernatural horror film. [src:Sinister-film]
- Sinister was written by [[people/Scott-Derrickson]] and [[people/C-Robert-Cargill]]. [src:Sinister-film]
- Sinister stars [[people/Ethan-Hawke]] as fictional true-crime writer [[people/Ellison-Oswalt]]. [src:Sinister-film]
- In Sinister, Ellison Oswalt discovers a box of home movies in his attic that puts his family in danger. [src:Sinister-film]
