# Criss Angel BeLIEve

> Television series on Spike TV starring Criss Angel.

## Key Facts

- "Criss Angel BeLIEve" is a television series starring [[people/Criss-Angel]]. [src:Criss-Angel]
- The series aired on [[organizations/Spike-TV]]. [src:Criss-Angel]
- "Criss Angel Believe" was a live performance illusion show by [[people/Criss-Angel]] in collaboration with [[organizations/Cirque-du-Soleil]]. [src:Criss-Angel]
- The show was staged at the [[places/Luxor]] casino in [[places/Las-Vegas-Valley]]. [src:Criss-Angel]
- "Criss Angel Believe" generated $150 million in tourist revenue to Las Vegas in 2010. [src:Criss-Angel]
- The show was replaced by [[works/Mindfreak-LIVE]] on 11 May 2016. [src:Criss-Angel]
