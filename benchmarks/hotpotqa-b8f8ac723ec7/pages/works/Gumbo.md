# Gumbo

> 2017 studio album by PJ Morton, his fourth overall and first self-released, issued on Morton Records.

## Key Facts

- Gumbo is the fourth and first self-released studio album by American singer-songwriter [[people/PJ-Morton]]. [src:Gumbo-PJ-Morton-album]
- Gumbo was released on April 14, 2017 by [[organizations/Morton-Records]]. [src:Gumbo-PJ-Morton-album]
- Gumbo was the follow-up to PJ Morton's third studio album New Orleans (2013). [src:Gumbo-PJ-Morton-album]
- The record incorporates R&B styles with elements of older soul music. [src:Gumbo-PJ-Morton-album]
- The album's lyrics discuss themes of romance and explore political and personal themes. [src:Gumbo-PJ-Morton-album]
- The album is entirely produced by PJ Morton himself. [src:Gumbo-PJ-Morton-album]
- The album features a cover of the [[organizations/Bee-Gees]]' song "How Deep Is Your Love". [src:Gumbo-PJ-Morton-album]
