# List of major opera composers

> A reference list of opera composers judged major by their presence on a majority of compiled lists of significant opera composers, organised by birthdate.

## Key Facts

- The list provides a guide to opera composers as determined by their presence on a majority of compiled lists of significant opera composers. [src:List-of-major-opera-composers]
- Full details of the sources used are given in the list's "Lists Consulted" section. [src:List-of-major-opera-composers]
- The composers range from [[people/Jacopo-Peri]], who wrote the first ever opera in late 16th century Italy, to [[people/John-Adams]], one of the leading figures in the contemporary operatic world. [src:List-of-major-opera-composers]
- Brief accompanying notes explain why each composer has been considered major. [src:List-of-major-opera-composers]
- The list includes a section about major women opera composers, compiled from the same lists. [src:List-of-major-opera-composers]
- The list is organised by birthdate. [src:List-of-major-opera-composers]
