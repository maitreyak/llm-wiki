# S

> Debut studio album by South Korean group WINNER, released August 12, 2014 by YG Entertainment, with the members credited for most of the writing and composition.

## Key Facts

- 2014 S/S is the debut album of South Korean group [[organizations/WINNER]]. [src:2014-S-S]
- 2014 S/S was released on August 12, 2014. [src:2014-S-S]
- The album was released by the group's record label, [[organizations/YG-Entertainment]]. [src:2014-S-S]
- The members of WINNER were credited for writing the lyrics and composing the majority of the album's songs. [src:2014-S-S]
