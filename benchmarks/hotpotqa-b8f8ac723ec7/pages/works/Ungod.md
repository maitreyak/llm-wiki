# Ungod

> The 1994 debut album by American industrial rock band Stabbing Westward, released on Columbia Records.

## Key Facts

- Ungod is the debut album of the American industrial rock band [[organizations/Stabbing-Westward]], released in 1994. [src:Ungod]
- The album was released on [[organizations/Columbia-Records]]. [src:Ungod]
- Ungod was recorded in six weeks of 1993 in Chiswick, England. [src:Ungod]
- Ungod was released on February 15, 1994. [src:Ungod]
- Adequate album sales and touring with acts such as [[organizations/Depeche-Mode]] warranted a second album, [[works/Wither-Blister-Burn-Peel]]. [src:Ungod]
- The guitar line in the chorus of the album's title track "Ungod" was later used in [[organizations/Filter]]'s song "Hey Man, Nice Shot". [src:Ungod]
