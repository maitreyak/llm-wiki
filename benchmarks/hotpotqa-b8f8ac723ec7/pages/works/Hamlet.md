# Hamlet

> An 1868 opera by Ambroise Thomas, based on Shakespeare's Hamlet.

## Key Facts

- Hamlet is an opera composed by [[people/Ambroise-Thomas]] in 1868. [src:Ambroise-Thomas]
- The opera Hamlet was written after Shakespeare. [src:Ambroise-Thomas]
- Hamlet is one of the two operas for which Ambroise Thomas is best known. [src:Ambroise-Thomas]
- Hamlet premiered at the [[organizations/Paris-Opera]] in 1868. [src:Armand-Castelmary]
- [[people/Armand-Castelmary]] created the role of Horatio in the 1868 premiere. [src:Armand-Castelmary]
