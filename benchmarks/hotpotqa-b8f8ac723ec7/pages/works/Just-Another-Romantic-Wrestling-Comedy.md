# Just Another Romantic Wrestling Comedy

> 2006 romantic comedy film starring April Hunter and Joanie Laurer, released on DVD in 2007 by Victory Multimedia.

## Key Facts

- Just Another Romantic Wrestling Comedy is a 2006 film starring [[people/April-Hunter]] and [[people/Joanie-Laurer]]. [src:Just-Another-Romantic-Wrestling-Comedy]
- The romantic comedy film premiered at [[places/New-Jersey]] and [[places/New-York-City]] on December 1, 2006. [src:Just-Another-Romantic-Wrestling-Comedy]
- The film was released on DVD in the United States and the United Kingdom on April 17, 2007. [src:Just-Another-Romantic-Wrestling-Comedy]
- After its DVD release, the film won an "Honorable Mention" award at the New Jersey International Festival awards. [src:Just-Another-Romantic-Wrestling-Comedy]
- The film's release is handled by [[organizations/Victory-Multimedia]]. [src:Just-Another-Romantic-Wrestling-Comedy]
