# Piano Girl

> 2009 Turkish comedy-drama film directed by Murat Saraçoğlu, starring Tarık Akan and Şerif Sezer.

## Key Facts

- Piano Girl (Turkish: "Deli Deli Olma") is a 2009 Turkish comedy-drama film. [src:Piano-Girl]
- The film was directed by [[people/Murat-Saracoglu]]. [src:Piano-Girl]
- It stars [[people/Tar-k-Akan]] and [[people/Serif-Sezer]] as two elderly people forced to question their histories and reveal their big secrets. [src:Piano-Girl]
- The film went on nationwide general release across Turkey in 2009. [src:Piano-Girl]
- It was the opening film at the [[events/Sinema-Burada-Film-Festival]] in İzmir, Turkey. [src:Piano-Girl]
- It has been screened in competition at a number of film festivals, including the 46th [[events/Antalya-Golden-Orange-Film-Festival]]. [src:Piano-Girl]
- According to [[people/Terry-Richardson]], writing for [[organizations/Todays-Zaman]], at the Antalya festival "the rapt audience gave it a standing ovation." [src:Piano-Girl]
