# Appetite for Destruction

> Debut studio album by Guns N' Roses, released in 1987 and a major commercial success.

## Key Facts

- Appetite for Destruction is the debut studio album of [[organizations/Guns-N-Roses]], released in 1987. [src:Guns-N-Roses-discography]
- The album reached the top of the Billboard 200. [src:Guns-N-Roses-discography]
- It sold 18 million units in the United States and approximately 33 million units worldwide. [src:Guns-N-Roses-discography]
