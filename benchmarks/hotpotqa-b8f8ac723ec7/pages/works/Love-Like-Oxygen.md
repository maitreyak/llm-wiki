# Love Like Oxygen

> First single from Shinee's debut album "The Shinee World", which gave the group their first Korean music show win.

## Key Facts

- The single won first place on [[media/M-Countdown]] on September 18, 2008, making it the group's first win on Korean music shows since debut. [src:List-of-awards-and-nominations-received-by-Shinee]
