# True Lies

> 1994 American action film written, co-produced and directed by James Cameron, starring Arnold Schwarzenegger as spy Harry Tasker.

## Key Facts

- True Lies is a 1994 American action film. [src:True-Lies]
- True Lies was written, co-produced and directed by [[people/James-Cameron]]. [src:True-Lies]
- The film stars [[people/Arnold-Schwarzenegger]], [[people/Jamie-Lee-Curtis]], [[people/Tom-Arnold]], [[people/Art-Malik]], [[people/Tia-Carrere]], [[people/Bill-Paxton]], [[people/Eliza-Dushku]], [[people/Grant-Heslov]] and [[people/Charlton-Heston]]. [src:True-Lies]
- True Lies is a loose remake of the 1991 French comedy film [[works/La-Totale]]. [src:True-Lies]
- The film follows U.S. government agent [[concepts/Harry-Tasker]], played by [[people/Arnold-Schwarzenegger]], who balances his life as a spy with his familial duties. [src:True-Lies]
