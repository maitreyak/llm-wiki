# List of Square Enix companion books

> A body of dozens of companion books produced since 1998 by Square and later Square Enix, focusing on artwork, developer interviews, and background on the fictional worlds and characters of its games.

## Key Facts

- Dozens of Square Enix companion books have been produced since 1998. [src:List-of-Square-Enix-companion-books]
- In 1998, video game developer [[organizations/Square]] began producing books focused on artwork, developer interviews, and background information on the fictional worlds and characters in its games rather than on gameplay details. [src:List-of-Square-Enix-companion-books]
- The first series of these companion books was the [[works/Perfect-Works]] series. [src:List-of-Square-Enix-companion-books]
- The [[works/Perfect-Works]] line was stopped in favor of the [[works/Ultimania]] series. [src:List-of-Square-Enix-companion-books]
- The companion books were published by [[organizations/DigiCube]] until the company was dissolved in 2003. [src:List-of-Square-Enix-companion-books]
- [[organizations/Square-Enix]] resumed publication of the companion books after the 2003 merger. [src:List-of-Square-Enix-companion-books]
