# Lady Gaga x Terry Richardson

> 2011 photo-book by Lady Gaga and Terry Richardson featuring more than 350 photographs of the singer.

## Key Facts

- Lady Gaga x Terry Richardson is a photo-book by American singer [[people/Lady-Gaga]] and American photographer [[people/Terry-Richardson]]. [src:Lady-Gaga-x-Terry-Richardson]
- The book was released on November 22, 2011 by Grand Central Publishing. [src:Lady-Gaga-x-Terry-Richardson]
- The book features more than 350 pictures of Gaga taken by Richardson during a ten-month period, from Gaga's performance at [[events/The-Monster-Ball-Tour]] until the 2011 Grammy Awards. [src:Lady-Gaga-x-Terry-Richardson]
- In addition to photographs, the book includes a foreword written by Lady Gaga about her relationship with Richardson. [src:Lady-Gaga-x-Terry-Richardson]
