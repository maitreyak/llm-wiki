# A Kiss for Corliss

> A 1949 American comedy film directed by Richard Wallace and written by Howard Dimsdale, starring Shirley Temple in her final starring role and final film appearance. A sequel to the 1945 film Kiss and Tell, it was retitled Almost a Bride before release and distributed by United Artists.

## Key Facts

- A Kiss for Corliss is a 1949 American comedy film. [src:A-Kiss-for-Corliss]
- The film was directed by [[people/Richard-Wallace]]. [src:A-Kiss-for-Corliss]
- The film was written by [[people/Howard-Dimsdale]]. [src:A-Kiss-for-Corliss]
- It stars [[people/Shirley-Temple]] in her final starring role as well as her final film appearance. [src:A-Kiss-for-Corliss]
- It is a sequel to the 1945 film [[works/Kiss-and-Tell]]. [src:A-Kiss-for-Corliss]
- A Kiss for Corliss was retitled Almost a Bride before release, and this title appears in the title sequence. [src:A-Kiss-for-Corliss]
- The film was released on November 25, 1949, by [[organizations/United-Artists]]. [src:A-Kiss-for-Corliss]
