# Megamorphs

> Animorphs books known for alternating points of view, a style shared by The Hork-Bajir Chronicles.

## Key Facts

- The Megamorphs books alternate points of view among characters. [src:The-Hork-Bajir-Chronicles]
- Jara Hamee's story in [[works/The-Hork-Bajir-Chronicles]] alternates narrators in similar fashion to the Megamorphs books. [src:The-Hork-Bajir-Chronicles]
- Megamorphs books are associated with the [[works/Animorphs]] series. [src:The-Hork-Bajir-Chronicles]
