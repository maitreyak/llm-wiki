# Lucifer

> Shinee's second album, released in 2010, which produced the singles "Lucifer" and "Hello".

## Key Facts

- Lucifer won the Disk Bonsang Award at the 25th [[events/Golden-Disk-Awards]] as well as the Popularity Award. [src:List-of-awards-and-nominations-received-by-Shinee]
- For their outstanding choreography, Shinee was nominated for the Best Dance Performance Award at the 2010 [[events/Mnet-Asian-Music-Awards]]. [src:List-of-awards-and-nominations-received-by-Shinee]
