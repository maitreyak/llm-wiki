# Skool Luv Affair

> BTS's second extended play, released in February 2014, their first album to chart internationally.

## Key Facts

- "Skool Luv Affair" is the second extended play by [[organizations/BTS]], released in February 2014. [src:BTS-discography]
- "Skool Luv Affair" charted at number 1 on the Week 18 [[concepts/Gaon-Weekly-Chart]]. [src:BTS-discography]
- "Skool Luv Affair" was the first BTS album to chart on international charts, namely [[concepts/Billboard-World-Albums]] and Japan's [[concepts/Oricon-Chart]]. [src:BTS-discography]
- A repackaged version of the album, [[works/Skool-Luv-Affair-Special-Addition]], was released in May 2014. [src:BTS-discography]
