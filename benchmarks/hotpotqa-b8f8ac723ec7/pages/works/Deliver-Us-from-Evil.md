# Deliver Us from Evil

> 2014 American supernatural horror film directed by Scott Derrickson, produced by Jerry Bruckheimer, based on the book "Beware the Night".

## Key Facts

- Deliver Us from Evil is a 2014 American supernatural horror film. [src:Deliver-Us-from-Evil-2014-film]
- Deliver Us from Evil was directed by [[people/Scott-Derrickson]]. [src:Deliver-Us-from-Evil-2014-film]
- Deliver Us from Evil was produced by [[people/Jerry-Bruckheimer]]. [src:Deliver-Us-from-Evil-2014-film]
- The film is officially based on the 2001 non-fiction book [[works/Beware-the-Night]] by [[people/Ralph-Sarchie]] and [[people/Lisa-Collier-Cool]]. [src:Deliver-Us-from-Evil-2014-film]
- The film's marketing campaign highlighted that it was "inspired by actual accounts". [src:Deliver-Us-from-Evil-2014-film]
- The film stars [[people/Eric-Bana]], [[people/Edgar-Ramirez]], [[people/Sean-Harris]], [[people/Olivia-Munn]], and [[people/Joel-McHale]] in the main roles. [src:Deliver-Us-from-Evil-2014-film]
- Deliver Us from Evil was released on July 2, 2014. [src:Deliver-Us-from-Evil-2014-film]
