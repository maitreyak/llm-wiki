# Hallelujah! I'm a Bum

> The seventh studio album by Chicago rock band Local H, a concept album about United States politics released in September 2012.

## Key Facts

- Hallelujah! I'm A Bum is the seventh studio album by the Chicago-based rock band [[organizations/Local-H]]. [src:Hallelujah-Im-a-Bum-album]
- Interviews with the band revealed that Hallelujah! I'm A Bum is a concept album based on politics in the United States. [src:Hallelujah-Im-a-Bum-album]
- The album was completed in spring of 2012, but the band chose to release it in September to be closer to the [[events/2012-United-States-presidential-election]]. [src:Hallelujah-Im-a-Bum-album]
- The album makes frequent use of the sounds of the El Train in [[places/Chicago]] to transition songs. [src:Hallelujah-Im-a-Bum-album]
