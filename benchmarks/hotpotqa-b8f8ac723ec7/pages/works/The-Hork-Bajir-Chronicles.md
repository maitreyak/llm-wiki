# The Hork-Bajir Chronicles

> The second companion book to the Animorphs series by K. A. Applegate, telling the story of the Yeerk enslavement of the Hork-Bajir.

## Key Facts

- The Hork-Bajir Chronicles is the second companion book to the [[works/Animorphs]] series. [src:The-Hork-Bajir-Chronicles]
- The Hork-Bajir Chronicles was written by [[people/K-A-Applegate]]. [src:The-Hork-Bajir-Chronicles]
- With respect to continuity within the series, The Hork-Bajir Chronicles takes place before book #23, [[works/The-Pretender]]. [src:The-Hork-Bajir-Chronicles]
- The events told in The Hork-Bajir Chronicles occur between the time of [[works/The-Ellimist-Chronicles]] and [[works/The-Andalite-Chronicles]]. [src:The-Hork-Bajir-Chronicles]
- The book is introduced by [[people/Tobias]], who flies to the valley of the free [[concepts/Hork-Bajir]]. [src:The-Hork-Bajir-Chronicles]
- In the valley, [[people/Jara-Hamee]] tells Tobias the story of how the [[concepts/Yeerks]] enslaved the Hork-Bajir. [src:The-Hork-Bajir-Chronicles]
- The story recounts how [[people/Aldrea]], an [[concepts/Andalite]], and her companion [[people/Dak-Hamee]], a Hork-Bajir, tried to save their world from the invasion. [src:The-Hork-Bajir-Chronicles]
- Jara Hamee's story is narrated from the points of view of Aldrea, Dak Hamee, and [[people/Esplin-9466]], alternating in similar fashion to the [[works/Megamorphs]] books. [src:The-Hork-Bajir-Chronicles]
