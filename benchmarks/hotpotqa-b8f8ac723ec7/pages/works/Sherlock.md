# Sherlock

> Shinee's fourth EP, released on March 21, 2012, which won Bonsang awards and earned a Song of the Year nomination.

## Key Facts

- For Sherlock, Shinee was awarded another Disk Bonsang Award at the 27th [[events/Golden-Disk-Awards]]. [src:List-of-awards-and-nominations-received-by-Shinee]
- Sherlock also earned the Bonsang Award at the 22nd [[events/Seoul-Music-Awards]]. [src:List-of-awards-and-nominations-received-by-Shinee]
- Following the success of its lead single, it was nominated for Song of the Year at the 2012 [[events/Mnet-Asian-Music-Awards]]. [src:List-of-awards-and-nominations-received-by-Shinee]
