# The Divide trilogy

> Fantasy young adult novel trilogy by Elizabeth Kay set in an alternate universe.

## Key Facts

- The Divide trilogy is a fantasy young adult novel trilogy by [[people/Elizabeth-Kay]] that takes place in an alternate universe. [src:The-Divide-trilogy]
- The trilogy consists of three books: "The Divide" (2002), "Back to The Divide" (2005), and "Jinx on The Divide" (2006). [src:The-Divide-trilogy]
- The first novel was originally published by the small press publisher [[organizations/Chicken-House]], now a division of [[organizations/Scholastic]]. [src:The-Divide-trilogy]
- Subsequent volumes were published by [[organizations/Scholastic]], which also reprinted the first novel. [src:The-Divide-trilogy]
- Interior illustrations for the trilogy are by [[people/Ted-Dewan]]. [src:The-Divide-trilogy]
