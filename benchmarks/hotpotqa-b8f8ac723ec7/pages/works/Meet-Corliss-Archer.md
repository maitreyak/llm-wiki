# Meet Corliss Archer

> Golden Age radio program (January 7, 1943 – September 30, 1956) whose title character was played by Janet Waldo; it aired on CBS, NBC and ABC, was based on short stories by F. Hugh Herbert, and was also adapted as a television series.

## Key Facts

- [[people/Janet-Waldo]] is best known on radio as the title character in Meet Corliss Archer. [src:Janet-Waldo]
- Meet Corliss Archer was a program from radio's Golden Age that ran from January 7, 1943 to September 30, 1956. [src:Meet-Corliss-Archer]
- Meet Corliss Archer was [[organizations/CBS]]'s answer to [[organizations/NBC]]'s popular program [[works/A-Date-with-Judy]]. [src:Meet-Corliss-Archer]
- Meet Corliss Archer was broadcast by [[organizations/NBC]] in 1948 as a summer replacement for [[works/The-Bob-Hope-Show]]. [src:Meet-Corliss-Archer]
- From October 3, 1952 to June 26, 1953, Meet Corliss Archer aired on [[organizations/ABC]], after which it returned to [[organizations/CBS]]. [src:Meet-Corliss-Archer]
- Despite the program's long run, fewer than 24 episodes of Meet Corliss Archer are known to exist. [src:Meet-Corliss-Archer]
- The radio series was based on a series of short stories by [[people/F-Hugh-Herbert]]. [src:Meet-Corliss-Archer-TV-series]
- The radio series was adapted into the television sitcom [[works/Meet-Corliss-Archer-TV-series]], which aired on CBS in 1951 and in syndication in 1954. [src:Meet-Corliss-Archer-TV-series]
