# Left Behind: The Kids

> A 40-book young adult novel series spun off from the adult Left Behind series, following teenagers through the rapture and tribulation.

## Key Facts

- The series was written by [[people/Jerry-B-Jenkins]], [[people/Tim-LaHaye]], and [[people/Chris-Fabry]]. [src:Left-Behind-The-Kids]
- The series consists of 40 short novels aimed primarily at the young adult market. [src:Left-Behind-The-Kids]
- The series follows a core group of teenagers as they experience the rapture and tribulation, based on scriptures found in the Bible and background plots introduced in the adult novels. [src:Left-Behind-The-Kids]
- Like the adult series, the books were published by [[organizations/Tyndale-House-Publishing]]. [src:Left-Behind-The-Kids]
- The books were released over the seven-year period of 1997-2004. [src:Left-Behind-The-Kids]
- The series has sold over 11 million copies worldwide. [src:Left-Behind-The-Kids]
