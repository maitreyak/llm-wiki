# Ultimania

> A series of Japanese video game companion books whose name is a portmanteau of "ultimate" and "mania", written by Studio BentStuff for Square and later Square Enix.

## Key Facts

- The Ultimania series replaced the [[works/Perfect-Works]] series of companion books. [src:List-of-Square-Enix-companion-books]
- The series is written by [[organizations/Studio-BentStuff]]. [src:List-of-Square-Enix-companion-books]
- Ultimania books were published by [[organizations/DigiCube]] until that company was dissolved in 2003. [src:List-of-Square-Enix-companion-books]
- Publication of the companion books resumed under [[organizations/Square-Enix]] after the 2003 merger. [src:List-of-Square-Enix-companion-books]
