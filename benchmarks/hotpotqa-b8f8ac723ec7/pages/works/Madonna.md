# Madonna

> A 2001 biography of American recording artist Madonna written by English author Andrew Morton.

## Key Facts

- Madonna is a biography by English author [[people/Andrew-Morton]] chronicling the life of American recording artist [[people/Madonna]]. [src:Madonna-book]
- The book was released in November 2001 by [[organizations/St-Martins-Press]] in the United States. [src:Madonna-book]
- The book was released in April 2002 by [[organizations/Michael-OMara-Books]] in the United Kingdom. [src:Madonna-book]
- Andrew Morton decided to write a biography on Madonna in 2000. [src:Madonna-book]
- The release of the book was announced in April 2001 by St. Martin's Press. [src:Madonna-book]
- President and publisher [[people/Sally-Richardson]] described the biography as containing details about Madonna's ambitions, her relationships and her lifestyle. [src:Madonna-book]
