# Animorphs

> Book series by K. A. Applegate, accompanied by companion books including The Hork-Bajir Chronicles.

## Key Facts

- Animorphs is a series written by [[people/K-A-Applegate]]. [src:The-Hork-Bajir-Chronicles]
- [[works/The-Hork-Bajir-Chronicles]] is the second companion book to the Animorphs series. [src:The-Hork-Bajir-Chronicles]
- [[works/The-Pretender]] is book #23 of the Animorphs series. [src:The-Hork-Bajir-Chronicles]
- Other companion books to the series include [[works/The-Ellimist-Chronicles]] and [[works/The-Andalite-Chronicles]]. [src:The-Hork-Bajir-Chronicles]
- The [[works/Megamorphs]] books are associated with the series and use alternating points of view. [src:The-Hork-Bajir-Chronicles]
- Animorphs is a science fantasy series of young adult books. [src:Animorphs]
- Animorphs was published by [[organizations/Scholastic]]. [src:Animorphs]
- Animorphs is told in first person, with all six main characters taking turns narrating the books through their own perspectives. [src:Animorphs]
- Horror, war, dehumanization, sanity, morality, innocence, leadership, freedom and growing up are the core themes of the Animorphs series. [src:Animorphs]
