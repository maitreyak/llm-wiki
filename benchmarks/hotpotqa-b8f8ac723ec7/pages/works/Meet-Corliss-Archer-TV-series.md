# Meet Corliss Archer (TV series)

> American television sitcom that aired on CBS in summer 1951 and in syndication via the Ziv Company in 1954, adapted from the radio series Meet Corliss Archer.

## Key Facts

- Meet Corliss Archer was an American television sitcom. [src:Meet-Corliss-Archer-TV-series]
- The series aired on [[organizations/CBS]] from July 13, 1951 to August 10, 1951. [src:Meet-Corliss-Archer-TV-series]
- The series aired in syndication via the [[organizations/Ziv-Company]] from April to December 1954. [src:Meet-Corliss-Archer-TV-series]
- The television program was an adaptation of the radio series of the same name, [[works/Meet-Corliss-Archer]]. [src:Meet-Corliss-Archer-TV-series]
- The radio series on which the television program was based was itself based on a series of short stories by [[people/F-Hugh-Herbert]]. [src:Meet-Corliss-Archer-TV-series]
