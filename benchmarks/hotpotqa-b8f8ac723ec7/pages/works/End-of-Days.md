# End of Days

> 1999 American fantasy action horror thriller film directed by Peter Hyams and starring Arnold Schwarzenegger; its soundtrack featured the Guns N' Roses song "Oh My God".

## Key Facts

- End of Days is a 1999 American fantasy action horror thriller film. [src:End-of-Days-film]
- End of Days was directed by [[people/Peter-Hyams]]. [src:End-of-Days-film]
- The film stars [[people/Arnold-Schwarzenegger]], [[people/Gabriel-Byrne]], [[people/Robin-Tunney]], [[people/Kevin-Pollak]], [[people/Rod-Steiger]], [[people/CCH-Pounder]], and [[people/Udo-Kier]]. [src:End-of-Days-film]
- The film follows former [[places/New-York-City]] Police Department detective Jericho Cane, played by [[people/Arnold-Schwarzenegger]]. [src:End-of-Days-film]
- The soundtrack to End of Days included the [[organizations/Guns-N-Roses]] song [[works/Oh-My-God]], released in 1999. [src:Oh-My-God-Guns-N-Roses-song]
- "Oh My God" was sent to radio stations in November 1999 as a promo for the End of Days soundtrack and the band. [src:Oh-My-God-Guns-N-Roses-song]
