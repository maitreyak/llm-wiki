# The Union Connecting

> The first model railroad built by the New York Society of Model Engineers, begun in 1926.

## Key Facts

- "The Union Connecting" was the first model railroad of the [[organizations/New-York-Society-of-Model-Engineers]]. [src:New-York-Society-of-Model-Engineers]
- Construction of The Union Connecting began in 1926, when the Society was incorporated. [src:New-York-Society-of-Model-Engineers]
