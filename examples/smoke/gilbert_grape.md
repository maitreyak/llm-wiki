What's Eating Gilbert Grape is a 1993 American coming-of-age drama film directed by Lasse Hallström. The film stars Johnny Depp as Gilbert Grape and features Leonardo DiCaprio, whose performance earned him an Academy Award nomination for Best Supporting Actor. It was adapted by Peter Hedges from his 1991 novel of the same name.

The film is set in the fictional town of Endora, Iowa, and follows Gilbert as he cares for his younger brother Arnie and his obese mother while working at a small grocery store.
