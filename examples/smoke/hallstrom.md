Lasse Hallström is a Swedish film director. He was born on 2 June 1946 in Stockholm, Sweden. He first became known for directing almost all of the music videos of the pop group ABBA. His film My Life as a Dog (1985) earned him nominations for the Academy Award for Best Director.

Hallström later directed What's Eating Gilbert Grape (1993), The Cider House Rules (1999), and Chocolat (2000), the latter two of which were nominated for the Academy Award for Best Picture.
