Titanic is a 1997 American epic romance and disaster film directed, written, and co-produced by James Cameron. Incorporating both historical and fictionalized aspects, it is based on accounts of the sinking of RMS Titanic in 1912. Leonardo DiCaprio and Kate Winslet star as members of different social classes who fall in love aboard the ship.

James Cameron is a Canadian filmmaker. He was born on 16 August 1954 in Kapuskasing, Ontario, Canada. Titanic won 11 Academy Awards including Best Picture and Best Director, and became the highest-grossing film of all time at its release.
